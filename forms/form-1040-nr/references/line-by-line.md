# Form 1040-NR Line-by-Line Reference (2025 form, filed in 2026)

Every line of Form 1040-NR (2025, created 9/8/25) and Schedules NEC, OI, A (Form 1040-NR), and P, built from the PDF text of the forms and the Instructions for Form 1040-NR (2025, dated Jan 29, 2026). Where the 1040-NR instructions say "see the Instructions for Form 1040", the Form 1040 rule applies with the exception listed here. Re-verify against the next revision before using this for any other year: https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1040-nr.

Throughout: "ECI" means income effectively connected with a U.S. trade or business. Page 1 of Form 1040-NR carries only ECI (plus the treaty-exempt total on line 1k). U.S.-source income that is not ECI goes on Schedule NEC.

**Filing status rules (Instructions, Filing Status):**

- Married on December 31 → MFS, even if not separated.
- Exception to check Single: a married resident of Canada, Mexico, or South Korea, a married U.S. national, or a married India Art. 21(2) student or business apprentice who meets all five tests: lived apart from the spouse the last 6 months of 2025; files separately; paid over half the cost of the home; the home was the main home of their child, stepchild, or foster child for more than half of 2025; and could claim the child as a dependent (or could except for the Form 8332 release).
- QSS only if (1) for 2025 a resident of Canada, Mexico, or South Korea, a U.S. national, or an India Art. 21(2) student or apprentice, and (2) a resident alien or U.S. citizen in the year the spouse died.
- Estate / Trust: personal representative or foreign trust filings (out of scope for this skill).

Include only deductions allocable to ECI, except charitable gifts and casualty losses, which are allowed regardless. Never use Schedule A (Form 1040) with Form 1040-NR.
