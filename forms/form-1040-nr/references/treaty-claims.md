# Treaty Claims on Form 1040-NR

How a nonresident claims an income tax treaty benefit on the 2025 return, when Form 8833 is required, and the one treaty benefit that changes line 12. Sources: Instructions for Form 1040-NR (2025), line 1a, line 1k, Schedule OI item L, "Treaty-based return position disclosure"; Form 8833 (Rev. December 2022) and its instructions; Treas. Reg. §301.6114-1; IRC §§6114, 6712; Pub. 519 (2025) chapters 5 and 9; Pub. 901; IRS Tax Treaty Table 1 (Rev. May 2023).

General condition (Instructions, Simplified Procedure note): to claim a reduced rate or exemption the user must generally be a resident of the treaty country within the meaning of the treaty and must not have a U.S. permanent establishment or fixed base the income is attributable to. Treaties and technical explanations: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z.

Never pick a treaty article for the user from memory. Ask for the country, open the treaty text, quote the article, and show the user the condition it imposes (for example, a dollar cap, a time limit, or "immediately before visiting" residence).

## Three ways a treaty shows up on the return

| Situation | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Income fully **exempt** by treaty (ECI or not) | Schedule OI item L(1): (a) country, (b) article, (c) months claimed in prior years, (d) exempt amount; total (e) → Form 1040-NR line 1k only. Attach the Form 1042-S for it |
| Non-ECI income taxed at a **reduced** treaty rate | Schedule NEC, the column for that rate ((a) 10%, (b) 15%, or (d) with the rate). Not item L (the income is taxed, just at a lower rate) |
| Treaty-exempt income where the payer **withheld anyway** | Interest: Schedule NEC line 2, column (d) at 0%, withholding on line 25g (line 2b exception 2). Wages: reduce line 1a by the exempt amount, put it on 1k, complete item L, attach a statement with all the information Form 8233 would have required |

Instructions example (wages): a French F-1 student earned $8,000 from an internship; Article 21 of the U.S.–France treaty exempts up to $5,000; the W-2 shows $3,000 in box 1 and the Form 1042-S shows $5,000 exempt. Report $3,000 on line 1a, $5,000 on line 1k, and complete item L; attach both forms. If the employer withheld on everything because no Form 8233 was given, still report $3,000 on 1a and $5,000 on 1k, and attach the Form 8233 statement.

Instructions example (scholarship): a treaty-exempt scholarship of $9,000 in Form 1042-S box 2 with $1,260 withheld (box 10): enter $9,000 in item L column (d), $9,000 on line 1k, $1,260 on line 25g, nothing on Schedule 1 line 8r.

Item L(2): answer whether the item L income was taxed by a foreign country. Item L(3): Competent Authority determination letter → attach.

If the user failed to give the payer adequate documentation, attach to the return all information the withholding certificate would have required (Form W-8BEN, W-8BEN-E, or 8233) (Schedule OI item L caution).

## Form 8833: required or waived?

A treaty-based return position is taken when the user maintains that a treaty overrides or modifies a Code provision and so reduces (or potentially reduces) tax. Report it on Form 8833 unless reporting is waived (Treas. Reg. §301.6114-1(c)). A separate Form 8833 is filed for each position. Penalty for a required but missing disclosure: **$1,000** per failure for individuals ($10,000 for C corporations), IRC §6712, waivable for reasonable cause.

**Waived for individuals** (Instructions for Form 1040-NR, item L; Form 8833 instructions), including:

1. A treaty reduces the withholding tax on interest, dividends, rents, royalties, or other FDAP income ordinarily subject to 30%.
2. A treaty reduces or modifies the taxation of income from dependent personal services, pensions, annuities, social security and other public pensions, or income of artists, athletes, students, trainees, or teachers (this includes taxable scholarships and fellowships).
3. A totalization agreement, or a diplomatic or consular agreement, reduces or modifies taxation.
4. The partnership, estate, or trust already disclosed the position.
5. The payments or income items otherwise required to be disclosed total no more than $10,000.

**Specifically required** (Form 8833 instructions list, not exhaustive), including:

- A treaty reduces or modifies the taxation of gain or loss from disposing of a U.S. real property interest.
- ECI is claimed not to be attributable to a U.S. permanent establishment or fixed base, or a treaty modifies the business profits attributable to one.
- A treaty grants a credit for a foreign tax not allowed by the Code.
- Residency is determined under a treaty and apart from the Code (dual-resident tie-breaker, Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-7).
- The 4% transportation tax exemption by treaty (1040-NR line 23c instructions).

Decision procedure for the agent:

1. List every treaty position on the draft.
2. For each, match against the waiver list. Cite the waiver number in the draft ("Form 8833 waived: Treas. Reg. §301.6114-1(c), student income").
3. If no waiver matches, mark Form 8833 required and ask the user to confirm the facts for Part II and Part III of the form.
4. If unsure, mark "Form 8833: review with CPA" rather than guessing; the downside of a missing required form is the §6712 penalty.

## India Article 21(2): the standard deduction exception

Students and business apprentices eligible for Article 21(2) of the U.S.–India treaty may take the standard deduction on line 12 instead of itemizing (Pub. 519 ch. 5; Instructions, line 12). 2025 amounts from Pub. 519 Worksheet 5-1:

| Worksheet line | 2025 rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | $15,750 single or MFS; $31,500 qualifying surviving spouse |
| 2–3 | If someone can claim the user as a dependent: earned income + $450, minimum $1,350 |
| 4 | Smaller of line 1 or line 3 |
| 5 | Born before January 2, 1961, or blind: $1,600 ($2,000 single); both: $3,200 ($4,000 single) |
| 6 | Net disaster loss from Form 4684 line 15 |
| 7 | Lines 4 + 5 + 6 → Form 1040-NR line 12, with "Standard Deduction Allowed Under U.S.-India Income Tax Treaty" written to the left |

Not available if MFS and the spouse files and itemizes. The same group can also use Single status under the five "lived apart" tests, may claim dependents on limited terms, and is exempt from filing if single with gross income of $15,750 or less ($31,500 QSS) for 2025.

Ask: "Were you a resident of India immediately before coming to the U.S., and are you here as a student or business apprentice?" Do not apply the exception on nationality alone.

Note on sources: Pub. 519 (2025) chapter 7 still prints the filing exception as $15,000 / $30,000 while Worksheet 5-1 and the Instructions for Form 1040-NR (2025) use $15,750 / $31,500. Use the instructions' figures and tell the user about the inconsistency if they cite Pub. 519.
