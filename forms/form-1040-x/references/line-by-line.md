# Form 1040-X Line-by-Line Reference

Every field of Form 1040-X (Rev. December 2025), built from the text of the official PDF (https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1040x.pdf, "Created 2/19/26") and the Instructions for Form 1040-X (Rev. December 2025, dated Feb 27, 2026, https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i1040x.pdf). Form 1040-X is a continuous-use form: confirm at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1040x that no later revision exists before relying on these line numbers.

The amounts that go on each line come from the corrected return for the **year being amended**, figured with that year's forms and instructions.

## Columns A, B, C (lines 1–15)

| Column | Rule |
|---|---|
| A. Original amount reported or as previously adjusted | The amount on the original return, **unless** the return was previously amended, changed in an audit, or changed by an IRS notice. Then column A is the adjusted amount. Instructions example: IRS added $10,000 of unreported income making taxable income $100,000 → column A taxable income is $100,000 |
| B. Net change | The increase or decrease for each changed line; explain each in Part II. Show decreases in parentheses |
| C. Correct amount | Column A plus the increase, or minus the decrease. For an unchanged line, copy column A |

Negative numbers in any column go in parentheses. Instructions example for line 1: AGI $21,000 originally; a $500 W-2 arrives later → A 21,000, B 500, C 21,500 (and any added withholding goes on line 12, column B).

Lines 16–23 take a single amount each (no columns).

|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | Federal income tax withheld and excess social security and tier 1 RRTA tax withheld | Withholding from the return; change only with new or corrected W-2/1099-R | Attach new/corrected W-2s and 1099-Rs showing withholding to the **front** of Form 1040-X |

## Refund or Amount You Owe

| Line | Label | What goes here | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | Overpayment, if any, as shown on original return or as previously adjusted by the IRS | Original overpayment, plus any additional overpayment from an IRS change | Do not include interest received. Include it even if it was applied to estimated tax |
| 19 | Subtract line 18 from line 17 | Computed | If negative, treat as positive and add to line 11 to get line 20 |
| 20 | Amount you owe | Line 11 column C − line 19 if line 11 is larger | Pay electronically if possible (Direct Pay, EFTPS, card, digital wallet, or direct debit with an e-filed 1040-X) or by check with Form 1040-V. Interest and penalties are not entered; the IRS figures them |
| 21 | If line 11, column C, is less than line 19, enter the difference | Overpayment on this amendment | |
| 22 | Amount of line 21 you want refunded to you | Refund | Sent separately from any original refund; the IRS adds interest. Paper 1040-X refund = paper check |
| 23 | Amount of line 21 you want applied to your (enter year) estimated tax | Credit forward | No interest paid; the election can't be changed later. An election on the original to apply an overpayment forward also can't be reversed |

## Part II — Explanation of Changes

Mandatory on every Form 1040-X. Examples in the instructions: received another W-2 after filing; forgot the child tax credit or credit for other dependents; changed filing status from QSS to HOH; carrying a credit to an earlier year; claiming a disaster-relief benefit from recent legislation; e-filing Form 8379 attached to a Form 1040-X without amending. Attach a statement if more space is needed.

## Part III — Direct Deposit (e-filed Form 1040-X only)

Not printed on the paper PDF. For an e-filed Form 1040-X for tax year 2021 or later, lines 31–33 carry routing number, account type, and account number for the line 22 refund; to split the refund, check the box on Form 1040/1040-SR/1040-NR line 35a and attach Form 8888, and do not complete lines 35b–35d of that return. The account must be in the taxpayer's name. If the deposit fails, a paper check goes to the most recent address.

## Signature block

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Your signature, date, occupation | Required; the 1040-X is not a valid return unless signed. Paper: handwritten signature only (no digital or typed signatures). E-file: Self-Select PIN (software) or Practitioner PIN (preparer) |
| Spouse's signature, date, occupation | Required on an amended joint return |
| Identity Protection PIN (you / spouse) | All six digits of the IP PIN issued for the current year if one was issued; do not use a prior year's IP PIN. Missing it on an e-filed return makes the signature invalid and the return rejects |
| Phone, email | Contact details |
| Paid preparer block | Name, signature, date, PTIN, self-employed box, firm name, address, phone, EIN. An unpaid helper does not sign |
| Representative signing | Attach Form 2848 specifically authorizing the representative to sign |

E-file signature data (Self-Select PIN): PIN of any five digits except five zeros, last name, date of birth, IP PIN if applicable, and prior-year AGI from the originally filed prior-year return (not from an amended return or an IRS math-error correction); "$0" if no prior-year return was filed or it was only recently processed; prior-year PIN can substitute for AGI.

## Penalties referenced in the instructions

| Penalty | Amount | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Late payment of tax due on Form 1040-X | 1/2 of 1% of the unpaid amount per month or part of a month, up to 25% | Applies if not paid within 21 calendar days of notice and demand (10 business days if the tax is $100,000 or more); reasonable cause exception |
| Erroneous claim for refund or credit | 20% of the disallowed amount | Unless reasonable cause; not figured on amounts already subject to accuracy-related or fraud penalties |
| Frivolous return | $5,000 | In addition to other penalties |
