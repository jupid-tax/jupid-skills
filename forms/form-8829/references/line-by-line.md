# Form 8829 Line-by-Line Reference

Every line of Form 8829, Expenses for Business Use of Your Home, built from the text of the **2025 Form 8829** (form footer "Created 10/2/25") and the **2025 Instructions for Form 8829** (dated Mar 4, 2026). This is the revision filed in 2026 for tax year 2025. Before using this map for a later year, download the new revision from https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-8829 and compare every line number and caption.

Form header facts (2025 revision):
- "File only with Schedule C (Form 1040). Use a separate Form 8829 for each home you used for business during the year."
- OMB No. 1545-0074. Attachment Sequence No. 176.
- Header fields: Name(s) of proprietor(s); Your social security number.

Rounding convention used by this skill: percentages to two decimals (e.g., 14.74%), line 6 to four decimals (e.g., 0.3210), dollar amounts to whole dollars. The instructions do not prescribe rounding; state the convention in the draft and apply it consistently.

| Line | Caption (from the form) | What to enter | Notes |

**Special computation for certain daycare facilities** (instructions, Part I): when the daycare area includes rooms used exclusively for business and rooms used only partly for business, do not use Part I. (1) Exclusive area ÷ total area. (2) Run Part I using only the partly-used area on line 1 (with lines 4–6). (3) Add the two percentages, enter the sum on line 7, attach a statement.

## Part II — Figure Your Allowable Deduction (lines 8–36)

### Line 8 — gross income limit base

| Line | Caption | What to enter |
|------|---------|---------------|
| 8 | Enter the amount from Schedule C, line 29, plus any gain derived from the business use of your home, minus any loss from the trade or business not derived from the business use of your home | All income from this home's business use: Schedule C line 29 + gain from business use of the home on Form 8949/Schedule D or Form 4797 − losses on Form 8949/Schedule D or Form 4797 allocable to the business but not to the use of the home |

If some gross income comes from a business location other than the home, first determine the part of gross income (Schedule C line 7 plus gains from Form 8949, Schedule D, Form 4797) from the business use of the home, considering time spent at each location and other facts; subtract Schedule C line 28 expenses and the non-home losses; enter the result. If the filer files more than one Form 8829, include only income earned and deductions attributable to that income during the period the filer owned this home.

### Columns (a) and (b) for lines 9–22

- **Column (a) Direct expenses**: benefit only the business part of the home (painting or repairs to the business rooms). Enter 100%.
- **Column (b) Indirect expenses**: keep up and run the entire home. Enter 100% of the expense; the form applies line 7 on lines 13 and 24.
- **Exception**: if an indirect expense's business percentage differs from line 7, enter only the business part in column (a) and leave column (b) blank for that line (instructions example: $800 electric bill, $300 reasonably for lighting, 10% business → $30 in column (a) of line 21, nothing in column (b)).
- Only expenses allowable because the home is used for business go on Form 8829. Salaries, supplies, advertising and similar business costs stay on their Schedule C lines.
- Part-year business use: include only expenses paid or incurred for the portion of the year the home was used for business.
- Daycare not used exclusively: multiply direct expenses for the daycare area by line 6 (time percentage) before entering them in column (a) (Pub. 587, Daycare Facility, Example 1).

## Part IV — Carryover of Unallowed Expenses to 2026 (lines 43–44)

| Line | Caption | Computation |
|------|---------|-------------|
| 43 | Operating expenses. Subtract line 27 from line 26. If less than zero, enter -0- | Goes to 2026 Form 8829 line 25 |
| 44 | Excess casualty losses and depreciation. Subtract line 33 from line 32. If less than zero, enter -0- | Goes to 2026 Form 8829 line 31 |

The carryover is subject to the next year's limit whether or not the filer lives in the same home (instructions, Part IV). In a year the simplified method is used, the carryover cannot be deducted; it waits for the next actual-expense year (Pub. 587, No deduction of carryover of actual expenses).

## Who does not use Form 8829 (instructions, "Who cannot use Form 8829")

- Partners claiming actual home expenses, and Schedule F filers: use the Worksheet To Figure the Deduction for Business Use of Your Home in Pub. 587.
- Employees: "You can't deduct these expenses on your personal return."
- All home expenses properly allocable to inventory costs: figure them in Schedule C, Part III.
- The filer elected the simplified method for this home for 2025. Only one home per year can use the simplified method.
