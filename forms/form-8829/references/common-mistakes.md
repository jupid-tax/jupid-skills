# Form 8829 Common Mistakes

Each entry: the mistake, how to detect it in the user's data, and the fix with its source. Sources are the 2025 Form 8829, the 2025 Instructions for Form 8829 (i8829), and Pub. 587 (2025).

## 1. Claiming a room that is not used exclusively

**Detect**: the user mentions a guest bed, kids' homework, a family computer, a TV, exercise equipment, or "I mostly work there."
**Fix**: no deduction for that area unless it is inventory/sample storage or a licensed daycare (Pub. 587, Exclusive Use; Exceptions to Exclusive Use). Ask whether a defined sub-area is business-only and measure only that.

## 2. Employees claiming the deduction

**Detect**: W-2 income, "my employer lets me work from home," no Schedule C.
**Fix**: stop. i8829: employees "can't deduct these expenses on your personal return." IRC §67(h), as amended by P.L. 119-21 §70110, disallows miscellaneous itemized deductions for tax years beginning after 2017 with no end date.

## 3. Using the purchase price on line 37 without comparing FMV

**Detect**: line 37 equals the purchase price, and the home's value dropped before business use began, or there were pre-business improvements not added.
**Fix**: line 37 is the smaller of adjusted basis (cost plus improvements before business use, less casualty losses and prior depreciation) or FMV on the first-business-use date (i8829, Lines 37 Through 40).

## 4. Depreciating the land

**Detect**: line 38 is zero or blank for an owned home.
**Fix**: enter the land's basis (or FMV if lower) on line 38. Land is not depreciable (Pub. 587, Depreciating Your Home). Ask the user for the source of the land figure.

## 5. Wrong line 41 percentage

**Detect**: 2.564% used for a home first used for business in 2025, or a month percentage used for a home first used before 2025.
**Fix**: 2025 first use → the month percentage (January 2.461% … December 0.107%); first use after May 12, 1993 and before 2025 → 2.564% (i8829, Line 41).

## 6. Putting first-year home depreciation on Schedule C line 13

**Detect**: Form 4562 total includes the home office and flows to Schedule C line 13.
**Fix**: the home's depreciation reaches Schedule C only through Form 8829 line 36 → Schedule C line 30. Report it on Form 4562 line 19j, but "Do not include the amount from Form 8829, line 42, on Schedule C, line 13" (i8829, Line 42).

## 7. Mortgage interest and taxes on the wrong lines

**Detect**: a standard-deduction filer with entries on lines 10 or 11; an itemizer with all interest on line 16.
**Fix**: standard deduction → lines 16(b) and 17(b). Itemizer → line 10(b) for deductible interest, line 16(b) only for interest disallowed by the Schedule A limits; line 11(b) when total SALT ≤ $10,000 ($5,000 MFS), else the Line 11 Worksheet with the result in column (a) (i8829, Lines 9, 10, and 11; Line 16; Line 17).

## 8. Double-counting the business share on Schedule A

**Detect**: Schedule A shows the full mortgage interest or real estate taxes while Form 8829 also claims a share.
**Fix**: Schedule A gets only the personal part; real estate taxes on Schedule A line 5b exclude the business portion, including Line 11 Worksheet line 6 (i8829, Mortgage interest reported on Schedule A; Real estate taxes reported on Schedule A).

## 9. Putting direct expenses in column (b)

**Detect**: office-only painting or repair entered in column (b), so it gets multiplied by line 7 again.
**Fix**: column (a) at 100% for expenses that benefit only the business part. Daycare areas not used exclusively: multiply the direct expense by line 6 first (Pub. 587, Daycare Facility, Example 1).

## 10. Non-home business expenses on Form 8829

**Detect**: supplies, advertising, software, a second phone line, or business insurance entered on line 22.
**Fix**: those stay on their Schedule C lines; Form 8829 takes only expenses allowable because the home is used for business (i8829, Columns (a) and (b)). Second business line and business long-distance → Schedule C line 25 (Pub. 587, Telephone).

## 11. Repairs vs improvements

**Detect**: a new roof, replacement HVAC, rewiring, or remodel entered on line 20.
**Fix**: improvements are depreciated, not expensed. After business use began → business percentage of cost, 39-year table, statement attached to line 42 (Pub. 587, Permanent improvements; Depreciating permanent improvements). Before business use → added to basis on line 37.

## 12. Letting Tier 2 or Tier 3 expenses create a Schedule C loss

**Detect**: line 27 > line 15, or line 33 > line 28, or Schedule C line 31 negative because of operating expenses or depreciation.
**Fix**: line 27 = smaller of line 15 or line 26; line 33 = smaller of line 28 or line 32; the excess goes to lines 43 and 44 (Form 8829 text).

## 13. Dropping the carryover

**Detect**: prior year Form 8829 showed amounts on lines 43 or 44; this year lines 25 and 31 are zero. Or the user used the simplified method last year and "lost" the carryover.
**Fix**: enter the prior amounts on lines 25 and 31; after a simplified year, use lines 6a and 6b of that year's Simplified Method Worksheet (i8829, Lines 25 and 31; Schedule C instructions, Simplified Method Worksheet line 6).

## 14. Wrong line 8 when the business has other locations or home-use gains

**Detect**: the user also works from a rented office, studio, or client sites with a fixed location; or sold business property connected to the home.
**Fix**: allocate gross income to the home first (time spent, other facts), then subtract Schedule C line 28 expenses and non-home losses; add home-use gains from Form 8949/Schedule D/Form 4797 (i8829, Line 8).

## 15. Full-year expenses for part-year use

**Detect**: business began, ended, or moved during the year, but twelve months of rent, utilities or interest are entered.
**Fix**: only expenses for the period of business use (Pub. 587, Part-year use; i8829, Columns (a) and (b)). For daycare that started or stopped, line 5 = 24 × days available, not 8,760.

## 16. Mixing methods for the same home in the same year

**Detect**: Schedule C line 30 shows square footage (simplified) and a Form 8829 is attached for the same home.
**Fix**: one method per home per year; the simplified election is made on a timely filed original return and is irrevocable for that year. A second home in the same year must use actual expenses (Pub. 587, Electing the Simplified Method; i8829, Who cannot use Form 8829).

## 17. Not telling the user about the sale consequence

**Detect**: an owner claims depreciation (line 42 > 0) and the draft says nothing about the eventual sale.
**Fix**: one sentence in the draft: depreciation allowed or allowable after May 6, 1997 cannot be excluded under §121 when the home is sold and is taxed as unrecaptured section 1250 gain (Pub. 587, Depreciation; Pub. 523). Skipping depreciation does not avoid this because basis is reduced by allowable depreciation.
