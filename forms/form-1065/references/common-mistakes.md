# Common Form 1065 Mistakes

Errors that recur on small-partnership returns, each with the rule that fixes it. Citations are to the Instructions for Form 1065 (2025) unless noted. Check the draft against every entry before declaring it ready.

---

## 1. Paying a partner on line 9 or through payroll

**Mistake:** A working member's monthly pay is booked as salary and lands on line 9, or the partnership issues the member a Form W-2.

**Rule:** Line 9 is "Salaries and wages (other than to partners)". A payment to a partner for services fixed without regard to income is a guaranteed payment on line 10 and Schedule K, line 4a; a draw of profits is a distribution on Schedule K, line 19 (p. 21; Form 1065, page 1).

**Fix:** Move the amounts to line 10 (fixed payments) or line 19 (draws). Ask the user which payments were fixed and which depended on profit. Tell the user that payroll filings already made for a partner need a CPA's review.

---

## 2. Deducting separately stated items on page 1

**Mistake:** The section 179 deduction is folded into line 16a, charitable contributions or investment interest into line 21, or partner retirement contributions into line 18.

**Rule:** Section 179 goes to Schedule K, line 12 (p. 23); contributions to lines 13a-13b; investment interest to line 13c; partner retirement contributions to box 13, code R (p. 23). Page 1 carries only items every partner treats the same way.

**Fix:** Remove them from page 1 and report them on Schedule K and each K-1. Line 23 rises; Analysis of Net Income line 1 does not change.

---

## 3. Portfolio or rental income on page 1

**Mistake:** Bank interest, dividends, or capital gains go on line 7; rent from a building goes on line 1a.

**Rule:** Report only trade or business income on lines 1a-8. Portfolio income goes to Schedule K, lines 5-9; rental real estate to Form 8825 and line 2; other rentals to line 3 (p. 19). Interest charged on customer receivables is business income (line 7); interest on a bank deposit is portfolio income (line 5) (p. 20).

**Fix:** Reclassify. Remember that line 5 interest still counts toward Schedule B, question 4 total receipts.

---

## 4. Answering Schedule B, question 4 "Yes" when it is "No"

**Mistake:** The partnership is small, so the preparer answers "Yes" even though the return is late or one K-1 went out after the due date; or total receipts are taken from line 1c only.

**Rule:** All four conditions must hold, including "Schedules K-1 are filed with the return and furnished to the partners on or before the due date (including extensions)". Total receipts = line 1a + lines 4-7 + Schedule K lines 3a, 5, 6a, 7 + income or net gain on lines 8, 9a, 10, 11 + Form 8825 lines 2, 21, 22a (p. 26).

**Fix:** Recompute; if any condition fails, complete Schedules L, M-1, M-2, item F, and every K-1's item L. See [`schedule-b-and-small-partnership.md`](./schedule-b-and-small-partnership.md).

---

## 5. Defaulting LLC members' self-employment treatment

**Mistake:** Box 14 is left blank for a member who runs the business, or every LLC member gets a full share of line 1 in box 14 without asking.

**Rule:** General partners include their share of ordinary income; limited partners include only guaranteed payments for services; whether a member is a limited partner depends on section 1402(a)(13) (pp. 3, 44). The item G checkbox does not decide it. Corporations, estates, trusts, exempt organizations, and IRAs get no box 14 entry.

**Fix:** Ask the user (and their CPA) for each member's treatment, record the answer, and run the worksheet in [`k1-allocation-and-capital.md`](./k1-allocation-and-capital.md).

---

## 6. Capital accounts that do not tie

**Mistake:** Item L ending balances do not add to Schedule M-2, line 9; guaranteed payments are included in item L; book (GAAP) capital is reported instead of tax-basis capital; section 743(b) adjustments are included.

**Rule:** Item L uses the tax-basis transactional approach; current-year income includes tax-exempt income and subtracts nondeductible expenses; guaranteed payment income contained in M-2 line 3 is removed on line 7; section 743(b) adjustments are excluded; M-2 line 9 equals the sum of ending item L balances (pp. 34-35, 63).

**Fix:** Rebuild item L partner by partner, then M-2, and reconcile before producing the draft.

---

## 7. Treating item L as the partner's basis

**Mistake:** The draft tells a partner their loss is deductible up to their item L balance, or that a distribution is tax-free because it is below item L.

**Rule:** Item L excludes the partner's share of liabilities (item K1) and partner-specific adjustments; each partner tracks the adjusted basis of their interest (p. 34).

**Fix:** State in the handoff that outside basis is the partner's computation, not a Form 1065 figure.

---

## 8. Distribution codes from before 2025

**Mistake:** Every cash distribution is coded A in box 19.

**Rule:** For 2025, code A is cash and marketable securities to partners **not** providing services; code F is cash to a partner who performed services, received an allocation of income, and was treated as receiving a distribution; codes G and C split property the same way; code D is a deemed distribution from a decrease in the partner's share of liabilities. Schedule K, line 19a = codes A + D + F; line 19b = codes B + C + G (p. 50).

**Fix:** Apply the three-part test to each partner. Guaranteed payments never go in box 19.

---

## 9. Partner medical insurance on line 19

**Mistake:** Health insurance paid for a partner is deducted as an employee benefit.

**Rule:** Insurance that constitutes medical care for a partner, spouse, dependents, or children under 27 is a guaranteed payment on line 10 and Schedule K, line 4 (box 4a), and is also reported on Schedule K, line 13e and box 13, code M; it is excluded from Schedule M-1, line 3 (pp. 21, 24, 43, 63).

**Fix:** Move it; show it in both places on the affected partner's K-1.

---

## 10. Invalid election out of the audit regime

**Mistake:** Question 33 is "Yes" although a member is a single-member LLC, a trust, or another partnership; or the election is made on a late return; or S corporation shareholders are not counted.

**Rule:** Eligible partners are individuals, C corporations, S corporations, eligible foreign entities, and estates of deceased partners, for the entire year; the count adds S corporation partners' shareholder K-1s; the election requires a timely filed return including extensions (pp. 30-31; Regulations section 301.6221(b)-1).

**Fix:** If ineligible or late, answer "No" and complete the PR designation. See [`bba-audit-and-penalties.md`](./bba-audit-and-penalties.md).

---

## 11. Incomplete PR designation

**Mistake:** The PR is named with a P.O. box, a foreign phone number, or no designated individual for an entity PR.

**Rule:** The PR and any DI need a U.S. street address, a U.S. TIN, a U.S.-area-code phone number, and availability to meet the IRS in the United States (p. 31).

**Fix:** Ask for the missing facts; do not file with placeholders.

---

## 12. Amending a BBA year with an amended return

**Mistake:** A partnership that did not elect out files an amended Form 1065 with amended K-1s to fix an error.

**Rule:** A BBA partnership files an administrative adjustment request (Form 1065 with box G(5) and Form 8082 if e-filed, or Form 1065-X on paper) and does not file amended K-1s (p. 9).

**Fix:** Route corrections through the AAR rules in [`bba-audit-and-penalties.md`](./bba-audit-and-penalties.md).

---

## 13. Paper filing when e-filing is mandatory

**Mistake:** A two-member partnership with employees mails a paper return because "we only have two partners".

**Rule:** E-filing is required if the partnership is required to file 10 or more returns of any type during the calendar year ending with or within its tax year (income tax, employment tax, excise tax, and information returns such as Forms W-2 and 1099; Schedules K-1 do not count), or had more than 100 partners during the year (p. 5; Regulations section 301.6011-3(a), (d)(5)). Paper filing when e-filing is required is treated as a failure to file in the prescribed manner (section 301.6011-3(c)).

**Fix:** Count the returns before choosing the channel. See [`../filing.md`](../filing.md).

---

## 14. Undercounting partners in the late-filing penalty

**Mistake:** The penalty estimate uses year-end partners only, or forgets that a partial month counts as a month.

**Rule:** $255 (returns due in 2026) for each month or part of a month, up to 12 months, multiplied by the number of persons who were partners at any time during the year (p. 7).

**Fix:** Use the partner count from item I (one K-1 per person who was a partner at any time).

---

## 15. Analysis of Net Income errors

**Mistake:** Page 6, line 1 is skipped because question 4 is "Yes", or LLC members are put on the general partners row.

**Rule:** Question 4 does not excuse the Analysis of Net Income. Report all amounts for LLC members on the limited partners row; line 2 must add to line 1, and line 1 must equal Schedule M-1, line 9 when M-1 is completed (p. 61).

**Fix:** Always complete it. For the active/passive column of LLC members, ask the user how each member participates and record the answer.

---

## 16. Filing Form 1065 for an entity that should not file one

**Mistake:** A single-member LLC, or a spouse-owned business that qualified and elected qualified joint venture treatment, files Form 1065; or a multi-member LLC with an accepted S election files Form 1065.

**Rule:** A domestic LLC with at least two members that has not filed Form 8832 is a partnership; a married couple who are the only members of a jointly owned business not held in a state-law entity, who both materially participate and file a joint return, may elect QJV status and file two Schedules C instead (pp. 3-4).

**Fix:** Confirm classification first. Redirect to [`../../schedule-c/SKILL.md`](../../schedule-c/SKILL.md), [`../../form-1120-s/SKILL.md`](../../form-1120-s/SKILL.md), or [`../../form-8832/SKILL.md`](../../form-8832/SKILL.md).
