# Centralized Audit Regime (BBA), Partnership Representative, Election Out, and Penalties

Mechanics for Schedule B, question 33, the partnership representative (PR) designation, corrections after filing, and the late-filing and K-1 penalties. Sources: Instructions for Form 1065 (2025) pp. 3, 7, 9-11, 25, 30-31; Schedule B-2 (Form 1065) (Rev. December 2018); Treasury Regulations sections 301.6221(b)-1 and 301.6011-3; Rev. Proc. 2024-40 and Rev. Proc. 2025-32; IRM 20.1.2.4.3.1 (12-30-2025).

This file explains what each choice does on the form. Whether a partnership should elect out is a decision for the partners and their CPA; the agent presents the facts and records the decision.

---

## 1. The centralized partnership audit regime

The Bipartisan Budget Act of 2015 (BBA) created the centralized partnership audit regime for partnership tax years beginning after 2017, replacing TEFRA. It applies to **all** partnerships unless the partnership is an eligible partnership and elects out on Schedule B-2 (Instructions p. 3).

Terms used on the form and in notices (Instructions p. 3):

- **Reviewed year**: the partnership tax year to which an adjustment relates.
- **Adjustment year**: the year a court decision becomes final, an administrative adjustment request (AAR) is filed, or a notice of final partnership adjustment is mailed (or a waiver is executed).
- **Imputed underpayment (IU)**: the entity-level amount computed from adjustments (figured under the Instructions for Form 8082).

## 2. Partnership representative (PR) designation

Required on every Form 1065 that does not validly elect out (Schedule B, question 33 "No"). Fields at the bottom of page 4:

- PR first and last name (or entity name), U.S. street address, city, state, ZIP, U.S. phone number
- If the PR is an entity: the designated individual (DI) first and last name, U.S. street address, city, state, ZIP, U.S. phone number

Rules (Instructions pp. 30-31):

- The PR can be a partner or any other person with **substantial presence** in the United States. Substantial presence means the person makes themselves available to meet in person with the IRS in the United States at a reasonable time and place, and has a U.S. street address, a U.S. TIN, and a phone number with a U.S. area code. A DI must also have substantial presence.
- The designation is made separately for each tax year on that year's Form 1065.
- The PR has sole authority to act for the partnership; the partnership and all partners are bound by the PR's actions in dealings with the IRS under the regime.
- A designation stays in effect until the PR or DI resigns, the partnership revokes it and designates a successor, or the IRS determines it is not in effect. Revocations and resignations use Form 8979 (see the Instructions for Form 8979 for timing).

Ask: "Who should be the partnership representative for this year? I need their full name, a U.S. street address (not a P.O. box), and a U.S. phone number. If it is a company, I also need the individual who will act for it." Never fill the PR fields with a placeholder; an incomplete designation is an incomplete return.

## 3. Election out under section 6221(b) (Schedule B, question 33 "Yes")

### Eligibility (Instructions pp. 30-31; Regulations section 301.6221(b)-1(b))

- The partnership must have **100 or fewer eligible partners** for the year. Count = number of Schedules K-1 the partnership must issue for the year **plus** the number of Schedules K-1 each S corporation partner must issue to its shareholders for its tax year ending with or within the partnership's year.
- Eligible partners: individuals, C corporations, S corporations, foreign entities that would be C corporations if domestic, and estates of deceased partners. An S corporation is an eligible partner even if some of its shareholders would not be (Regulations section 301.6221(b)-1(b)(3)(i)); its shareholders are still counted toward the 100.
- Every K-1 must be furnished to a partner that was an eligible partner for the partnership's **entire** taxable year (Regulations section 301.6221(b)-1(b)(1)(ii)). A member that was a single-member LLC for part of the year breaks eligibility.
- The partnership is **not eligible** if it must issue a K-1 to any of: a partnership; a trust; a foreign entity that would not be a C corporation if domestic; a disregarded entity (for example, a single-member LLC owned by an individual); an estate of an individual other than a deceased partner; any person holding an interest on behalf of another person (nominee). The regulation also lists a qualified subchapter S subsidiary (Regulations section 301.6221(b)-1(b)(3)(ii)(G)).

### How to make it

- Answer question 33 "Yes", attach Schedule B-2, and enter the total from Schedule B-2, Part III, line 3.
- Schedule B-2: Part I lists each eligible partner (name, TIN, type code I, C, E, F, or S); Part II lists the shareholders of each S corporation partner (name, TIN, type code I, E, T, or O); Part III adds the K-1 counts. "If line 3 is more than 100, the partnership cannot make the election under section 6221(b)."
- Completing Part I is an affirmative statement that all partners are eligible partners and that all required information is provided (Schedule B-2, page 1).
- The election can be made **only on a timely filed return, including extensions** (Instructions p. 30). A late return cannot elect out; it must designate a PR.
- The election is made year by year.
- The partnership must notify each of its partners of the election within 30 days of making it, in the form and manner the partnership determines (Regulations section 301.6221(b)-1(c)(3)). Put a dated notice in the deliverable checklist.

### What changes on the return

- Question 33 "Yes": no PR designation for that year.
- Corrections for that year are made by amended return (see section 4), not by AAR.

Ask, in this order: (1) list every partner and its tax type, including whether any member is a single-member LLC, trust, partnership, or nominee; (2) for each S corporation partner, the number of shareholders and their names, TINs, and types; (3) "Do you want the partnership to elect out of the centralized audit regime for this year, or designate a partnership representative?" Do not answer the third question for the user.

## 4. Correcting a filed return

| Situation | How (Instructions pp. 9-10) |
|-----------|----------------------------|
| BBA partnership (did not elect out), e-filing the correction | File an **AAR**: complete Form 1065 with corrected amounts, check box G(5), and complete Form 8082. Do not file an amended return or amended K-1s/K-3s |
| BBA partnership, AAR with an imputed underpayment the partnership pays | Report the IU, estimated interest, and estimated penalties on page 1, line 26, with the schedule required by the Instructions for Form 8082. Check payments go to Internal Revenue Service, Ogden Service Center, Ogden, UT 84201-0011, payable to "United States Treasury", with the partnership name, Form 1065, TIN, tax year, and "BBA AAR Imputed Underpayment"; electronic payments choose the payment description "BBA AAR Imputed Underpayment". The partnership may instead elect to have partners take the adjustments into account (Form 8082 instructions) |
| Partnership that elected out (or other non-BBA year), e-filing | Complete Form 1065, check box G(5), attach a statement listing each amended line, the corrected amount, and the reason; file amended K-1s (and K-3s) and furnish copies, checking "Amended K-1" |
| Any correction filed on paper | Form 1065-X, Amended Return or Administrative Adjustment Request (AAR) |
| Partnership-partner filing a modification amended return during a BBA exam | Form 8982 rules; payment on page 1, line 26 |

A federal change can change the state return; tell the user to check with the state tax agency (Instructions p. 10).

## 5. Late filing penalty (IRC §6698)

Imposed when the partnership (a) fails to file by the due date **including extensions**, or (b) files a return that fails to show all the information required, unless due to reasonable cause (Instructions p. 7).

| Return | Amount per partner per month or part of a month | Source |
|--------|-----------------------------------------------|--------|
| Returns required to be filed in 2026 (2025 tax years) | $255 | Instructions for Form 1065 (2025), p. 7; Rev. Proc. 2024-40, section 2.56 |
| Returns required to be filed in 2027 (2026 tax years) | $260 | Rev. Proc. 2025-32, section 4.55 |

Formula:

```
penalty = amount_per_month × months_late (each month or part of a month, capped at 12)
          × number of persons who were partners at any time during the tax year
```

- Count every person who was a partner during any part of the year, including partners who joined or left.
- A month starts on the due date (including any valid extension). Example: due March 16, 2026, filed October 20, 2026 = 7 full months plus a part month = 8 months.
- Maximum months: 12.
- Re-check the per-month amount every year in the Rev. Proc. that adjusts IRC §6698(b)(1) for inflation (the annual inflation-adjustment Rev. Proc., section titled "Failure to File Partnership Return").

### Relief

- **Reasonable cause.** Do not attach an explanation when filing. If the partnership receives a penalty notice after filing, it may send an explanation and the IRS decides (Instructions p. 7).
- **Rev. Proc. 84-35 (small partnerships).** IRM 20.1.2.4.3.1 (12-30-2025) says reasonable cause is presumed if: the partnership has 10 or fewer partners (spouses filing jointly count as one); each partner is an individual (excluding nonresident aliens) or the estate of a deceased partner; each partner's items of income, deduction, and credit are allocated in the same proportion as all other items; and each partner reported their share on a timely filed income tax return. If any criterion is uncertain (for example, a partner is a single-member LLC, or the agreement has special allocations), say so and leave the relief request to the user's CPA.
- Penalty abatement requests are made after the notice, not in the return.

## 6. Schedule K-1 (and K-3) penalties (Instructions p. 7)

| Failure | Amount (returns due in 2026) |
|---------|------------------------------|
| Each K-1 (and K-3) not furnished when due, or missing or incorrect information | $340 per schedule |
| Calendar-year maximum, entities with gross receipts over $5,000,000 | $4,098,500 |
| Calendar-year maximum, entities with gross receipts of $5,000,000 or less | $1,366,000 |
| Intentional disregard | $680 per schedule or, if greater, 10% of the aggregate amount of items required to be reported; no maximum |

These are year-indexed amounts; re-check them in the next year's Form 1065 instructions.

## 7. Electronic filing failure

A partnership required to e-file (10 or more returns of any type required to be filed during the calendar year ending with or within its tax year, or more than 100 partners during the year) that files on paper "will be deemed to have failed to file the return in the manner prescribed for purposes of the information return penalty under section 6721" (Regulations section 301.6011-3(c)). Count returns under section 301.6011-3(d)(5): income tax, employment tax, excise tax, and information returns such as Forms W-2 and 1099, but not schedules attached to the partnership return (so Schedules K-1 do not count). Waiver and religious-exemption procedures are in [`../filing.md`](../filing.md).

## 8. Trust fund recovery penalty

Unrelated to Form 1065 itself, but partnerships with employees can face it: responsible persons who willfully fail to collect, account for, or pay withheld income and employment taxes (Forms 720, 941, 943, 944, 945) can be personally assessed the unpaid trust fund tax (Instructions p. 7). If the user mentions unpaid payroll taxes, flag it and refer to [`../../form-941/SKILL.md`](../../form-941/SKILL.md) and a CPA.
