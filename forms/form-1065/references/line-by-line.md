# Form 1065 Line-by-Line Reference

Every line of the 2025 Form 1065 (6 pages), Schedule K-1 (Form 1065), and the two short Schedule B attachments, built from the text of the official PDFs:

- Form 1065 (2025), `f1065.pdf`, footer "Created 11/25/25"
- Instructions for Form 1065 (2025), `i1065.pdf`, dated Jan 14, 2026 (page numbers below refer to this PDF)
- Schedule K-1 (Form 1065) 2025, `f1065sk1.pdf`
- Schedule B-1 (Form 1065) (Rev. August 2019), Schedule B-2 (Form 1065) (Rev. December 2018)

The 2025 form is used for calendar year 2025 and for fiscal years that begin in 2025 and end in 2026 (Instructions, Period Covered, p. 5). Re-check every line against the next revision at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1065 before using this map for a later year.

Rounding: either round every amount to whole dollars or use cents everywhere; when adding several amounts, add with cents and round only the total (Instructions, Rounding Off to Whole Dollars, p. 8).
