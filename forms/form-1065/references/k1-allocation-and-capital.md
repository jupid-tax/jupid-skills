# Schedules K and K-1: Allocation, Guaranteed Payments, Self-Employment, Distributions, Capital Accounts, K-2/K-3

How to turn the partnership totals on Schedule K into one Schedule K-1 per partner, and how to build the tax-basis capital accounts (K-1 item L and Schedule M-2). Sources: Instructions for Form 1065 (2025) pp. 31-63, Partner's Instructions for Schedule K-1 (Form 1065) (2025), and Partnership Instructions for Schedules K-2 and K-3 (Form 1065) (2025), unless another source is named.

---

## 1. Allocation rules

- Allocate every item according to the partnership agreement's general profit and loss ratio. Items the agreement allocates in a different ratio (special allocations) go on the affected partners' K-1 boxes, and the total goes on the Schedule K line, never on page 1, Form 1125-A, or Schedule D (Instructions p. 31).
- If the agreement is silent, or an allocation lacks substantial economic effect, the share follows the partner's interest in the partnership (Regulations section 1.704-1).
- Property contributed with a built-in gain or loss must be allocated so the contributing partner bears that built-in amount (section 704(c), Regulations section 1.704-3). Report the contribution in K-1 item M with a statement.
- **Varying interests.** When a partner joins, leaves, or changes percentage during the year, items are allocated only for the part of the year the person was a partner, using either a proration method or an interim closing of the books, under section 706(d) and Regulations section 1.706-4 (Instructions p. 31). The choice and the convention are the partnership's. **Ask** the user which method the partnership uses and the exact transfer date; do not pick one.
- **Sale of an interest.** When a partner sells or exchanges an interest and the partnership holds section 751 property (unrealized receivables, which under IRC §751(c) include potential depreciation recapture, or inventory items), the transferor must notify the partnership in writing within 30 days, the partnership files Form 8308, and the transferor's K-1 reports the section 751, collectibles, and unrecaptured section 1250 shares in box 20, codes AB, AC, and AD (Instructions pp. 12, 59). These computations need the assets' fair market values at the sale date; ask the user's CPA for them and do not estimate.
- Every Schedule K line must equal the sum of that box across all K-1s. When rounding to whole dollars, assign the rounding difference so the sums tie, and record which partner absorbed it.
- Married partners each get their own K-1 (Instructions p. 32).

Ask before allocating: "Does the operating or partnership agreement allocate all income, loss, and deductions by ownership percentage, or are any items allocated differently? Did anyone join, leave, or change percentage during the year?"

## 2. Guaranteed payments

Definition: payments to a partner for services or for the use of capital, determined without regard to partnership income (Instructions p. 21, line 10).

| Where | What |
|-------|------|
| Page 1, line 10 | Deductible guaranteed payments allocable to a trade or business activity, including premiums for medical insurance for a partner, the partner's spouse, dependents, and children under 27 who are not dependents |
| Schedule K, line 4a / K-1 box 4a | Guaranteed payments for services (including the partner medical insurance) |
| Schedule K, line 4b / K-1 box 4b | Guaranteed payments for use of capital |
| Schedule K, line 4c / K-1 box 4c | Total, including guaranteed payments that must be capitalized and so are not on line 10 |
| Schedule K, line 13e / K-1 box 13, code M | Medical insurance paid for the partner (information the partner needs for their own deduction) |
| K-1 box 13, code R | Payments for a partner to an IRA, qualified plan, SEP, or SIMPLE (never on page 1, line 18) |
| Schedule M-1, line 3 | Guaranteed payments other than health insurance |

Rules the agent enforces:

- Line 9 is "Salaries and wages (other than to partners)". Anything paid to a partner for work is a guaranteed payment (line 10) or a distribution (Schedule K, line 19), never line 9.
- A transfer of partnership property to satisfy a guaranteed payment is a sale or exchange, not a distribution (Rev. Rul. 2007-40, cited at Instructions p. 36).
- Guaranteed payments are not qualified business income to the partner who receives them (IRC §199A(c)(4)(B)). The partnership's QBI already reflects the line 10 deduction.
- Guaranteed payments for services are generally not passive income; guaranteed payments for capital are treated as interest for section 469 (Instructions p. 36).

Ask: "List every payment the business made to each partner during the year, with dates and amounts, and say for each whether it was a fixed payment for work, a fixed return on capital, health insurance, a retirement contribution, or a draw of profits."

## 3. Self-employment: Schedule K, line 14 and K-1 box 14

### The worksheet (Instructions p. 44, "Worksheet for Figuring Net Earnings (Loss) From Self-Employment")

| Line | Text (shortened) |
|------|-----------------|
| 1a | Ordinary business income (loss) (Schedule K, line 1) |
| 1b | Net income (loss) from certain rental real estate activities (dealer rentals, or rentals with services to occupants) |
| 1c | Other net rental income (loss) (Schedule K, line 3c) |
| 1d | Net loss from Form 4797, Part II, line 17, included on line 1a (enter as positive) |
| 1e | Combine lines 1a through 1d |
| 2 | Net gain from Form 4797, Part II, line 17, included on line 1a |
| 3a | Subtract line 2 from line 1e |
| 3b | Part of line 3a allocated to limited partners, estates, trusts, corporations, exempt organizations, and IRAs |
| 3c | Subtract line 3b from line 3a; each general partner's share goes in box 14, code A |
| 4a | Guaranteed payments to partners (Schedule K, line 4c) derived from a trade or business under section 1402(c) |
| 4b | Part of line 4a allocated to limited partners for other than services and to estates, trusts, corporations, exempt organizations, and IRAs |
| 4c | Subtract line 4b from line 4a; each general and limited partner's share goes in box 14, code A |
| 5 | Net earnings (loss) from self-employment. Combine lines 3c and 4c. Enter on Schedule K, line 14a |

Line 4a also picks up other ordinary business items reported on Schedules K and K-1 that count for section 1402, but not expense items limited at the partner level such as the section 179 deduction (Instructions p. 44).

### Who gets what in box 14, code A

- Individual **general partner**: share of line 3c plus share of line 4c.
- Individual **limited partner**: only guaranteed payments for services actually rendered (line 4c share). The distributive share is excluded under section 1402(a)(13).
- **Estates, trusts, corporations (including S corporations), exempt organizations, IRAs**: leave box 14 blank (Instructions p. 44).

### LLC members: ask, do not decide

The instructions say "whether a partner qualifies as a limited partner for purposes of self-employment tax depends on whether the partner is considered a limited partner under section 1402(a)(13)" (Instructions pp. 3, 44). An LLC member is not automatically a general partner or a limited partner, and checking "LLC member-manager" in item G does not settle the question. For each LLC member, ask the user (and their CPA if they have one):

> "For self-employment tax, should [member]'s share of ordinary income be treated like a general partner's (included in box 14) or like a limited partner's (only guaranteed payments for services included)? Does [member] work in the business, and what does the operating agreement say about their management rights?"

Record the answer and its source in the draft. Do not default either way.

### Codes B and C

- Code B (line 14b): gross farming or fishing income, for the farm optional method.
- Code C (line 14c): gross nonfarm income, for the nonfarm optional method on Schedule SE, Part II.

The Form 1065 instructions do not give a formula for line 14c. The 2025 Instructions for Schedule SE (p. 5) tell the partner how to figure their share: guaranteed payments plus the partner's share of the partnership's gross income after it is reduced by all guaranteed payments; a limited partner includes only guaranteed payments for services. Ask the user's preparer which gross income figure to use as the starting point (page 1, line 8 is the trade or business gross income shown on the return) and record the choice.

### Partner side (not part of this return)

Box 14, code A flows to the partner's Schedule SE; a general partner reduces it there by the section 179 deduction, unreimbursed partnership expenses, and oil and gas depletion (Partner's Instructions for Schedule K-1 (2025), p. 21). Hand off to [`../../schedule-se/SKILL.md`](../../schedule-se/SKILL.md).

## 4. Distributions: Schedule K, line 19 and K-1 box 19 (codes updated for 2025)

| Code | Meaning (Instructions p. 50) | Schedule K line |
|------|-------------------------------|-----------------|
| A | Cash and marketable securities to partners **not** providing services | 19a |
| B | Distributions subject to section 737 (attach statement) | 19b |
| C | Other property (not cash, marketable securities, or section 737 property) | 19b |
| D | Deemed distributions of money under section 752(b) (decrease in share of liabilities) | 19a |
| F | Cash and marketable securities to a partner performing services | 19a |
| G | Other property to a partner performing services | 19b |

Codes F and G apply when (i) the partner performed services for the partnership, (ii) the partnership allocated income and distributed cash, marketable securities, or other property to the partner, and (iii) the partnership treated the transaction as a distribution to a partner. Guaranteed payments for services stay in box 4a, not code F; payments to a partner acting in a non-partner capacity (section 707(a)) are not reported with F or G (Instructions p. 50).

Apply the three-part test to each partner and each distribution. A working member who takes a draw of profits generally meets all three parts; a passive investor does not. If the user is unsure whether a partner "performed services", ask; record the answer.

Report property at the partnership's adjusted basis immediately before the distribution and attach a statement with adjusted basis and FMV for each property (Instructions p. 50). Distributions are not income on the K-1; they reduce capital and the partner's basis.

## 5. Items J and K1

- **Item J** — profit, loss, and capital percentages at the beginning and end of the year, per the agreement. Capital is the share the partner would receive on a liquidation by distribution of undivided interests in assets and liabilities; express zero if the capital account is zero or negative; never negative. Totals equal 100% for each category, excluding the beginning percentage of partners who joined during the year and the ending percentage of partners who left. A partner who joins uses the percentages immediately after admission as "Beginning"; a partner who leaves uses the percentages immediately before termination as "Ending". Check "Sale" or "Exchange" when a decrease came from a taxable sale or a nontaxable exchange of an interest (Instructions p. 33).
- **Item K1** — the partner's share of nonrecourse liabilities, qualified nonrecourse financing, and recourse liabilities, determined under Regulations section 1.752-2. A departing partner shows the share immediately before the disposition (Instructions pp. 33-34). Liability allocation is a legal analysis (who bears the economic risk of loss, guarantees, deficit restoration obligations). **Ask** the user or their CPA for the allocation; check K3 if any listed liability is subject to a partner guarantee or other payment obligation.

## 6. Item L and Schedule M-2: the tax-basis method

Required unless Schedule B, question 4 is "Yes" (Instructions p. 34). Use the transactional approach:

| Item L line | Rule (Instructions pp. 34-35) |
|-------------|-------------------------------|
| Beginning capital account | Last year's ending tax-basis capital. A partner who joined by contributing this year starts at zero |
| Capital contributed during the year | Cash plus adjusted tax basis of property contributed, reduced by liabilities the partnership assumed or that the property was subject to (can be negative) |
| Current year net income (loss) | Partner's share of income and gain **including tax-exempt income**, minus share of losses and deductions **including nondeductible, noncapital expenditures**, all as figured for tax purposes |
| Other increase (decrease) | Everything else, with an explanation statement. Transferee who bought, was given, or inherited an interest: increase equal to the transferor's ending capital for the transferred interest immediately before the transfer. Transferor: decrease by the same amount. Section 734(b) adjustments and certain depletion items also go here |
| Withdrawals and distributions | Cash plus adjusted tax basis of distributed property, reduced by liabilities the partner assumed (can be negative) |
| Ending capital account | Sum of the lines above; can be negative |

Section 743(b) adjustments are not part of tax-basis capital (Instructions p. 35). Guaranteed payments are not part of the partner's distributive share, so they never enter item L.

Item L is not the partner's outside basis: outside basis also includes the partner's share of liabilities and partner-specific adjustments, and the partner tracks it (Instructions p. 34).

### Schedule M-2 tie-out

- Line 1 = sum of beginning item L balances (explain any difference).
- Line 3 = Analysis of Net Income (Loss) per Return, line 1.
- Line 3 contains guaranteed payment income (Analysis line 1 adds back Schedule K, line 4c, and subtracts line 13e, which includes code M medical insurance). Remove the guaranteed payment income contained in line 3 on line 7, so the capital accounts exclude it (Instructions p. 63).
- Tax-exempt income (Schedule K, lines 18a-18b) is not in Analysis line 1: add it on line 4. Nondeductible expenses (line 18c) are not subtracted in Analysis line 1: subtract them on line 7.
- A transfer between partners appears as an equal other increase (transferee) on line 4 and other decrease (transferor) on line 7.
- Line 9 = sum of ending item L balances. If it does not tie, stop and find the difference before producing the draft.
- If Schedule L is kept on the tax basis, Schedule L, line 21 should equal M-2 at both dates; attach a reconciliation if the aggregate capital accounts differ from Schedule L on the tax basis (Instructions p. 63).

## 7. Section 199A: box 20, code Z (Statement A)

Report code Z with an asterisk and "STMT", attaching Statement A (or a substantially similar statement) that gives each partner's share, per trade or business, of qualified items of income, gain, deduction, and loss; W-2 wages; unadjusted basis immediately after acquisition (UBIA) of qualified property; qualified PTP items; and qualified REIT dividends. The statement also flags any specified service trade or business and any aggregation (Statement B) (Instructions, Code Z, p. 54). Ask the user for the W-2 wage amount computed for section 199A and the UBIA of qualified property from the fixed-asset list; do not estimate either. Hand off the partner side to [`../../form-8995/SKILL.md`](../../form-8995/SKILL.md) or [`../../form-8995-a/SKILL.md`](../../form-8995-a/SKILL.md).

## 8. Schedules K-2 and K-3

Schedule K-2 extends Schedule K and Schedule K-3 extends Schedule K-1 for items of international tax relevance (2025 Partnership Instructions for Schedules K-2 and K-3, p. 1). A partnership with no foreign activity can still be required to complete parts of them: for example, Part IV (foreign-derived intangible income) must be completed when a domestic partnership with no foreign activity has direct or indirect domestic corporate partners (same instructions, p. 2). Two exceptions remove the filing requirement:

**Domestic filing exception** (p. 2). All four criteria for the 2025 tax year, and not a QDD partnership:

1. No foreign activity, or only passive category foreign income with not more than $300 of creditable foreign income taxes, shown on a payee statement furnished to the partnership.
2. All direct partners during 2025 are U.S. citizen or resident alien individuals; domestic decedents' estates, domestic grantor trusts, or domestic non-grantor trusts whose grantors and beneficiaries are all U.S. citizens or resident aliens; S corporations; single-member LLCs disregarded and owned by one of those persons; or domestic partnerships whose direct partners are all among those persons. A C corporation partner fails this criterion.
3. Partners are notified, at the latest when K-1s are furnished, that they will not receive Schedule K-3 unless they request it.
4. No partner requests Schedule K-3 information on or before the 1-month date (1 month before the date the partnership files Form 1065; for calendar-year 2025 filers that extend, the latest 1-month date is August 17, 2026).

**Small partnership filing exception** (p. 3). Schedule B, question 4 answered "Yes", plus the same notification and 1-month-date request rule. See [`schedule-b-and-small-partnership.md`](./schedule-b-and-small-partnership.md).

If either exception applies, check Schedule K, line 16b. If neither applies and the partnership has items of international tax relevance, check line 16a, attach Schedule K-2, and check box 16 on each K-1 that has a Schedule K-3. Completing K-2/K-3 parts is out of scope for this skill; refer the user to a CPA.

## 9. Furnishing K-1s

- Furnish a K-1 to every person who was a partner at any time during the year, on or before the day the partnership return is required to be filed (Instructions p. 32), together with the Partner's Instructions for Schedule K-1 or equivalent item-by-item instructions.
- File a copy of every K-1 with the return. Item C on the K-1 says "e-file" or the service center name.
- A DE partner's K-1 goes to the DE, with the beneficial owner's TIN in item E and the DE's name and TIN in item H2.
- The partnership may truncate the partner's SSN or EIN on the partner's copy (***-**-1234 format), never on the IRS copy and never its own EIN (Instructions p. 33).
- A substitute K-1 that is an exact copy of the IRS schedule needs no approval; other substitutes do (Instructions p. 31).
- Penalties for late, missing, or incorrect K-1s are in [`bba-audit-and-penalties.md`](./bba-audit-and-penalties.md).
