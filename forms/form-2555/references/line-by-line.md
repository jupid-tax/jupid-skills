# Form 2555 Line-by-Line Reference

Complete lookup for every line on Form 2555. Use when the agent needs to confirm where a value belongs or what a line means.

Verified against the **2025 Form 2555** (created 5/14/25, filed in 2026) and the **2025 Instructions for Form 2555** (Sep 17, 2025). The form is revised every year; before using this map for a later year, re-check line numbers and dollar amounts at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-2555.

## Part I — General Information

| Line | Field | What goes here |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Your foreign address (including country) | Entire address: city, province, country, foreign postal code. Military/diplomatic address: include the country where the filer lives (i2555 line 1) |
| 2 | Your occupation | Plain description of the work performed abroad |
| 3 | Employer's name | The employer paying the foreign earned income. Self-employed: the filer's business name or "Self" |
| 4a | Employer's U.S. address | If the employer has one |
| 4b | Employer's foreign address | If the employer has one |
| 5 | Employer is (check any that apply) | a A foreign entity; b A U.S. company; c Self; d A foreign affiliate of a U.S. company; e Other (specify) |
| 6a | Last year you filed Form 2555 or 2555-EZ | Year, if previously filed |
| 6b | Never filed to claim either exclusion | Check the box and go to line 7 |
| 6c | Have you ever revoked either of the exclusions? | Yes / No |
| 6d | If 6c is Yes | Type of exclusion revoked (foreign earned income or housing) and the tax year the revocation was effective |
| 7 | Of what country are you a citizen/national? | Matters for resident aliens using the bona fide residence test (treaty-country citizens/nationals only) |
| 8a | Separate foreign residence for family because of adverse living conditions at your tax home? | Yes / No ("Second foreign household" in i2555 Part VI) |
| 8b | If 8a is Yes | City and country of the separate residence and the number of days in the tax year it was maintained |
| 9 | Tax home(s) during the tax year and date(s) established | The regular or principal place of business, employment, or post of duty (i2555 "Tax home test") |

**Revocation check (lines 6c–6d).** A revoked exclusion cannot be claimed again for the next 5 tax years without IRS approval (IRC §911(e)(2); i2555 "Choosing the Exclusion(s)"). Approval is requested as a ruling from the IRS Associate Chief Counsel (International) (Pub. 54, "Effect of Revoking the Exclusions"). Claiming the foreign tax credit, the additional child tax credit, or the earned income credit in a later year is treated as a revocation (Pub. 54, same section). If 6c is Yes and the revocation year is within the last 5 tax years, STOP and ask whether IRS approval was obtained.

**Tax home and abode (line 9).** The filer has no foreign tax home for any period the abode is in the United States, unless serving in a combat zone in support of the U.S. Armed Forces (IRC §911(d)(3); i2555 "Tax home test"). Abode follows family, economic, and personal ties. i2555 example: a 28-days-on / 28-days-off offshore worker who returns to a family residence in the U.S. has a U.S. abode and fails the test.

After Part I, complete **either** Part II **or** Part III, not both (i2555 "Caution" under Part I). Enter "N/A" for items that don't apply.

## Part II — Taxpayers Qualifying Under Bona Fide Residence Test

Who can use it: U.S. citizens, and U.S. resident aliens who are citizens or nationals of a country with which the U.S. has an income tax treaty in effect (i2555 Part II; Table 3 at IRS.gov/TreatyTables). The bona fide residence must be uninterrupted and include an entire tax year (January 1–December 31 for a calendar-year filer) (IRC §911(d)(1)(A)).

| Line | Field | What goes here |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 10 | Date bona fide residence began, and ended | Ending date, or "Continues" if still a bona fide resident (i2555 line 10) |
| 11 | Kind of living quarters | a Purchased house; b Rented house or apartment; c Rented room; d Quarters furnished by employer |
| 12a | Did any of your family live with you abroad during any part of the tax year? | Yes / No |
| 12b | If 12a is Yes: who and for what period | Relationship (child, spouse, parent, etc.) and dates. If 12a is No, leave blank or enter "None" (i2555 lines 12a–12b) |
| 13a | Submitted a statement of nonresidence to the foreign country's authorities? | Yes / No |
| 13b | Required to pay income tax to the country where you claim bona fide residence? | Yes / No |
| 14 | U.S. presence table | (a) date arrived in U.S.; (b) date left U.S.; (c) number of days in U.S. on business; (d) income earned in U.S. on business (attach computation) |
| 15a | Contractual terms or conditions on the length of employment abroad | Text |
| 15b | Type of visa used to enter the foreign country | Text |
| 15c | Did the visa limit the length of stay or employment? | Yes / No; if Yes, attach explanation |
| 15d | Did you maintain a home in the United States while living abroad? | Yes / No |
| 15e | If 15d is Yes | Address of the U.S. home, whether rented, names of occupants, and their relationship to the filer |

**Line 13 disqualifier.** "Yes" on 13a and "No" on 13b means the filer does not qualify as a bona fide resident; do not complete the rest of Part II (form text; IRC §911(d)(5)).

**Line 14, column (d).** Income earned in the U.S. on business is NOT foreign earned income. Do not include it in Part IV; report it on Form 1040 (form text, line 14).

**Partial first and last years.** Once the uninterrupted residence includes one entire tax year, the partial years at either end also count, limited to the days in the qualifying period (i2555 line 31 example: residence from August 14, 2025 through January 31, 2027 gives 140 qualifying days in 2025). If the full year has not yet been completed by the return's due date, see "When to claim the exclusion(s)" in i2555: file Form 2350 for a special extension, or file without the exclusion and amend with Form 1040-X.

## Part III — Taxpayers Qualifying Under Physical Presence Test

Who can use it: U.S. citizens and all resident aliens (form note; IRC §911(d)(1)(B)).

| Line | Field | What goes here |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 16 | The 12-month period, from ... through ... | Must contain 365 days (366 in a leap year), part of which falls in 2025. Enter both dates; never "Continues" (i2555 line 16) |
| 17 | Principal country of employment during the tax year | Country |
| 18 | Travel table | (a) name of country (including U.S.); (b) date arrived; (c) date left; (d) full days present in country; (e) number of days in U.S. on business; (f) income earned in U.S. on business (attach computation) |

**Line 18 rules (form text).** List travel during the 12-month period on line 16. Exclude travel between foreign countries that did not involve travel on or over international waters, or in or over the United States, for 24 hours or more. If there was no travel, enter "Physically present in a foreign country or countries for the entire 12-month period." Income in column (f) is not foreign earned income: report it on Form 1040, not in Part IV.

**Counting full days.** A full day is the 24-hour period starting at midnight (i2555 Part III). Days need not be consecutive. Time on or over international waters when leaving or returning to the United States does not count. Travel from one foreign country to another loses no full days unless the part outside any foreign country takes 24 hours or more (IRS "Foreign earned income exclusion — physical presence test" page, "Change of location" examples: London 11 p.m. → Stockholm 5 a.m. loses no days; Norway → Portugal by ship over 24+ hours loses July 6, 7 and 8). Transit through the U.S. for less than 24 hours between two foreign points is treated as travel over areas not within any foreign country (same page). "Foreign country" includes territorial waters and airspace but not international waters, U.S. territories, or Antarctica (i2555 "Foreign country").

**Choosing the window.** The 12-month period can begin on any day and ends the day before the same calendar day 12 months later; periods may overlap from year to year; pick the one that gives the largest exclusion (IRS physical presence page, "How to figure the 12-month period").

## Part IV — All Taxpayers (2025 Foreign Earned Income)

Report in U.S. dollars, using the exchange rates in effect when the income was actually or constructively received (form note). Do not include line 14 column (d) or line 18 column (f) amounts. A cash-basis filer reports on Form 1040 all income received in 2025 regardless of when the services were performed.

If capital is not a material income-producing factor, the entire gross income from the business is earned income; if both personal services and capital are material, a reasonable amount for services, not more than 30% of the filer's share of net profits after the deduction for the employer-equivalent portion of SE tax (i2555 line 20) |

| 21c | Noncash income: car | Same |

| 22a | Allowances, reimbursements, or expenses paid on your behalf: cost of living and overseas differential | |
| 22b | Family | |

| 22d | Home leave | |
| 22e | Quarters | |

**Not foreign earned income** (i2555 Part IV; IRC §911(b)(1)(B)): pensions and annuities (including Social Security), interest, ordinary dividends, capital gains, alimony, pay to a U.S. Government employee from the U.S. Government or its agencies, amounts received after the end of the tax year following the year the services were performed, and amounts included because of employer contributions to a nonexempt trust or nonqualified annuity. Distributions of corporate earnings and profits are not compensation either.

**Timing rules** (i2555 Part IV). Income received in 2025 for services performed in 2024 is excluded under 2024's limit: leave it out of Part IV, attach a computation, and enter the excludable amount to the left of line 45 with "Exclusion of Income Earned in 2024", included in the line 45 total. Income received in 2024 for 2025 services is claimed by amending 2024 on Form 1040-X.

## Part VI — Taxpayers Claiming the Housing Exclusion and/or Deduction

Both employees (exclusion) and self-employed filers (deduction) start here. The housing deduction itself is figured in Part IX.

| Line | Field | Computation / source |
|------|-------|----------------------|
| 28 | Qualified housing expenses for the tax year | Reasonable expenses paid or incurred by the filer or on the filer's behalf for foreign housing for the filer and spouse/dependents living with the filer, plus a qualifying second foreign household (i2555 line 28) |
| 29a | Location where housing expenses were incurred | City and country, only if the location is listed in Notice 2025-16; otherwise leave blank |
| 29b | Limit on housing expenses | $39,000 if the location is not listed and line 31 is 365; otherwise use the Limit on Housing Expenses Worksheet—Line 29b (below) |
| 30 | Smaller of line 28 or line 29b | |
| 31 | Number of days in the qualifying period that fall within the 2025 tax year | Qualifying period = the period the filer meets the tax home test and either test (i2555 line 31) |
| 32 | $56.99 × line 31 | If line 31 is 365, enter $20,800 (base housing amount, 16% of $130,000) |
| 33 | Line 30 minus line 32 | If zero or less, stop: do not complete the rest of Part VI or any of Part IX |
| 34 | Employer-provided amounts | Any amount the employer paid or incurred on the filer's behalf that is foreign earned income included in gross income without regard to §911: wages and salaries, fair rental value of employer lodging not excluded on line 25, rent paid by the employer to the landlord, housing and education reimbursements, tax equalization payments (i2555 line 34; IRC §911(c)(4)(D)) |
| 35 | Line 34 ÷ line 27 | Decimal rounded to at least three places, not more than 1.000 |
| 36 | Housing exclusion: line 33 × line 35 | Not more than line 34. Also complete Part VIII |

**Housing expenses include** (i2555 line 28): rent, utilities other than telephone charges, real and personal property insurance, nonrefundable fees paid to obtain a lease, rental of furniture and accessories, residential parking, household repairs, and the fair rental value of employer-provided housing not excluded on line 25.

**Housing expenses exclude** (i2555 line 28): deductible interest and taxes, amounts deductible by a cooperative tenant-stockholder, the cost of buying or improving a house, mortgage principal, depreciation, domestic labor, pay television, buying furniture or accessories, and lavish or extravagant amounts. Count only expenses for periods when housing value is not excluded under §119 and the filer meets the tax home test and one of the two tests.

**Employees who pay their own rent still get the exclusion.** Line 34 counts wages, not only housing paid by the employer. A filer with no self-employment income has line 34 = line 27, so line 35 = 1.000 and the whole housing amount is excluded (Pub. 54, "Foreign Housing Exclusion": "If you do not have self-employment income, all of your earnings are employer-provided amounts").

**Self-employed filers.** If all foreign earned income is self-employment income, skip lines 34 and 35 and enter zero on line 36; the housing amount is figured as a deduction in Part IX (i2555 line 34). A filer with both wages and SE income excludes the employer-provided share (line 35 ratio) and deducts the rest in Part IX (Pub. 54 example: $18,000 housing amount, half of $100,000 FEI from SE → $9,000 excluded, $9,000 deductible subject to the limit).

**Married couples in one household** may figure housing jointly or separately on a joint return; separately if filing separate returns. Jointly: one spouse (not both) claims it, aggregating both spouses' expenses subject to the line 29b limit and that spouse's base amount (i2555 "Married couples").

**Cuba.** Housing expenses in Cuba while in violation of U.S. travel restrictions are not qualified housing expenses (i2555 "Travel to Cuba"; IRC §911(d)(8)).

### Limit on Housing Expenses Worksheet—Line 29b (i2555 p.6)

Skip it when the location is not listed in Notice 2025-16 and line 31 is 365: enter $39,000.

1. Days in the qualifying period that fall within 2025 (same as line 31)
2. If line 1 is 365: enter the "Limitation on Housing Expenses (full year)" amount for the location from Notice 2025-16 on line 29b and stop
3. Otherwise: the "Limitation on Housing Expenses (daily/365 days)" amount for the location from Notice 2025-16, or $106.85 if not listed
4. Line 1 × line 3 → line 29b

Moving between locations: one worksheet per location, using the days lived there; add the line 4 results; the total days may not exceed line 31 (i2555 "More than one foreign location").

**Election to use the next year's limits.** Notice 2026-25 §4 lets a filer incurring 2025 housing expenses in a listed location apply the 2026 table instead of Notice 2025-16 where the 2026 limit is higher (e.g., London $68,600 for 2026 vs $67,000 for 2025; Lisbon $44,800 vs $40,000). Ask the user before applying it and cite the notice in the draft.

## Part VIII — Taxpayers Claiming the Housing Exclusion, Foreign Earned Income Exclusion, or Both

| Line | Field | Computation |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 43 | Add lines 36 and 42 | Total exclusions |
| 44 | Deductions allowed in figuring AGI that are allocable to the excluded income | Attach computation. Report the deductions in full on Schedule 1 and related forms; enter here the part that is not allowed because it is definitely related to the excluded income, such as the deductible part of self-employment tax and Schedule C expenses (i2555 line 44). The 2025 form text says "Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 11"; on the 2025 Form 1040 AGI is line 11a |
| 45 | Line 43 minus line 44 | Enter on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8d (as a negative amount). Complete the Foreign Earned Income Tax Worksheet in the Form 1040 instructions |

**Line 44 allocation.** If only part of the foreign earned income is excluded, prorate definitely related items by the ratio of excludable earned income to total foreign earned income (i2555 Part VIII). The deduction for self-employment tax is allocable to excluded income; the housing deduction and the §119 exclusion are not (Pub. 54, ch. 5 "Items Related to Excluded Income"; i2555 Part VIII). Items not definitely related to any income (qualified retirement contributions, alimony, charitable contributions, medical expenses, mortgage interest, real estate taxes on a personal residence) are not allocated (Pub. 54, same section). Statutory basis: IRC §911(d)(6).

**Schedule 1 / Form 1040 flow** (i2555 line 45). Line 45 → Schedule 1 line 8d (negative) → Schedule 1 line 9 and line 10 → Form 1040 line 8 (in parentheses if negative). Form 1040 line 1z wages are NOT reduced directly.

## Downstream: Foreign Earned Income Tax Worksheet (Form 1040 line 16)

From the 2025 Instructions for Form 1040, p.37. Skip it if Form 1040 line 15 (taxable income) is zero.

1. Form 1040 line 15
2a. Form 2555 lines 45 and 50 (both spouses if joint)
2b. Itemized deductions or exclusions not allowed because related to excluded income
2c. 2a minus 2b (not below zero)
3. Line 1 + line 2c
4. Tax on line 3 (Tax Table, Tax Computation Worksheet, Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet, Schedule D Tax Worksheet, or Form 8615; with the capital gain excess modifications in the worksheet footnote)
5. Tax on line 2c (Tax Table if under $100,000, otherwise Tax Computation Worksheet)

## Special situations

### Filer changes country mid-year

Bona fide residence: the uninterrupted residence can span several foreign countries; moving back to the United States ends it. Physical presence: days in any foreign country count; only the 330-day total in the chosen 12-month window matters. Housing: one Limit on Housing Expenses Worksheet per location.

### Both spouses have foreign earned income

Each qualifying spouse files a separate Form 2555 with the joint return and computes Part VII separately; each can exclude up to the maximum (Pub. 54, "Limit on Excludable Amount"). Housing follows the married-couple rules in Part VI.

### Resident alien using bona fide residence

Only if a citizen or national of a country with a U.S. income tax treaty in effect (i2555 Part II). Otherwise use Part III.

### Waiver of time requirements (war, civil unrest)

IRC §911(d)(4). The IRS lists the qualifying countries and departure dates each year; for 2025 it is Rev. Proc. 2026-16 (Haiti and Ukraine from January 1, 2025; Democratic Republic of the Congo from January 28; South Sudan from March 7; Iraq from June 11; Lebanon from June 22; Mali from October 30, 2025). Write "Claiming Waiver" in the top margin of page 1 and attach the statement (i2555 "Waiver of Time Requirements").

### Combat zone

A citizen or resident serving in a combat zone in support of the U.S. Armed Forces can have a foreign tax home even with a U.S. abode (IRC §911(d)(3); i2555 "Service in a combat zone").

### U.S. Government employees

Pay to a U.S. Government employee from the U.S. Government or its agencies is not foreign earned income; such income qualifies for neither exclusion nor the housing deduction. Don't file Form 2555 for it (i2555 "Who Qualifies" note; IRC §911(b)(1)(B)(ii)).

### Cuba

Time in Cuba in violation of U.S. travel restrictions does not count toward either test; income earned there is not foreign earned income (i2555 "Travel to Cuba"; IRC §911(d)(8)).

### U.S. employer, work performed abroad

Pay from a U.S. employer for services performed in a foreign country is foreign earned income if the other tests are met. Taxes an employer withheld and paid to the foreign tax authority do not go on Form 1040 line 25a/25b (i2555 "Caution").

### Foreign employer, work performed in the United States

U.S.-source. Report it on line 14 column (d) (Part II filers) or line 18 column (f) (Part III filers) with a computation, and on Form 1040; never in Part IV.
