# Schedule H Line-by-Line Reference

Every line of Schedule H (Form 1040), built from the text of the 2025 Schedule H (Created 4/15/25) and the 2025 Instructions for Schedule H (dated Dec 1, 2025), with the 2026 draft Schedule H (Created 5/8/26) and 2026 draft instructions (dated Aug 19, 2026) side by side. Line numbers did not change. Re-check the final 2026 form at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-schedule-h-form-1040.

Source abbreviations: **SH25** = 2025 Schedule H; **ISH25** = 2025 Instructions for Schedule H; **SH26D / ISH26D** = 2026 drafts; **P926** = Publication 926 (2026).

### Line A exclusions (ISH25, line A; P926, "Wages not counted")

Do not count amounts paid to:

- Your spouse.
- Your child who was under age 21.
- Your parent, unless the exception for parents applies.
- An employee who was under age 18 at any time during the year, unless the exception for employees under 18 applies.

**Exception for parents.** Count your parent's cash wages for work in or around your home if both: (1) your child (including an adopted child or stepchild) who lived with you was under 18, or had a physical or mental condition requiring the personal care of an adult for at least 4 continuous weeks in the calendar quarter of the services; and (2) you were divorced and not remarried, a widow or widower, or married to and living with a person whose physical or mental condition prevented your spouse from caring for the child for at least 4 continuous weeks in that quarter. (IRC §3121(b)(3)(B).)

**Exception for employees under 18.** Count the cash wages of a person under 18 who was not a student if providing household services was the employee's principal occupation. For a student, household work is never the principal occupation (P926). (IRC §3121(b)(21).)

**Cash wages.** Paid by check, money order, and similar means. Not the value of food, lodging, clothing, transit passes, or other noncash items; cash given in place of those items is cash wages. Noncash wages are not Social Security or Medicare wages but are subject to income tax unless excluded; report them in W-2 box 1 only (ISH25, "Cash wages").

**Transportation benefits.** Cash reimbursements for qualified parking, commuter highway vehicle, or transit passes may be excluded from Social Security and Medicare cash wages up to $325 per month each for 2025 (ISH25) and $340 per month each for 2026 (ISH26D; P926). Reimbursements above the limit are wages.

**Test year.** The test applies to cash wages paid in the year regardless of when earned (ISH25, "$2,800 test").

## Part I — Social Security, Medicare, and Federal Income Taxes

| Line | Text | Computation and rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Total cash wages subject to social security tax | Cash wages paid in the year to each employee who meets the line A test, including the first dollars. Per employee, include only the first $176,100 (2025) / $184,500 (2026) (ISH25 line 1 caution; ISH26D; P926). |
| 2 | Social security tax | Line 1 × 12.4% (0.124) |
| 3 | Total cash wages subject to Medicare tax | Cash wages of each employee who meets the test; no limit (ISH25 line 3) |
| 4 | Medicare tax | Line 3 × 2.9% (0.029) |
| 5 | Total cash wages subject to Additional Medicare Tax withholding | Each employee's cash wages paid in the year that exceeded $200,000 (ISH25 line 5) |
| 6 | Additional Medicare Tax withholding | Line 5 × 0.9% (0.009). Employee-only tax; no employer share (ISH25 Part I) |
| 7 | Federal income tax withheld, if any | Withholding is optional; only if the employee asked and gave a completed Form W-4 and you agreed (ISH25 Part I; P926). See Pub. 926 and Pub. 15-T for amounts. |
| 8 | Total social security, Medicare, and federal income taxes | Lines 2 + 4 + 6 + 7. State disability adjustment: see below. |
| 9 | Did you pay total cash wages of $1,000 or more in any calendar quarter of 2024 or 2025 [2025 or 2026] to all household employees? (excluding spouse, child under 21, parent) | No → stop; include line 8 on Schedule 2 (Form 1040) line 9 (2025) / line 17a (2026 draft); if no Form 1040, see the line 9 instructions (Part IV). Yes → line 10. |

**State disability payments (ISH25, "State Disability Payments").** If a state disability plan that withheld the employee's share made payments treated as wages, include them on lines 1, 3, and 5 if applicable, complete Part I through line 7, add lines 2, 4, 6, and 7, subtract the taxes the state withheld, enter the result on line 8, and write "disability" and the amount subtracted on the dotted line next to line 8.

**Employee share paid by the employer.** The employer must pay both shares even if it did not withhold (ISH25 Part I). If the employer paid the employee's share without deducting it, that tax is added to income-tax wages (W-2 box 1) but is not Social Security, Medicare, or FUTA wages (ISH25, "Employee's portion of taxes paid by employer").

## Part II — Federal Unemployment (FUTA) Tax

FUTA is 6.0% of the first $7,000 paid to each employee, reduced by a credit of up to 5.4% for state contributions paid by April 15 of the next year, for a net 0.6%. The employer pays FUTA from its own funds; never withhold it from the employee (ISH25 Part II).

| Line | Question | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Did you pay unemployment contributions to only one state? If you paid contributions to a credit reduction state, see instructions and check "No" | Credit reduction state → No |
| 11 | Did you pay all state unemployment contributions for 2025 by April 15, 2026? [2026 by April 15, 2027] Fiscal year filers, see instructions | Fiscal-year filers: Yes if paid by the return due date, not including extensions (ISH25, "Fiscal year filers") |
| 12 | Were all wages that are taxable for FUTA tax also taxable for your state's unemployment tax? | |

All three Yes → Section A. Any No → Section B.

### Section A

| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 13 | Two-letter abbreviation of the state (DC, PR, VI included) |
| 14 | Total contributions paid to the state unemployment fund for the year; if the state gave a 0% experience rate, enter "0% rate" |
| 15 | Total cash wages paid in the year to each household employee, including employees paid less than $1,000, but not spouse, child under 21, or parent; only the first $7,000 per employee |
| 16 | Line 15 × 0.6% (0.006); skip Section B; go to line 25 |

Line 15 includes employees under 18: the under-18 exclusion applies to line A and Part I, not to FUTA (ISH25 line 15 lists only spouse, child under 21, and parent; P926 FUTA "Wages not counted").

### Section B (complete only if a "No" box was checked on line 10, 11, or 12)

**Line 17 columns.** Complete all columns that apply or the credit is lost. Columns (a), (b), and (h) are always required; with an experience rate of 5.4% or higher, also (c) and (d); with a rate below 5.4%, all columns. Use a separate row for each rate period. Attach a statement in the same format if more room is needed (ISH25 line 17).

| Column | Entry |
|---|---|
| (a) | Name of state |
| (b) | Taxable wages as defined in the state act. With a 0% rate, the wages that would have been taxed without the 0% rate |
| (c) | State experience rate period, From and To |
| (d) | State experience rate |
| (e) | Column (b) × 0.054 |
| (f) | Column (b) × column (d) |
| (g) | Column (e) − column (f); if zero or less, enter -0- |
| (h) | Contributions paid to the state unemployment fund for the year by April 15 of the next year (fiscal-year filers: by the return due date without extensions). Attach the state's letter if claiming excess credits as payments |

| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 18 | Totals of columns (g) and (h) |
| 19 | Line 18 (g) + line 18 (h) |
| 20 | Total cash wages subject to FUTA tax (same rules as line 15) |
| 21 | Line 20 × 6.0% (0.06) |
| 22 | Line 20 × 5.4% (0.054) |
| 23 | Smaller of line 19 or line 22. If contributions were paid late or the state is a credit reduction state, use Worksheet 1 and/or Worksheet 2 instead and check the box |
| 24 | Line 21 − line 23; go to line 25 |

"Late" means paid after the due date for filing Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-SS, not including extensions. A credit reduction state is one with a rate greater than zero in Worksheet 2. If both apply, complete Worksheet 1 first (ISH25 line 23). Worksheets: [`futa-section-b-worksheets.md`](./futa-section-b-worksheets.md).

## Part IV — Address and Signature

Complete only if Schedule H is filed by itself (line 27 instructions). Address (number and street or P.O. box; apt., room, or suite), city, state, ZIP. Signature under penalties of perjury, including the statement that no part of any payment to a state unemployment fund claimed as a credit was or will be deducted from employees' pay. Date.

**Paid Preparer Use Only.** Completed only if the preparer was paid, is not the filer's employee, and Schedule H is not attached to Form 1040, 1040-SR, 1040-SS, 1040-NR, or 1041 (ISH25, "Paid Preparers"). Preparer name, signature, date, self-employed box, PTIN, firm name, firm EIN, firm address, phone.
