# Schedule C (Form 1120): Dividends and the Dividends-Received Deduction

How to classify dividends a corporation receives, compute the special deduction in column (c), and apply the section 246(b) taxable-income limit. Built from the 2025 Instructions for Form 1120, Schedule C and its "Worksheet for Schedule C, Lines 9 and 22". Re-check the worksheet line numbers each year at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1120.

Schedule C (Form 1120) is the corporate dividends schedule on page 2 of Form 1120. It is unrelated to Schedule C (Form 1040), the sole proprietor's profit or loss schedule.

---

## 1. Facts to collect for every dividend

Ask for each payer, and do not assume any of these:

1. **Domestic or foreign payer**, and whether it is subject to U.S. income tax.
2. **Ownership percentage by vote and by value** at the time of the dividend. The 20% test on lines 1 through 7 uses voting power and value; preferred stock described in section 1504(a)(4) is not counted (Instr., Schedule C).
3. **Debt-financed stock?** Was the stock bought with borrowed money (section 246A, stock acquired after July 18, 1984)?
4. **Holding period.** Was the share held at least 46 days during the 91-day period beginning 45 days before the ex-dividend date? For preferred stock dividends attributable to periods totaling more than 366 days: at least 91 days during the 181-day period beginning 90 days before the ex-dividend date. Days when the risk of loss was diminished do not count (section 246(c)(4); Instr., Line 20).
5. **Related-payment obligations.** Is the corporation obligated (for example, through a short sale) to make related payments on substantially similar property? If yes, no deduction (Instr., Line 20).
6. **Payer type.** Regulated investment company (RIC) dividends: only the portion the RIC designates as eligible for the deduction (section 854(b)); REIT dividends: no deduction; tax-exempt organization dividends: no deduction.
7. **Affiliated group or consolidated return?** Members joining in a consolidated return eliminate intercompany dividends and do not report them on Schedule C (Instr., Consolidated returns).

Line 23 = column (a) lines 9 through 20 → page 1 line 4. Line 24 = column (c) lines 9 through 22 → page 1 line 29b.

## 3. The section 246(b) taxable-income limit (Worksheet for Schedule C, Lines 9 and 22)

Complete page 1 through line 28, Schedule C lines 1 through 8 and 10 through 13, and Form 8993 Part III lines 28 and 29 (if any) first. The worksheet (2025 Instructions, page 20) runs:

| Ws line | Computation |
|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Refigure page 1 line 28 without any section 1059 adjustment and without any capital loss carryback |
| 2 | Schedule C lines 10, 11, 12, 13, column (c) |
| 3 | Line 1 − line 2 |
| 4 | Line 3 × 65% |
| 5 | Schedule C lines 2, 5, 7, 8 column (c) + the part of line 3 column (c) from 20%-or-more-owned corporations |
| 6 | Form 8993 Part III lines 28 + 29 |
| 7 | Line 5 + line 6 |
| 8 | Line 4 − line 7. If zero or more: enter the line 5 amount, skip lines 9–15, go to line 16. If less than zero: leave blank, go to line 9 |
| 9 | Line 5 ÷ line 7 (decimal, at least three places) |
| 10 | Line 7 − line 4 |
| 11 | Line 10 × line 9 |
| 12 | Line 5 − line 11 |
| 13 | 1.000 − line 9 |
| 14 | Line 13 × line 10 |
| 15 | Line 6 − line 14 |
| 16 | Total dividends from 20%-or-more-owned corporations in column (a) lines 2, 3, 5, 7, 8 |
| 17 | Line 3 − line 16 |
| 18 | Line 17 × 50% |
| 19 | Schedule C lines 1, 4, 6 column (c) + the part of line 3 column (c) not from 20%-or-more-owned corporations |
| 20 | Line 15 (or line 6 if line 15 is blank) + line 19 |
| 21 | Line 18 − line 20. If zero or more: enter the line 19 amount, skip lines 22–28, go to line 29. If less than zero: leave blank, go to line 22 |
| 22 | Line 19 ÷ line 20 (decimal, at least three places) |
| 23 | Line 20 − line 18 |
| 24 | Line 23 × line 22 |
| 25 | Line 19 − line 24 |
| 26 | 1.000 − line 22 |
| 27 | Line 23 × line 26 |
| 28 | Line 15 (or line 6 if line 15 is blank) − line 27 |
| 29 | Dividends-received deduction after limit: line 12 (or line 8 if line 12 is blank) + line 25 (or line 19 if line 25 is blank) → Schedule C line 9 column (c) |
| 30 | Section 250 deduction after limit: line 28 (or line 15, or line 6) → Schedule C line 22 column (c) |

What the worksheet enforces: deductions for 20%-or-more-owned dividends cannot exceed 65% of the line 3 taxable income, and deductions for less-than-20%-owned dividends cannot exceed 50% of the line 3 taxable income reduced by the 20%-or-more-owned dividends (IRC §246(b)(1) and (3)).

**Edge case.** When line 17 is negative (taxable income on line 3 is smaller than the 20%-or-more-owned dividends), the arithmetic on lines 18 and 25 can produce a negative number. A deduction cannot be negative. Stop and have the CPA or tax software confirm the line 9 amount.

### NOL-year exception

The limit does not apply in a year in which the corporation has a net operating loss, even if the full dividends-received deduction creates the loss (Instr., Line 9, Column (c), which cites sections 172(c), 172(d)(5), and 246(b)). Test: if page 1 line 28 minus the full, unlimited column (c) total is less than zero, the limit does not apply and the full deduction is allowed. If the result is zero or more, apply the worksheet.

## 4. Worked mini-example (limit binds)

Facts: line 28 = $52,940; $48,600 of dividends from a 25%-owned domestic corporation (line 2); $8,214 of eligible portfolio dividends from less-than-20%-owned domestic corporations (line 1); no section 250 items.

- Unlimited column (c): $48,600 × 65% = $31,590; $8,214 × 50% = $4,107; total $35,697.
- NOL test: $52,940 − $35,697 = $17,243, not a loss, so the limit applies.
- Ws 1 = 52,940; 2 = 0; 3 = 52,940; 4 = 34,411; 5 = 31,590; 6 = 0; 7 = 31,590; 8 = 2,821 ≥ 0, so line 8 = 31,590 and lines 9–15 are skipped.
- Ws 16 = 48,600; 17 = 4,340; 18 = 2,170; 19 = 4,107; 20 = 4,107; 21 = −1,937 < 0, so go to line 22.
- Ws 22 = 1.000; 23 = 1,937; 24 = 1,937; 25 = 2,170; 26 = 0.000; 27 = 0; 28 = 0.
- Ws 29 = 31,590 + 2,170 = **$33,760** on Schedule C line 9 column (c). The 65% class is allowed in full; the 50% class is cut from $4,107 to $2,170.

## 5. Common errors

- Putting REIT dividends or dividends that fail the 46-day test on line 1. They go on line 20 with no deduction.
- Using voting power alone for the 20% test. The instructions use voting power and value.
- Deducting the full amount without the worksheet when page 1 line 28 is small relative to dividends.
- Applying the limit in an NOL year. It does not apply when the full deduction produces or increases a loss.
- Reporting dividends from a consolidated group member on Schedule C. They are eliminated in consolidation.
- Netting Schedule C into page 1 line 4 at the net amount. Line 4 takes gross dividends (line 23); the deduction goes on line 29b.
