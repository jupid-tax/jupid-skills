# Form 1120: Schedule K Questions, the $250,000 Test, and Schedules L, M-1, M-2

How to answer the Schedule K questions on pages 4–5 of the 2025 Form 1120, which answers add a form, how question 13 decides whether Schedules L, M-1, and M-2 are required, and how to complete and tie those schedules. Built from the 2025 Form 1120 and the 2025 Instructions for Form 1120. Recheck question numbers each year at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1120.

---

Answer every question. A blank is an incomplete return. Ask the user for each fact; do not infer ownership percentages from names or titles.

**Question 15a for 2026 payments:** the section 6041 reporting threshold is $2,000 for payments made after December 31, 2025 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32, §2.15). The 2025 return asks about payments made in 2025 under the rules for that year.

## 2. Question 13: the $250,000 test

The form asks: "Are the corporation's total receipts (page 1, line 1a, plus lines 4 through 10) for the tax year and its total assets at the end of the tax year less than $250,000?"

- **Total receipts** = line 1a (gross receipts before returns) + line 4 + line 5 + line 6 + line 7 + line 8 + line 9 + line 10. Use the line 9 and line 8 amounts as entered (a Form 4797 loss on line 9 is a negative number on that line; ask the CPA how to treat it if it changes the answer).
- **Total assets** = year-end total assets per books (item D).
- Both must be under $250,000.

If "Yes": Schedules L, M-1, and M-2 are not required. Enter on the question 13 line the total cash distributions plus the book value of property distributions (other than cash) made during the year (Form, Question 13). Item D is still completed (Instr., Item D).

If "No": complete Schedules L, M-1 (or M-3), and M-2.

Comparison with Form 1120-S: on Form 1120-S, Schedule B question 11 excuses only Schedules L and M-1. On Form 1120, question 13 excuses L, M-1, and M-2.

## 3. Schedule M-3 boundary

- Total assets (nonconsolidated, or consolidated for the group) of $10 million or more on the last day of the year: file Schedule M-3 (Form 1120) instead of Schedule M-1 and check item A box 4 (Instr., Schedule M-3).
- A corporation required to file M-3 with less than $50 million of total assets, or one filing M-3 voluntarily, may complete M-3 Part I and Schedule M-1 instead of M-3 Parts II and III; Schedule M-1 line 1 must then equal Schedule M-3 Part I line 11.
- The agent flags M-3 and hands off. It does not prepare M-3.
- The $10 million line also changes the paper filing address (filing.md).

## 4. Schedule L: balance sheets per books

- Use the corporation's books, not tax figures. Beginning-of-year columns must equal last year's end-of-year columns; ask for last year's return if the user does not have the prior balance sheet.
- Line 1 cash includes certificates of deposit. Line 5 is state and local obligations and RIC stock paying exempt-interest dividends. Lines 7 and 19 are loans to and from shareholders: ask whether founder advances were documented as loans; do not reclassify without the user's records. Line 25 unappropriated retained earnings ties to Schedule M-2. Line 26 holds equity adjustments such as unrealized gains and losses on available-for-sale securities and foreign currency translation adjustments (Instr., Line 26).
- Ties: line 15 = line 28 in each column; item D = line 15 column (d).

## 5. Schedule M-1: book income to taxable income

Line 10 must equal page 1 line 28 (taxable income before NOL and special deductions). The NOL deduction and Schedule C special deductions are not M-1 items.

| M-1 line | Typical entries |
|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Net income (loss) per books, after book income tax expense |
| 2 | Federal income tax per books (add back; never deductible) |
| 3 | Excess of capital losses over capital gains (book loss not allowed on the return) |
| 4 | Taxable income not on books this year (itemize) |
| 5a | Book depreciation above tax depreciation |
| 5b | Book charitable contributions above the amount on line 19 (carryover year) |
| 5c | Nondeductible travel and entertainment: 50% of meals, entertainment, nondeductible club dues, gifts over $25, qualified transportation fringes |
| 7 | Book income not taxable this year (itemize); tax-exempt interest here and on question 9 |
| 8a | Tax depreciation above book depreciation (bonus depreciation, section 179) |
| 8b | Charitable carryover deducted on the return but expensed on the books in a prior year |
| 8 (other) | Other return deductions not on the books (itemize), such as amortization of research costs expensed for books in earlier years |
| 10 | Line 6 − line 9 = page 1 line 28 |

If the books record deferred income taxes, line 2 uses the federal income tax expense per books; ask the user for the book tax expense figure rather than assuming it equals Schedule J line 12.

## 6. Schedule M-2: unappropriated retained earnings

| Line | Entry |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Beginning balance = Schedule L line 25 column (b) |
| 2 | Net income (loss) per books = M-1 line 1 |
| 3 | Other increases (itemize) |
| 4 | 1 + 2 + 3 |
| 5a/5b/5c | Distributions: cash, stock, property |
| 6 | Other decreases (itemize) |
| 7 | 5 + 6 |
| 8 | 4 − 7 = Schedule L line 25 column (d) |

Distributions to shareholders appear here (or on the question 13 line), never on page 1. Dividends paid require Forms 1099-DIV to the shareholders. If the user says distributions exceeded earnings and profits, question 6 is "Yes" and Form 5452 applies; hand off the earnings-and-profits computation.
