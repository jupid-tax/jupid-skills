# Form 1120 Line-by-Line Reference (2025 revision)

Every line of the 2025 Form 1120 (form created 9/26/25), mapped from the form PDF text and the 2025 Instructions for Form 1120 (dated Jan 15, 2026). Use it to place an amount or to confirm what a line means. Before using it for a later year, compare the line numbers against the current form at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1120.

Conventions: "Instr." = 2025 Instructions for Form 1120. Round to whole dollars consistently or use cents everywhere; when adding amounts, round only the total (Instr., Rounding Off to Whole Dollars).

### Signature block

- Signed and dated by the president, vice president, treasurer, assistant treasurer, chief accounting officer, or another officer authorized to sign; a receiver, trustee, or assignee signs for a corporation in their charge (Instr., Who Must Sign).
- "May the IRS discuss this return with the preparer shown below?" authorizes only the individual paid preparer; it ends no later than the due date (excluding extensions) of the 2026 return.
- Paid preparer: name, signature, date, PTIN, firm name, firm EIN, address, phone. Leave blank if an employee or unpaid person prepared it.
