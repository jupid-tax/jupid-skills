# Filing Form 1120

How an agent takes a finished Form 1120 draft to a filed return: choosing the channel, the e-file mandate, signatures, payment, extensions, paper addresses, and the consent and security rules. Produce and validate the draft with `SKILL.md` first.

There is no IRS free online fillable option for corporate income tax returns. The IRS says: "Corporations must use approved software and/or a tax professional who is an authorized e-file provider" (IRS, Form 1120/1120-F/1120-H/1120-L/1120-PC/1120-REIT/1120-RIC/1120-S e-file page). Paper remains available only when the e-file mandate does not apply.

---

## 1. Channel decision tree

```
Does a CPA, EA, or other paid preparer handle the corporation's return?
  → Section 2: hand them the draft package. They e-file under their EFIN.

Is the corporation required to e-file? Count every return the corporation was required to
file during the calendar year ending with or within its tax year (for a calendar-2025 Form
1120: returns due in calendar 2025, such as the 2024 Form 1120, Forms 941, 940, W-2, 1099).
Count income tax, employment tax, excise tax, and information returns. Members of a
controlled group count their returns together. 10 or more → e-file required for returns
filed on or after January 1, 2024, unless the IRS grants a waiver.
  YES → Section 3: approved MeF business software (1120x provider list), or a preparer.
  NO  → Section 3 (e-file, recommended) or Section 4 (paper).

Does the corporation need more time?
  → Form 7004 by the original due date (Section 5), then come back to this tree.
```

Source for the mandate: Instructions for Form 1120 (2025), Electronic Filing; Treasury Reg. §301.6011-5(a)(1)–(2) and (d)(5) (https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-301/section-301.6011-5). Waiver requests go to the Ogden Submission Processing Center, Attn: Form 1120 e-file Waiver Request, Mail Stop 1057, Ogden, UT 84201 (fax 877-477-0575); a corporation exempt on religious grounds writes "Religious Exemption" at the top of page 1 and files on paper (same section; Notice 2024-18).

## 2. Handoff to a paid preparer

Give the preparer:

- The completed draft (every line, Schedules C, J, K, and L/M-1/M-2 or the question 13 note)
- The trial balance or financial statements, the prior-year Form 1120, the NOL and charitable carryover statements
- Payroll reports and Forms W-2/W-3 for the officer compensation tie-out
- Brokerage statements for dividends (with ownership percentages and holding-period notes)
- The estimated tax payment log with dates and EFTPS confirmation numbers
- The list of boundary items the agent flagged (CAMT, PHC, section 382, M-3, foreign items)

The preparer signs the "Paid Preparer Use Only" block and gives the corporation a copy (Instr., Who Must Sign). The officer authorizes the e-filed return on Form 8879-C, IRS e-file Signature Authorization for Form 1120, which the electronic return originator keeps.

---

## 3. E-file through approved software

1. Use a product listed on the IRS page "Approved IRS e-file for business providers" under "1120x Modernized e-File (MeF) providers" (https://www.irs.gov/e-file-providers/approved-irs-e-file-for-business-providers). The IRS does not endorse these products; confirm the product supports Form 1120 and every attached form and schedule in the draft.
2. Pre-flight. The agent must have: the user's permission to work in the software account; the EIN (required for e-file; "Applied For" is not accepted electronically); the officer's name and title; the completed draft; every attachment the draft lists; bank details only if the user wants direct deposit or an electronic funds withdrawal.
3. Enter the return from the draft line by line. Where the software computes a line (1c, 3, 11, 27, 28, 29c, 30, Schedule J totals, 33–36), compare its figure to the draft. If they differ, stop; one of them is wrong.
4. Attach statements in the order the instructions give (Instr., Assembling the Return), each with the corporation's name and EIN.
5. Run the software's diagnostics and resolve every item.
6. Signature: the authorized officer signs electronically through the software's process (Form 8879-C when an ERO transmits).
7. Submit only after the officer gives explicit consent at that moment.
8. Record the submission ID and keep the acknowledgment. If rejected, read the reject code, correct, and resubmit.

## 4. Paper filing (only when e-file is not required)

### Who signs

The president, vice president, treasurer, assistant treasurer, chief accounting officer, or another officer authorized to sign; a receiver, trustee, or assignee signs instead for a corporation in its charge (Instr., Who Must Sign).

### Assembly order (Instr., Assembling the Return)

Attach schedules and forms after page 6 of Form 1120 in this order: Schedule N, Schedule D (Form 1120), Form 8949, Form 8996, Schedule O, Form 4626, Form 4797, Form 1125-A, Form 4136, Form 8978, Form 965-B, Form 8941, Form 3800, Form 8997, Form 8283, Form 6252, Schedule A (Form 8936), Form 4255, other schedules in alphabetical order, other forms in numerical order, then supporting statements. Do not write "See Attached" in an entry space.

### Where to file (Instructions for Form 1120 (2025), Where To File)

| Principal business, office, or agency in | Total assets at year end | Address |
|------------------------------------------|--------------------------|---------|
| Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Georgia, Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, Tennessee, Vermont, Virginia, West Virginia, Wisconsin | Less than $10 million and Schedule M-3 not filed | Department of the Treasury, Internal Revenue Service, Kansas City, MO 64999-0012 |
| Same states | $10 million or more, or less than $10 million and Schedule M-3 filed | Department of the Treasury, Internal Revenue Service Center, Ogden, UT 84201-0012 |
| Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Florida, Hawaii, Idaho, Iowa, Kansas, Louisiana, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Mexico, North Dakota, Oklahoma, Oregon, South Dakota, Texas, Utah, Washington, Wyoming | Any amount | Department of the Treasury, Internal Revenue Service, Ogden, UT 84201-0012 |
| A foreign country or U.S. territory | Any amount | Internal Revenue Service, P.O. Box 409101, Ogden, UT 84409 |

A group whose members sit in more than one service center area may file at the service center for the area of the managing corporation's principal office. These addresses change; recheck the current instructions before mailing. Private delivery services: use only IRS-designated services and the street addresses at IRS.gov/PDSStreetAddresses; they cannot deliver to a P.O. box.

## 5. Due dates and Form 7004

- Due: the 15th day of the 4th month after the tax year ends; a calendar-year 2025 return was due April 15, 2026. A dissolved corporation files by the 15th day of the 4th month after dissolution. A June 30 year end files by the 15th day of the 3rd month under the 2025 instructions. Weekend or holiday: next business day (Instr., When To File).
- Form 7004, filed by the original due date, gives an automatic 6-month extension; Form 1120 uses form code 12 on Form 7004. C corporations with tax years ending June 30 and beginning before January 1, 2026 get 7 months; for tax years beginning in 2026 the period is 6 months (Instructions for Form 7004, Rev. December 2025). Route extension work to [`../form-7004/SKILL.md`](../form-7004/SKILL.md).
- Form 7004 does not extend the time to pay. Pay the expected balance with the extension.

---

## 6. Paying a balance due

- Payment must be made electronically: EFTPS (EFTPS.gov, 800-555-4477) or the corporation's IRS business tax account (IRS.gov/BusinessAccount); a tax professional, financial institution, or payroll service can deposit on its behalf; a same-day wire through the Federal Tax Collection Service is the fallback (Instr., Electronic Deposit Requirement).
- EFTPS cutoffs: same-day payments of $1 million or less before 3:00 p.m. Eastern on a business day; payments over $1 million by 8:00 p.m. Eastern the day before the due date.
- Online installment agreement (IRS.gov/OPA) if the corporation cannot pay the full line 35 amount, owes $25,000 or less, and can pay in full within 24 months; fees, penalties, and interest still apply (Instr., Line 35).
- Estimated tax for next year: same electronic methods, due the 15th day of the 4th, 6th, 9th, and 12th months.

## 7. After filing

| Event | What the agent does |
|-------|--------------------|
| E-file accepted | Save the acknowledgment with the return copy |
| E-file rejected | Read the reject code, fix, resubmit; do not paper-file a duplicate |
| Refund requested (37b) | Direct deposit only to a U.S. account; refund by check if the name, routing number, or account type is wrong (Instr., Line 37b) |
| Penalty notice | Respond to the notice with a reasonable-cause explanation; do not attach one to the original return (Instr., Interest and Penalties) |
| Error found later | Amend on Form 1120-X; no skill yet, hand off |
| Address or responsible party change | Form 8822-B |

Keep records supporting income and deductions for at least 3 years from the later of the due date or filing date, and basis records for as long as needed (Instr., Recordkeeping).

---

## 8. Consent and security rules for the agent

1. Never submit or mail a return without the authorized officer's explicit consent given at the time of submission.
2. Never invent an officer signature, PIN, or EIN. If the EIN is missing, stop: e-file requires it.
3. Do not store EINs together with bank routing and account numbers, SSNs of officers listed on Form 1125-E, or software credentials in logs or transcripts. Collect bank details only at filing time and discard them afterward.
4. Do not bypass identity verification, multi-factor prompts, or CAPTCHAs in provider software; hand control to the user.
5. If a computed figure in the software disagrees with the draft, stop and surface the difference. Do not override either one silently.
6. Do not file if a preparer or prior session already filed for the same year; a second original return is a duplicate. Check with the user first.
