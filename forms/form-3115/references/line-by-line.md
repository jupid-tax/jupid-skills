# Form 3115 Line-by-Line Reference

Complete lookup for Form 3115 fields, built from the text of **Form 3115 (Rev. December 2022)** and the **Instructions for Form 3115 (Rev. December 2022)**, still the current revisions on 2026-10-06 (https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-3115). The form is not annual; check that page for a newer revision before use, because a new revision can renumber lines.

The instructions still cite Rev. Proc. 2022-14 and Rev. Proc. 2023-1. For a change filed now, use the current List of Automatic Changes (**Rev. Proc. 2025-23**, as modified by Rev. Proc. 2025-28 for research or experimental expenditures) and the current user-fee and address procedure (**Rev. Proc. 2026-1**).

The form has 8 pages: page 1 (identification, Part I, Part II lines 4–5, signature), pages 2–3 (Part II lines 6–19, Part III), page 4 (Part IV and Schedule A), page 5 (Schedules B and C), pages 6–8 (Schedule D, Schedule E).

## Part I — Information for Automatic Change Request

### Line 1a — DCN(s)

Enter the designated automatic accounting method change number from the List of Automatic Changes (Rev. Proc. 2025-23 or a later list). Room for up to 12 DCNs; enter more than one only when the guidance allows concurrent changes on one Form 3115 (Rev. Proc. 2015-13 §6.03(1)(b)).

### Line 1b — Other

If the automatic change has no DCN, check "Other", describe the change and cite the published guidance that provides it (i3115, Line 1b).

### Line 2 — Eligibility rules

"Do any of the eligibility rules restrict the applicant from filing the requested change using the automatic change procedures?" The rules are in Rev. Proc. 2015-13 §5.01(1): under examination with certain limits, §381(a) transaction in the year, final year of the trade or business, an overall method change within the 5 tax years ending with the year of change, a change for the same item within those 5 years. The section of the List of Automatic Changes for the DCN can waive a rule (for example, DCN 7 waives the final-year rule). "Yes" → attach an explanation.

### Line 3 — All information provided

Has the filer provided all information and statements required on the form and by the List of Automatic Changes for the DCN? Each DCN's section lists the Form 3115 lines it requires.

**Note on the form:** complete Part II and Part IV, and Schedules A through E if applicable.

**Signature (bottom of page 1):** "Signature of filer (and spouse, if joint return)", date, name and title; preparer section. For an automatic change, the original Form 3115 attached to the return does not need to be signed; the duplicate copy filed with the IRS must be signed (it may be a photocopy) (i3115, "When and Where To File").

## Part IV — Section 481(a) Adjustment

| Line | Content |
|------|---------|
| 25 | Does published guidance require (or permit, and the applicant elects) a cut-off basis? "Yes" → explain and skip lines 26–29 |
| 26 | The §481(a) adjustment, marked increase (+) or decrease (−) in income. Attach a summary of the computation and methodology, per component if more than one. Overall method changes carry the Schedule A line 2h amount here |
| 27 | Remaining portion of a §481(a) adjustment from a prior change that must be taken into account in the year of change (for example DCNs 234 and 262) |
| 28 | Election to take the entire adjustment into account in the year of change: **$50,000 de minimis election** (positive adjustment under $50,000; Rev. Proc. 2015-13 §7.03(3)(c)) or **eligible acquisition transaction election** (§7.03(3)(d)) |
| 29 | Any part attributable to transactions between related parties? Explain |

**The form has no "spread" line.** The adjustment period comes from Rev. Proc. 2015-13 §7.03: 1 tax year for a negative adjustment; 4 tax years (year of change and next 3) for a positive adjustment, taken ratably; 2 tax years for a positive adjustment when the applicant is under examination, unless a line 7b window category applies (i3115, Line 25). A DCN section can set a different period. If the taxpayer ceases the trade or business (including incorporating it or transferring substantially all its assets), the remaining balance is taken into account in the year of cessation (Rev. Proc. 2015-13 §§3.04, 7.03(4)).

Reporting the yearly portion: Schedule C, positive on line 6, negative in Part V (line 27b) (2025 Instructions for Schedule C, Line 6); Form 1120-S, positive on line 5 (Other income), negative on line 20 (Other deductions), each with a statement (2025 Instructions for Form 1120-S); Form 1120, line 10 (Other income) for a positive amount.

## Schedule A — Change in Overall Method of Accounting (page 4)

Part I (required for DCNs 122, 126, 127, 128, 233, 257, 258 and 259, per i3115):

| Line | Content |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Present and proposed method boxes: Cash / Accrual / Hybrid |
| 2a | Income accrued but not received (accounts receivable) |
| 2b | Income received or reported before it was earned (advance payments); describe and give the legal basis |
| 2c | Expenses accrued but not paid (accounts payable) |
| 2d | Prepaid expenses previously deducted |
| 2e | Supplies on hand previously deducted and/or not previously reported |
| 2f | Inventory on hand previously deducted and/or not previously reported (complete Schedule D, Part II) |
| 2g | Other amounts (describe, with legal basis) |
| 2h | Net §481(a) adjustment (combine 2a–2g, +/−); also enter on Part IV line 26 |
| 3 | Requesting the recurring item exception under §461(h)(3)? |
| 4 | Attach the P&L and balance sheet as of the close of the preceding year (and a reconciliation if 2a–2g differ) |
| 5 | Changing to the overall cash method or to accrual for inventory with cash for everything else? |

Amounts are as of the close of the tax year preceding the year of change; attach a breakdown of lines 2a–2g. Part II (lines 1–2) applies only to non-automatic requests to change to the cash method.

## Schedule B — Advance payments, cost offset methods, AFS income inclusion rule (page 5)

Lines 1–3: attach the information the instructions require for a change to the deferral method for advance payments (Regs. §1.451-8(c) or (d)), to or within a cost offset method (§1.451-3(c), §1.451-8(e)), or to conform to the AFS income inclusion rule (§451(b), §1.451-3).

## Schedule C — Changes Within the LIFO Inventory Method (page 5)

Part I (lines 1–6): present and proposed LIFO submethods (valuing, pooling, pricing dollar-value pools, current-year cost), Form 970 consistency, partial changes, non-cost valuation, IPIC (attach Form 970). Part II (lines 1–4): pooling changes and NBU pools.

Adopting LIFO is **not** a Form 3115 change: it is made on **Form 970** with the return for the first LIFO year (Form 970 instructions, "When To File"; Reg. §1.472-3). Changing **from** LIFO uses Form 3115 (automatic DCN 56, Rev. Proc. 2025-23 §23.01).

## Schedule D — Long-Term Contracts, Inventories, Other §263A Assets (pages 6–8)

- **Part I** (lines 1–4b): long-term contract methods (§460): description, contract types, percentage-of-completion choices.
- **Part II** (lines 1–6): inventories: goods changed (1) and not changed (2); §263A status (3a–3b); line 4a chart of identification methods (specific identification, FIFO, LIFO, other) and valuation methods (cost, lower of cost or market, retail cost, retail LCM, other) for present / proposed / not-changed; line 4b inventory value at the end of the preceding year under present and proposed methods; line 5 (changing from LIFO: Forms 970, statements required by the List of Automatic Changes); line 6 cost offset methods.
- **Part III** (Sections A–C): §263A / §460 cost allocation methods, the 28-line checklist of direct and indirect costs, and other costs not required to be allocated.

Complete for each item or class of property:

| Line | Content |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Property under CLADR (§1.167(a)-11)? |
| 2 | Depreciation required to be capitalized (e.g. §263A)? |
| 3 | Any depreciation, amortization, expense, or disposition election made for the property (e.g. §168(f)(1), §168(i)(4), §179, §179C, §1.168(i)-8(d))? State it. (Elections out of §168(k) bonus depreciation also matter here; see the depreciation example.) |
| 4a | Statement describing the property: description, type, placed-in-service year, use, credits/grants and basis adjustments |
| 4b | Residential rental: did the applicant live in it before renting? |
| 4c | Public utility property? |
| 5 | How the property is treated under the present method |
| 6 | If not currently depreciated: facts supporting the change to depreciate |

The form note says Form 3115 is not filed for certain late elections and election revocations; see i3115 and Rev. Proc. 2025-23 §6.19 (DCN 245) for the limited ones allowed as automatic changes.

## Common errors

1. **DCN not in the current list** — DCNs are reassigned between lists; cite the section of Rev. Proc. 2025-23 (or a later list) on the draft.
2. **Present/proposed method too vague** — "use the right method" is not a description (lines 14a–14d).
3. **§481(a) computation missing** — attach the summary required by line 26.
4. **Wrong adjustment period** — positive 4 years ratably (2 years under exam), negative 1 year, unless a line 28 election or the DCN section says otherwise.
5. **Schedule E detail missing** for depreciation changes — each item or class of property.
6. **Exam or prior-change questions answered without the attachments** (lines 6, 8, 11).
7. **Duplicate copy unsigned** — the copy filed with the IRS must be signed; the original attached to the return need not be.
