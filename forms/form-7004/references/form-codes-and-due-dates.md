# Form 7004: Form Codes, Covered Returns, and Due Dates

Use this file to answer three questions before drafting: (1) is the return covered by Form 7004 at all, (2) which code goes on line 1, and (3) when are Form 7004 and the extended return due.

Sources: Form 7004 (Rev. December 2025), https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f7004.pdf; Instructions for Form 7004 (Rev. December 2025), https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i7004.pdf; IRS "E-filing Form 7004" page and its due date chart, https://www.irs.gov/Efile7004. Re-check all three each filing season at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-7004.

## 2. Returns Form 7004 does NOT cover

If the return is not in the table above, Form 7004 is the wrong form. Redirect:

| Return | Extension form | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Form 1040, 1040-SR, 1040-NR, 1040-SS (including Schedule C, Schedule E, Schedule F income and a disregarded single-member LLC owned by an individual) | Form 4868 | Form 4868 text: extension "to file Form 1040, 1040-SR, 1040-NR, or 1040-SS" |
| Form 709 or 709-NA (gift/GST tax) | Form 4868 also extends Form 709/709-NA; Form 8892 if no income tax extension is requested or to pay gift/GST tax | Form 4868 text; Form 8892 (Rev. December 2024) chart |
| Form 706, 706-A, 706-NA, 706-QDT (estate tax) | Form 4768 | Form 4768 (Rev. February 2020), Part II heading |
| Form 990, 990-EZ, 990-PF, 990-T, 1041-A, 4720, 5227, 6069, 8870 | Form 8868 | Form 8868 (Rev. January 2025) return code list; i7004 Line 1 Note for 1041-A |
| Form 5500 series, Form 8955-SSA | Form 5558 | Form 5558 (Rev. January 2025), Part II heading |
| Forms W-2, W-2G, 1042-S, 1094-C, 1095, 1097, 1098, 1099, 3921, 3922, 5498, 8027 | Form 8809 | Form 8809 (Rev. December 2025) title; it also says to use Form 7004 for Form 1042 |
| DISC returns under §6011(c)(2) | No Form 7004 extension | Treas. Reg. §1.6081-3(d) |
| Employment and excise returns (Forms 940, 941, 943, 944, 945, 720, 2290) | Not on the Form 7004 code list | Form 7004 (Rev. December 2025), Part I |
| State returns | State's own rule | Outside the Internal Revenue Code |

Two boundary facts to state plainly to the user:

- A partnership or S corporation extension does not extend the owners' returns. Treas. Reg. §1.6081-2(e): the partnership extension "does not extend the time for filing a partner's income tax return or the time for the payment of any tax due on a partner's income tax return." Each owner who will wait for a K-1 files Form 4868 for their own Form 1040.
- Form 8868 says: "All corporations required to file an income tax return other than Form 990-T (including 1120-C filers), partnerships, REMICs, and trusts must use Form 7004 to request an extension of time to file income tax returns." An exempt organization's Form 990-T goes on Form 8868.

## 3. Extension length

From i7004, Extension Period:

| Filer | Automatic extension | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Most returns on the code list | 6 months | i7004; Treas. Reg. §1.6081-2(a) (partnerships), §1.6081-3(a) (corporations) |
| Form 1041 estate (other than a bankruptcy estate) or trust (codes 04, 05) | 5½ months | i7004; Treas. Reg. §1.6081-6(a)(1) |
| Form 1041 bankruptcy estate, Form 1041-N, Form 1041-QFT (codes 03, 06, 07) | 6 months | Treas. Reg. §1.6081-6(a)(2); IRS Form 7004 due date chart PY2026 |
| C corporation with a tax year ending June 30 and beginning **before January 1, 2026** | 7 months (6 months if filing Form 1120-POL) | i7004; IRC §6081(b); Treas. Reg. §1.6081-3(e) |
| C corporation with a June 30 year end, tax year beginning in 2026 | 6 months | i7004: "For tax years beginning in 2026, the automatic extension period is 6 months." |
| §1.6081-5 filers with line 4 checked | Additional 3 months (partnerships, S corporations) or 4 months (C corporations, Form 1120-POL) beyond the 15th day of the 6th month | i7004, Line 4 |

A short tax year ending anytime in June is treated as ending June 30 (i7004 Note; Treas. Reg. §1.6081-3(e)).

## 4. Original due dates (= Form 7004 deadline)

Form 7004 is due by the return's original due date (i7004, When To File). Main returns:

| Return | Original due date | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Form 1065 | 15th day of the 3rd month after the tax year ends | IRC §6072(b); 2025 Instructions for Form 1065, When To File |
| Form 1120-S | 15th day of the 3rd month after the tax year ends | IRC §6072(b); 2025 Instructions for Form 1120-S |
| Form 1120 | 15th day of the 4th month after the tax year ends | IRC §6072(a); 2025 Instructions for Form 1120 |
| Form 1120, June 30 year end, tax year beginning before 2026 | 15th day of the 3rd month | 2025 Instructions for Form 1120; P.L. 114-41 §2006(a)(3)(B) |
| Form 1120, dissolved corporation | 15th day of the 4th month after the date it dissolved | 2025 Instructions for Form 1120 |
| Form 1041 | 15th day of the 4th month after the tax year ends | 2025 Instructions for Form 1041 |
| Foreign corporation with no U.S. office (Form 1120-F, line 2 checked) | 15th day of the 6th month | IRC §6072(c); i7004 Line 2 |
| §1.6081-5 filers (line 4) | Automatic to the 15th day of the 6th month; Form 7004 with line 4 by then | Treas. Reg. §1.6081-5; i7004 Line 4 |
| Form 1120-C meeting IRC §6072(d) | 15th day of the 9th month | IRC §6072(d); IRS due date chart PY2026 |
| Form 1042, Form 706-GS(T) | Calendar year only: March 15 (1042), April 15 (706-GS(T)) | IRS due date chart PY2026 |
| Any other code | See that return's instructions | i7004, When To File |

### Weekend and holiday rule

If a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, a filing on the next day that is not a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday is timely (IRC §7503). The 2025 Instructions for Form 1065 apply this to "a legal holiday in the District of Columbia or the state in which you file your return." DC legal holidays include DC Emancipation Day (April 16, observed on the nearest weekday).

Compute the extended due date from the statutory date (the 15th), not from a date that rolled forward: the regulations grant the extension "after the date prescribed for filing the return" (Treas. Reg. §1.6081-2(a), §1.6081-3(a)). Then apply §7503 to the extended date.

### Calendar-year dates, computed

Computed with the rules above (python, weekends plus DC legal holidays). Re-check against the IRS due date chart at https://www.irs.gov/Efile7004 before relying on them.

| Return | Tax year 2025: Form 7004 due | TY2025 extended return due | Tax year 2026: Form 7004 due | TY2026 extended return due |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1065, 1120-S (3rd month) | Mon March 16, 2026 (March 15 is a Sunday) | Tue Sept 15, 2026 | Mon March 15, 2027 | Wed Sept 15, 2027 |
| 1120 (4th month) | Wed April 15, 2026 | Thu Oct 15, 2026 | Thu April 15, 2027 | Fri Oct 15, 2027 |
| 1041 estate/trust (5½ months) | Wed April 15, 2026 | Wed Sept 30, 2026 | Thu April 15, 2027 | Thu Sept 30, 2027 |
| 1041 bankruptcy estate, 1041-N, 1041-QFT | Wed April 15, 2026 | Thu Oct 15, 2026 | Thu April 15, 2027 | Fri Oct 15, 2027 |

The 2025 Instructions for Form 1120-S and Form 1065 both state the March 16, 2026 date for calendar-year 2025 returns.

### Fiscal-year dates, worked

| Case | Original due | Extended due |
|---|---|---|
| Partnership, year ending Sept 30, 2026 | Tue Dec 15, 2026 | Tue June 15, 2027 |
| C corporation, year ending March 31, 2026 | Wed July 15, 2026 | Fri Jan 15, 2027 |
| C corporation, year July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026 (7-month rule) | Tue Sept 15, 2026 | Thu April 15, 2027 |
| C corporation, year July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 (6 months) | Fri Oct 15, 2027 | April 15, 2028 is a Saturday and Monday April 17, 2028 is DC Emancipation Day observed: Tue April 18, 2028 |
| C corporation, year ending Aug 31, 2026 | Tue Dec 15, 2026 | Tue June 15, 2027 |
| C corporation dissolved July 22, 2026 (short final year) | Nov 15, 2026 is a Sunday: Mon Nov 16, 2026 | May 15, 2027 is a Saturday: Mon May 17, 2027 |

Known inconsistency: the IRS "Form 7004 Due Date Chart PY2026" (dated 11/03/2025) lists 10/15/2026 for a Form 1120 tax period ending June 2026, while its own footnote and the 2025 Instructions for Form 1120 put that return at the 15th day of the 3rd month (Sept 15, 2026). Follow the instructions and the statute; tell the user about the conflict if their year ends in June.

## 5. Missed the original due date?

Form 7004 filed after the original due date is late and does not extend anything (i7004: the extension is granted only if Form 7004 is filed "by the due date of the return for which the extension is requested"). The IRS e-file page says Form 7004 cannot be e-filed for "Reasonable cause for failing to file application timely" (https://www.irs.gov/Efile7004). Tell the user to file the return as soon as possible and, if a penalty notice arrives, respond with a reasonable-cause explanation (i7004, Reasonable cause). Do not attach an explanation to the return.
