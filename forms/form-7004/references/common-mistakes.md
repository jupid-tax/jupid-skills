# Common Form 7004 Mistakes

Each entry: the mistake, what it costs, the fix, and the authority. Sources are the Form 7004 (Rev. December 2025) PDF, its Instructions (Rev. December 2025), the IRS e-file page for Form 7004 (https://www.irs.gov/Efile7004), and the regulations cited.

---

## 1. Treating the extension as time to pay

**Mistake.** A C corporation files Form 7004, enters a tentative tax on line 6, and pays nothing until the extended return.

**Cost.** Failure-to-pay penalty of 1/2 of 1% per month or part of a month on unpaid tax, up to 25% (i7004, Payment of Tax), plus interest from the original due date (i7004, Interest). For corporations, the extension itself depends on remitting the properly estimated unpaid tax by the due date (Treas. Reg. §1.6081-3(a)(3)).

**Fix.** Pay line 8 by the original due date through EFTPS or Electronic Funds Withdrawal with an e-filed Form 7004. For corporations, make line 6 (or the tax paid by the regular due date) at least 90% of the final total tax and pay the rest by the extended due date (i7004 90% rule).

---

## 2. Wrong form code on line 1

**Mistake.** An S corporation enters 12 (Form 1120) instead of 25 (Form 1120-S). A multi-member LLC enters 12 instead of 09. An estate enters 05 (trust) instead of 04.

**Cost.** The extension applies "only to the specific return identified on Part I, line 1" (i7004, No Blanket Requests). The return the entity actually files is unextended.

**Fix.** Confirm the return the entity files, then read the code from the form's own table (see [form-codes-and-due-dates.md](./form-codes-and-due-dates.md)). For a homeowners association electing Form 1120-H, ask which form type the IRS assigned; i7004 says to use "the original form type assigned to the entity."

---

## 3. Using the new name after a name change

**Mistake.** The LLC renamed itself this year and enters the new name on Form 7004.

**Cost.** "If the name entered on Form 7004 does not match the IRS database and/or the identifying number is incorrect, you will not have a valid extension" (i7004).

**Fix.** Enter the name exactly as on the previous year's return (i7004). Report the name change through the channel the return's instructions give. The IRS cannot accept "Name change applications" on an e-filed Form 7004 (https://www.irs.gov/Efile7004).

---

## 4. Filing Form 7004 against the extended date instead of the original date

**Mistake.** The user thinks Form 7004 for a calendar-year 2026 Form 1065 can be filed any time before September 15, 2027.

**Cost.** Form 7004 must be filed "by the due date of the return for which the extension is requested" (i7004). Filed after March 15, 2027, it extends nothing. The partnership owes $260 per partner per month or part of a month for returns required to be filed in 2027 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32 §4.55; IRC §6698).

**Fix.** Compute the original due date first (see [form-codes-and-due-dates.md](./form-codes-and-due-dates.md)) and treat it as the hard deadline.

---

## 5. One Form 7004 for several returns

**Mistake.** A partnership expects one Form 7004 to cover both Form 1065 and Form 8804; a group expects one form to cover members that do not join the consolidated return.

**Cost.** The uncovered return is unextended.

**Fix.** "File a separate Form 7004 for each return" (i7004). A member of a controlled or affiliated group not joining in the consolidated return files its own Form 7004 (i7004, Line 3 Caution).

---

## 6. Assuming the owners' returns are extended too

**Mistake.** Partners or S corporation shareholders skip Form 4868 because the business filed Form 7004.

**Cost.** Late filing and late payment on each owner's Form 1040. The partnership extension "does not extend the time for filing a partner's income tax return or the time for the payment of any tax due on a partner's income tax return" (Treas. Reg. §1.6081-2(e)).

**Fix.** Tell every owner who will wait for a K-1 to file Form 4868 for their own return (see [`form-1040`](../../form-1040/SKILL.md)).

---

## 7. Paper Form 7004, e-filed return

**Mistake.** The entity mails Form 7004 and later e-files the return.

**Cost.** "If you file Form 7004 on paper and file your tax return electronically, your return may be processed before the extension is granted. This may result in a penalty notice" (i7004).

**Fix.** E-file Form 7004 when the return will be e-filed. If paper is unavoidable, keep proof of mailing and be ready to answer the notice.

---

## 8. Mailing to the wrong service center

**Mistake.** A Form 1065 filer in Georgia mails to Ogden; a Form 1120 filer in New York with $14 million of assets mails to Kansas City; a foreign-owned disregarded entity mails to the regular Form 7004 address.

**Cost.** Misrouted paper and a possible dispute about timely filing.

**Fix.** Use the i7004 Where To File table (reproduced in [filing.md](../filing.md)). Georgia is in the Kansas City group. For Forms 1065, 1120, 1120-REIT, 1120-RIC, 1120-S, 8612, and 8613 in the Kansas City states, total assets at the end of the tax year of $10 million or more go to Ogden. Foreign-owned U.S. DEs fax to 855-887-7737 or mail to the PIN Unit in Ogden (Instructions for Form 5472).

---

## 9. Expecting an approval letter

**Mistake.** The user waits for confirmation before relying on the extension.

**Cost.** None directly, but the user has no proof of filing if they did not keep one.

**Fix.** "We will notify you only if your request for an extension is disallowed" (i7004). Keep the e-file acknowledgment or the certified-mail receipt.

---

## 10. Giving Form 1041 six months

**Mistake.** A calendar-year trust calendars October 15, 2027 for its 2026 Form 1041.

**Cost.** The return is late after September 30, 2027.

**Fix.** Estates (other than bankruptcy estates) and trusts get 5½ months (i7004; Treas. Reg. §1.6081-6(a)(1)). Bankruptcy estates and Forms 1041-N and 1041-QFT get 6 months (Treas. Reg. §1.6081-6(a)(2)).

---

## 11. Misapplying the June 30 seven-month rule

**Mistake.** A C corporation with a June 30 year end applies 7 months to its year that began July 1, 2026, or applies the 3rd-month due date to it.

**Cost.** A late return.

**Fix.** 7 months applies only to C corporation years ending June 30 that begin before January 1, 2026 (i7004; IRC §6081(b); Treas. Reg. §1.6081-3(e)). Those years are due the 15th day of the 3rd month (2025 Instructions for Form 1120). Years beginning in 2026 get 6 months; their return is due the 15th day of the 4th month (P.L. 114-41 §2006(a)(3)(B)).

---

## 12. Using Form 7004 for a return it does not cover

**Mistake.** Form 7004 for a Form 1040 with Schedule C, a Form 990, a Form 5500, or a payroll return.

**Cost.** No extension for the actual return.

**Fix.** Form 4868 for individual returns, Form 8868 for exempt organization returns and Form 1041-A, Form 5558 for Form 5500, Form 8809 for information returns (see [form-codes-and-due-dates.md](./form-codes-and-due-dates.md), section 2).

---

## 13. Checking line 4 without qualifying, or skipping the statement

**Mistake.** A domestic partnership with U.S. books checks line 4 to get extra months; or a qualifying entity relies on the automatic §1.6081-5 extension without attaching the statement to the return.

**Cost.** The additional extension does not exist for non-qualifiers; a qualifier without the statement invites a late-filing notice.

**Fix.** Line 4 is only for the four categories in Treas. Reg. §1.6081-5(a)(1) to (4) as listed in i7004. Qualifying entities "attach a statement to the entity's tax return stating that the entity qualifies for the extension to file and pay" (i7004).

---

## 14. Change in accounting period without approval

**Mistake.** Line 5b "Change in accounting period" checked with no Form 1128 filed.

**Cost.** i7004: the entity "must have applied for approval to change its tax year unless certain conditions have been met." The IRS will not take the request electronically without that (https://www.irs.gov/Efile7004).

**Fix.** Ask whether Form 1128 was filed or which automatic-change condition applies; if neither, stop and refer the user to their preparer.

---

## 15. Missing the consolidated group member list

**Mistake.** Line 3 checked, no member statement attached.

**Cost.** "Failure to list members of the affiliated group on an attachment may result in the group's inability to elect to file a consolidated return" (i7004).

**Fix.** Attach the list (name, address, EIN of each member) in the paper format i7004 prescribes, or include it in the e-file submission.
