## Full-pay screen

Equity without allowances: 1,486 + 2,214 + 4,720 + 0 = $8,420. Box F × months = $0. Capacity $8,420 against a $23,867 balance: cannot full pay.

CSED check: the 2019 period has 49 months left and the 2020 period 60; both exceed 24, so IRM 5.8.5.25 does not shorten the multiplier (which is moot at Box F = 0).

## Payment option

Lump sum would require paying the remaining $3,970 within 5 months of acceptance. Her daughter, Dana Whitfield-Ross, can contribute $180 a month, not a lump sum. The agent recommends asking about a periodic offer; Gloria chooses periodic over 22 months.

Schedule: $180 first payment, then $180 on the 15th of each month for 20 months, then a final $190. Check: 180 + 20 × 180 + 190 = $3,970; 1 + 20 + 1 = 22 months (limit 24).

## Low-Income Certification

- AGI from her most recently filed return (2025): $18,740 ≤ $39,900 (family of 1, 48 states/DC/territories). Qualifies.
- Second test for the record: 2,305 × 12 = $27,660 ≤ $39,900.

Effect: no $205 fee, no first payment with the offer, no monthly payments while the offer is considered; first payment due 30 calendar days after acceptance (Form 656-B, page 3).
