# Form 433-A (OIC) and Form 433-B (OIC) (Rev. 4-2026) — Line Maps

Every numbered line and box of the two offer-in-compromise financial statements, built from the PDF text of the Form 656-B booklet (Rev. 4-2026), https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f656b.pdf. These forms are used only with Form 656. The allowance amounts ($1,000; $3,450; $11,980) and the multipliers (× .8, × 12, × 24) are printed on the forms and change between revisions; re-check them in the current booklet.

General rules printed throughout both forms:

- Round to the nearest whole dollar.
- Do not enter a negative number; if a line computes negative, enter 0.
- Lines with a letter (1a, 6b, 9c …) feed the numbered line; add only numbered lines into the boxes.
- Include assets located in foreign countries; attach lists when space runs out.
- Every value comes from the user and a current statement. Ask; never estimate.

---

## Form 433-A (OIC) — Collection Information Statement for Wage Earners and Self-Employed Individuals

Who uses it (page 1): an individual who owes Form 1040 income tax; an individual with personal liability for excise tax; an individual responsible for a Trust Fund Recovery Penalty; a self-employed individual; an individual personally responsible for a partnership liability (only if the partnership is submitting an offer); an individual submitting an offer for the estate of a deceased person.

### Section 1 — Personal and Household Information

Last name, first name, date of birth, SSN or ITIN; marital status (unmarried/married) and date of marriage; home physical address; own, rent, or other; checkbox if married and lived in AZ, CA, ID, LA, NM, NV, TX, WA or WI within the last ten years (community property states); county of residence; phones; mailing address; spouse's name, date of birth, SSN; every other household member or dependent with age, relationship, whether claimed as a dependent on Form 1040, and whether they contribute to household income.

Ask: county (drives the housing standard), household size, and anyone 65 or older (drives the health care standard).

### Section 2 — Employment Information for Wage Earners

Employer name and address, pay period (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, other), ownership interest in the employer (if yes, also file Form 433-B (OIC)), occupation, time with employer; same for spouse. If either spouse has Schedule C, E, F income, also complete Sections 4, 5, 6.

### Section 3 — Personal Asset Information (Domestic and Foreign)

Notes:

- The $11,980 on line (7) equals the 2026 levy exemption for fuel, provisions, furniture, and household personal effects under IRC §6334(a)(2) (Rev. Proc. 2025-32, §3.49). The form subtracts it once from the total of (7a) through (7c), not only from art and jewelry.
- The $1,000 and $3,450 allowances apply only to individuals and only after the IRS determines the taxpayer cannot full pay from equity, an installment agreement, or both (Form 656-B, page 1; IRM 5.8.5.7; IRM 5.8.5.12). Use them for the offer; drop them for the full-pay screen.
- IRM 5.8.5.12: the $3,450 exclusion is for vehicles used for work, the production of income, or the welfare of the family; one vehicle for a single taxpayer, two for joint taxpayers.
- IRM 5.8.5.4.1: × .8 is the normal quick sale value. The IRS may use a different percentage, or full value when an asset is being sold to fund the offer (then actual sale price less costs of sale and expected tax).

"NOTE: If you or your spouse are self-employed, Sections 4, 5, and 6 must be completed before continuing with Sections 7 and 8."

### Section 4 — Self-Employed Information

Sole proprietorship yes/no; business address; name; phone; EIN; website; trade name or DBA; description; number of employees; frequency of tax deposits; average gross monthly payroll; other business interests (LLC, LLP, corporation, partnership) with ownership percentage, title, business name, phone, EIN, type. An interest in an entity also requires Form 433-B (OIC) for that entity.

### Section 5 — Business Asset Information (for Self-Employed)

Do not repeat personal assets from Section 3.

| Line | Description | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| (8a), (8b) | Business cash and bank accounts | Balance (no $1,000 allowance) |
| (8c) | Business digital assets | U.S. dollar value today |
| (8d) | Business bank accounts from attachment | Balance |
| **(8)** | Business cash | (8a) through (8d) |
| (9a), (9b) | Other business assets (tools, books, machinery, equipment, business vehicles, real property owned, leased, or rented) | Market value × .8 − loan balance; **enter 0 if leased or used in the production of income** |
| (9c) | Assets from attachment | Market value × .8 − loan balance |
| **(9)** | Other business assets | (9a) through (9c) |
| **(10)** | "IRS allowed deduction for professional books and tools of trade for individuals and sole-proprietors" | **No amount printed on the form.** See below |
| **(11)** | Net business assets | (9) − (10), not below 0 |
| — | Notes receivable yes/no (attach list); accounts receivable including e-payment, factoring, bartering, online auction accounts yes/no (attach list with age and amount) | Attach lists; no line value |
| **Box B** | Available Business Equity in Assets | (8) + (11) |

**Line (10) handling.** The form names a deduction but prints no figure. IRM 5.8.5.16 says a statutory levy exemption applies to an individual's tools used in a trade or business, that it is updated annually, and that it is not available to LLC, partnership, or corporation property. The 2026 amount under IRC §6334(a)(3) (books and tools necessary for the taxpayer's trade, business, or profession) is $5,990 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32, §3.49). This figure is derived, not printed:

1. If line (9) is 0, line (10) has no effect; note that and move on.
2. If line (9) is above 0, tell the user the line (10) figure is derived from Rev. Proc. 2025-32 and not printed on the form, show Box B and the offer amount both with $5,990 and with $0 on line (10), and let the user decide (ask them to confirm with the IRS Pre-Qualifier, the Individual Online Account offer calculator, or a representative). Record the choice in the validation summary.
3. Never apply it to an entity's assets.

**Income-producing assets.** The form enters 0 for assets used in the production of income. IRM 5.8.5.15 says equity in income-producing assets is generally not added to the RCP of a viable ongoing business unless the assets are not critical to operations, and that equity in real property is included. Record the market value and loan balance of each zeroed asset anyway, and tell the user the IRS can add the equity if it decides the asset is not critical.

The most recent Schedule C may be used unless income or expenses changed significantly in the past year. Income and expenses may be averaged over 6 to 12 months. Record the period ("Period provided beginning ___ through ___").

### Section 8 — Calculate Your Minimum Offer Amount

| Box | Formula |
|---|---|
| Box G | Box F × 12 — offer paid in 5 or fewer payments within 5 months or less |
| Box H | Box F × 24 — offer paid in 6 to 24 months |
| Offer Amount | Box A + Box B (if applicable) + Box G or Box H; more than $0; whole dollars |

Form note: the multipliers and the vehicle and bank allowances do not apply if the IRS determines the taxpayer can pay in full within the collection period. If special circumstances prevent paying this amount, the taxpayer enters what they can pay on Form 656 Section 4 and explains in Section 3.

### Section 9 — Other Information

Incarceration (inmate number); bankruptcy proceeding (not eligible if open); litigation (plaintiff/defendant, location, representation, docket, amount, completion date, subject); bankruptcy in the past 7 years (dates, petition number, location); lived outside the U.S. 6 months or longer in the past 10 years (dates); litigation involving the IRS/United States (tax types and periods); beneficiary of a trust, estate, or life insurance policy (place recorded, EIN, name, anticipated amount, when); trustee, fiduciary, or contributor of a trust; safe deposit box (location, contents, value); assets with fair market value over $10,000 transferred for less than full value in the past 10 years (asset, value, date, recipient); assets or real property outside the U.S.; funds held in trust by a third party.

Every "Yes" needs detail. A transfer for less than full value can be added to the RCP as a dissipated asset (IRM 5.8.5.18).

### Section 10 — Signatures

Taxpayer and spouse sign and date under penalties of perjury. Required attachments listed on the form: most recent pay stubs; most recent investment and retirement statements; digital asset records; statements for pensions, Social Security, rental, interest, dividends, child support or alimony orders, and other income; three most recent complete bank statements (six for each business account if operating a business); Form 433-B (OIC) if either spouse has a non-sole-proprietor business interest; lender statements showing payments, payoffs, balances; receivables lists; delinquent state/local tax verification; court orders; trust documents; documentation of special circumstances; Form 2848 if represented (with all years and the current tax year); the signed current Form 656.

## Form 433-B (OIC) — Collection Information Statement for Businesses

For a corporation, an LLC classified as a corporation, a partnership, or another LLC. A sole proprietorship uses Form 433-A (OIC) instead.

### Section 1 — Business Information

Business name, EIN, physical address, county, description and DBA, phones, mailing address, website, fax, whether payroll and return preparation are outsourced (provider name and address), federal contractor yes/no, number of employees (or "only employee" checkbox), frequency of tax deposits, average gross monthly payroll; every partner, officer, LLC member, and major shareholder with title, ownership percentage and annual salary, SSN, home address, phones.

### Section 3 — Business Income Information (average of most recent 6 to 12 months)

(6) gross receipts; (7) gross rental income; (8) interest income; (9) dividends; (10) other income. **Box B** Total Business Income = (6) through (10). With a current P&L, enter the total in Box B and skip (6) to (10).

### Section 4 — Business Expense Information (average of most recent 6 to 12 months)

(11) materials purchased; (12) inventory purchased; (13) gross wages and salaries; (14) rent; (15) supplies; (16) utilities/telephones; (17) vehicle costs; (18) insurance other than life; (19) current taxes; (20) other expenses such as secured debt payments (no credit card payments). **Box C** Total Business Expenses = (11) through (20). **Box D** Remaining Monthly Income = Box B − Box C, not below 0. Depreciation and other non-cash expenses are not allowed.

### Section 5 — Calculate Your Minimum Offer Amount

| Box | Formula |
|---|---|
| Box E | Box D × 12 — offer paid in 5 or fewer payments within 5 months or less |
| Box F | Box D × 24 — offer paid in 6 to 24 months |
| Offer Amount | Box A + Box E or Box F; more than $0; whole dollars |

Footnote on the form: "You may exclude any equity in income producing assets (except real estate) shown in Section 2 of this form."

### Section 6 — Other Information

Current bankruptcy (not eligible if yes); bankruptcy in the past 10 years; affiliations (subsidiary or parent, EINs); related parties owing money to the business; litigation; litigation involving the IRS/United States; assets over $10,000 transferred for less than full value in the past 10 years; real property transferred in the past 3 years; located outside the U.S. 6 months or longer in the past 10 years; assets outside the U.S.; funds held in trust by a third party; lines of credit (limit, amount owed, collateral).

### Section 7 — Signatures

Signed by an authorized officer with title, under penalties of perjury. Attachments: current P&L covering at least the most recent 6 to 12 months if appropriate; six most recent statements for each business bank account and three for each investment account; lender statements; receivables statements; digital asset records; special-circumstances documentation; Form 2848 if represented; signed Form 656.
