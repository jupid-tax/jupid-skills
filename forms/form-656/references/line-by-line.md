# Form 656 (Rev. 4-2026) — Section-by-Section Map

Every field of Form 656, Offer in Compromise, as printed in the Form 656-B booklet (Rev. 4-2026), pages 1 to 7 of Form 656. Built from the PDF text of https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f656b.pdf. The financial statements (Form 433-A (OIC), Form 433-B (OIC)) are mapped in `collection-information-statements.md`. Re-check the revision date at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-656 before use; the Low-Income Certification table changes with each revision.

Form 656 identifies who owes, which periods, why the IRS should accept, how much, and how it will be paid. Most of pages 4 to 6 are terms the taxpayer agrees to; the taxpayer fills in Sections 1 (or 2), 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, and 9 if a paid preparer was used.

## Section 1 — Individual Information (Form 1040 filers)

Use Section 1 if the taxpayer is a Form 1040 filer, an individual with personal liability for excise tax, an individual responsible for a Trust Fund Recovery Penalty (TFRP), a self-employed individual, or an individual personally responsible for partnership liabilities.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Your first name, middle initial, last name | As on the tax return |
| SSN or ITIN | Nine digits. A deactivated ITIN cannot be used (Form 656-B, page 1) |
| If a joint offer, spouse's name | Joint offers only |
| Spouse's SSN or ITIN | Joint offers only |
| Your home physical address (street, city, state, ZIP, county of residence) | Include the county; it ties to the housing and utilities standard |
| Your home mailing address | Only if a P.O. box or different from physical |
| Is this a new address since your last filed tax return | Yes / No |
| If yes, would you like us to update our records to this address | Yes / No |
| Your Employer Identification Number (if applicable) | Sole proprietors with an EIN |

### Individual Tax Periods (list all years/periods owed)

| Checkbox line | Format printed on the form |
|---|---|
| Form 1040 U.S. Individual Income Tax Return | e.g., 12-31-2023 |
| Trust Fund Recovery Penalty as a responsible person of (business name) … for the quarterly period(s) ending | e.g., 03-31-2023 |
| Form 941 Employer's Quarterly Federal Tax Return | e.g., 3-31-2023 |
| Form 940 Employer's Annual Federal Unemployment (FUTA) Tax Return - Year(s) | e.g., 12-31-2023 |
| Other Federal Tax(es) | Specify type(s) and period(s) |

If more space is needed: an attachment titled "Attachment to Form 656 dated ____", signed and dated.

**Warning printed on the form:** the IRS will not compromise restitution assessed by the IRS, periods referred to the Department of Justice, or liabilities for which an IRC §965(i) election was made; §965(h)(1) deferred liabilities are processed only if acceleration under §965(h)(3) has occurred and no part arose from a §965(h)(3) transfer agreement.

### Low-Income Certification (Individuals and Sole Proprietors Only) — page 2

The taxpayer qualifies if adjusted gross income from the most recently filed Form 1040, **or** household gross monthly income from Form 433-A (OIC) × 12, is equal to or less than the amount in the table for family size and location. Check one of two boxes:

- "… because my adjusted gross income for my household's size is equal to or less than the amount shown in the table below."
- "… because my household's size and gross monthly income x 12 is equal to or less than the income shown in the table below."

| Size of family unit | 48 contiguous states, D.C., and U.S. Territories | Alaska | Hawaii |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $39,900 | $49,875 | $45,900 |
| 2 | $54,100 | $67,625 | $62,225 |
| 3 | $68,300 | $85,375 | $78,550 |
| 4 | $82,500 | $103,125 | $94,875 |
| 5 | $96,700 | $120,875 | $111,200 |
| 6 | $110,900 | $138,625 | $127,525 |
| 7 | $125,100 | $156,375 | $143,850 |
| 8 | $139,300 | $174,125 | $160,175 |
| Each additional person, add | $14,200 | $17,750 | $16,325 |

Rules printed with the table:

- A business other than a sole proprietorship, or an offer filed for a deceased individual, does not qualify.
- The IRS verifies the certification.
- "IF YOU QUALIFY FOR THE LOW-INCOME CERTIFICATION DO NOT INCLUDE ANY PAYMENTS WITH YOUR OFFER." Payments sent anyway are generally not returned and are applied to the liability.
- Statutory basis: IRC §7122(c)(3) waives the partial payments and the user fee for individuals with AGI not above 250% of the poverty level.

The table is year-dependent. Use only the table printed in the current Form 656.

## Section 2 — Business Information (Form 1120, 1065, etc., filers)

For a corporation, partnership, LLC, or LLP compromising its own debts. Requires Form 433-B (OIC). The $205 fee and initial payment are always due with a business offer (no low-income waiver).

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Business name | Legal name |
| Business physical address | Street, city, state, ZIP |
| Business mailing address | If different |
| Is this a new address since your last filed tax return / update records | Yes / No each |
| Employer Identification Number (EIN) | Nine digits |
| Name and title of primary contact; telephone number | Officer, partner, or member |

### Business Tax Periods (list all years/periods owed)

| Checkbox line | Format |
|---|---|
| Form 1120 U.S. Corporate Income Tax Return | e.g., 12-31-2023 |
| Form 941 | e.g., 03-31-2023 |
| Form 940 | e.g., 12-31-2023 |
| Other Federal Tax(es) | Type(s) and period(s) |

Attachment rule is the same as Section 1. A partner's share of a partnership debt cannot be compromised by the individual; the partnership submits its own offer (Form 656-B, page 4).

## Section 3 — Reason for Offer (page 3)

"Select only ONE of the three check boxes."

| Box | Meaning | Required attachment |
|---|---|---|
| Doubt as to Collectibility | "I do not have enough in assets and income to pay my full tax liability. I have offered the minimum offer amount calculated on Form 433-A(OIC) and/or Form 433-B(OIC)." | If special circumstances prevent paying the calculated minimum due to economic hardship, a detailed explanation |
| Effective Tax Administration — Economic Hardship | Liability owed and could be paid in full, but full payment would cause economic hardship. Individuals only | Detailed explanation |
| Effective Tax Administration — Public Policy or Equity | Liability owed and could be paid in full, but exceptional circumstances make collection inequitable (example on the form: a payroll service provider misappropriated withheld taxes) | Detailed explanation |

Doubt as to liability is not on this form; it uses Form 656-L.

## Section 4 — Payment Terms (page 3)

"Check only one of the payment options." The offer must be more than $0, in whole dollars.

### Lump Sum

Checkbox: "Check here if you will pay your offer in 5 or fewer payments within 5 or fewer months from the date of acceptance."

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Total offer amount | Whole dollars; at least the Form 433-A (OIC) or 433-B (OIC) minimum unless special circumstances are explained in Section 3 |
| 20% initial payment | 20% of the offer (waived with Low-Income Certification). The form does not say how to round; this skill rounds up to the next whole dollar so the payment is never below 20%, and tells the user it did so |
| Remaining balance | Total minus initial |
| Up to 5 rows: "Amount of payment $ ___ payable within ___ Month(s) after acceptance" | Payments must total the remaining balance; none later than 5 months |

Statutory basis: IRC §7122(c)(1)(A) (20% with a lump-sum offer; lump sum = 5 or fewer installments).

### Periodic Payment

Checkbox: "Check here if you will pay your offer in full in 6 to 24 months."

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Enter the amount of your offer | Whole dollars |
| "The first monthly payment of $ ___ is included with this offer" | Waived with Low-Income Certification |
| "then $ ___ will be paid on the (pick number 1-28) ___ day of each month thereafter for ___ months" | Day must be 1 to 28 |
| "with a final payment of $ ___ to be paid on the ___ day of the ___ month" | |

The form's note: total months may not exceed 24; on a 24-month schedule the first payment is month 1, the last is month 24, and there are 22 payments between them. Payments must continue while the IRS considers the offer, or the offer is returned with no appeal rights (IRC §7122(c)(1)(B)(ii) allows the IRS to treat a missed installment as a withdrawal). With Low-Income Certification, the first payment is due 30 calendar days after acceptance unless an amended offer sets another date.

## Section 7 — Offer Terms (pages 4 to 6)

No entries. The taxpayer agrees to all of these by signing. Summarize them to the user before signature.

| Term | Substance |
|---|---|
| (a) | Offer covers the listed debts under Title 26 as of the form date; IRS may add assessed periods the taxpayer failed to list; joint offer authorizes disclosure of separate liabilities |
| (b) | IRS may remove periods with no balance. Offer is accepted by law unless the IRS notifies otherwise in writing within 24 months of receipt by the Memphis/Brookhaven centralized unit (the period starts when the correct site receives it; periods in judicial dispute are excluded). See IRC §7122(f) |
| (c) | Payments are voluntary and not returned even if the offer is withdrawn, rejected, or returned; the application fee is kept unless the offer is not accepted for processing |
| (d) | With Low-Income Certification, no payments until acceptance; voluntary payments are applied and not returned |
| (e) | No amended returns for the listed years after submission or acceptance; refunds for periods ending before acceptance are offset; a refund received before acceptance must be returned within 30 days |
| (f) | Offer amount cannot include an expected or current refund, money already paid, funds attached by collection, or anticipated capital or net operating loss benefits |
| (g) | IRS keeps money collected before the offer; it may levy until an IRS official signs the offer as pending (IRC §6331(k)); a continuous wage levy may be kept or released |
| (h) | Payments and later periodic payments are treated as payments |
| (i) | Overpayment of the final amount is applied to the debt, not returned |
| (j) | Pending starts on the IRS official's signature date; also pending 30 days after rejection and during Appeals; withdrawal rules; one spouse withdrawing withdraws a joint offer |
| (k) | Appeal right is waived if no written request within 30 days of a rejection notice |
| (l) | Five-year compliance: file and pay timely through the fifth year after acceptance; pay later-assessed liabilities for pre-acceptance years; no installment agreement and no new offer during the five years; non-compliance by one joint spouse defaults only that spouse; restitution non-payment can default |
| (m) | Full liability, penalties, and interest remain until all terms are met; bankruptcy before completion lets the IRS claim the full amount |
| (n) | After acceptance, no court challenge or refund claim for listed periods |
| (o) | Default: IRS may revoke the lien release, file a new lien, levy or sue for amounts up to the original debt less payments plus accruals; false information lets the IRS reopen or terminate |
| (p) | Waiver extending the assessment period by the pending time plus one year if rejected, returned, terminated, or withdrawn; collection period suspended while pending, 30 days after rejection, and during Appeals; submitting withdraws any pending installment agreement |
| (q) | IRS may file a Notice of Federal Tax Lien; liens generally released within 45 days after final payment is received and verified |
| (r) | IRS may correct typographical or clerical errors |
| (s) | Third-party contacts without notice (IRC §7602(c)); credit report authorized |
| (t) | Individual offer on a joint liability does not release the co-obligor |
| (u) | Shared responsibility payment: no penalties, lien, or levy; interest accrues; refunds may be offset |
| (v) | Taxpayer name, city/state/ZIP, liability amount, and offer terms are open to public inspection for one year after acceptance |
| (w) | Accepting final determination letters through IRS Secure Messaging |
| (x) | A joint offer stays joint unless both spouses submit amended Forms 656 |

## Section 9 — Paid Preparer Use Only (page 7)

Signature, phone, date, voicemail checkbox, name, CAF number or PTIN, firm name, address, ZIP. Voluntary for the preparer per the Privacy Act statement, but complete it if a paid preparer prepared the form.

Representation: attach a signed Form 2848 (representation) or Form 8821 (information only; cannot represent in a collection matter), listing all years and forms in the offer and the current tax year.

## IRS Use Only

Signature of the authorized IRS official accepting the waiver of the assessment period described in Section 7(p). Leave blank.
