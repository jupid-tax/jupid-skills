# Nonresident Members — FTB 3832 Consent, Schedule T, and Pass-Through Withholding

When an LLC filing Form 568 has nonresident members (members who are not California residents), it has **two separate obligations** that are often confused (2025 Form 568 Booklet, General Information F, P, and R):

1. **Consent or Schedule T tax.** Each nonresident member signs FTB 3832, consenting to California's jurisdiction to tax their distributive share. For each member who doesn't sign, the LLC pays the "nonconsenting nonresident members' tax" on Schedule T.
2. **Withholding on distributions.** The LLC withholds 7% of distributions of California-source income to domestic nonresident members and remits it with Form 592-Q, reports it annually on Form 592-PTE, and issues Form 592-B. Foreign (non-U.S.) members are withheld on under R&TC §18666 (Form 592-A / 592-F).

The booklet is explicit: "The nonresident withholding requirements and procedures discussed above are not related to the nonconsenting nonresident members' tax paid by an LLC on behalf of nonresident members" (General Information R). Signing FTB 3832 does **not** remove the withholding obligation.

The agent's job: identify each nonresident member, determine consent status and distributions, and generate the right combination of consents, Schedule T entries, and withholding filings.

Sources: 2025 Form 568 Booklet (https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-568-booklet.pdf); 2025 FTB 3832; 2025 Forms 592-Q, 592-A, 592-PTE and the 2025 Form 592-PTE instructions (https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2025/2025-592-pte-instructions.html).

## FTB 3832 — Limited Liability Company Nonresident Members' Consent

**Purpose**: Each nonresident member signs FTB 3832 to consent to California's jurisdiction to tax their distributive share of LLC income attributable to California sources.

**Mechanic**:
1. List the nonresident members of record at the end of the taxable year, with identifying numbers
2. Each member signs (separate forms per member or group are allowed)
3. Attach FTB 3832 to Form 568 and give a copy to each nonresident member
4. File it for the first taxable period the LLC became subject to tax with nonresident members, and for any period with a nonresident member who has not yet signed; keep signed forms available for examination (2025 booklet, General Information P)

**What it does not do**: it does not satisfy the member's own California filing requirement (the member still files Form 540NR or is included in a group return), and it does not remove the LLC's withholding obligation on distributions.

A **single-member LLC** never files FTB 3832. Its owner consents by signing the Single Member LLC Information and Consent on Form 568, Side 3; a nonresident owner who doesn't sign is treated like a nonconsenting nonresident member (Schedule T).

## Schedule T (568) — Nonconsenting Nonresident Members' Tax Liability

For each nonresident member who did not sign FTB 3832 (and a nonresident single owner who did not sign the Side 3 consent), the LLC pays tax on the member's distributive share at these rates (2025 booklet, Schedule T instructions):

| Member type | Rate |
|-------------|------|
| Individual, partnership, LLC, estate, or trust | 12.3% |
| C corporation | 8.84% |
| S corporation | 1.5% |

| Column | What goes here |
|--------|----------------|
| (a) | Member's name |
| (b) | SSN, ITIN, or FEIN |
| (c) | Distributive share of income |
| (d) | Tax rate (above) |
| (e) | Member's total tax due = (c) × (d) |
| (f) | Amount withheld by this LLC on this member, reported on Form 592-B |
| (g) | Member's net tax due = (e) − (f), not less than zero |

Total of column (g) → Form 568, Side 1, **line 5** (the 2025 Schedule T caption says "line 4"; the Side 1 label and the booklet's line 5 instruction control). The tax is due by the **original** due date of the return; if the LLC files on extension, it pays with **FTB 3537**. The amount paid is a payment made on behalf of the member, shows on the member's Schedule K-1 (568) line 15e, and the LLC may recover it from the member. Late payment draws penalties and interest (R&TC §§19132, 19101).

Because column (f) subtracts the withholding already paid for that member, the LLC does not pay 12.3% on top of 7% for the same income; the Schedule T tax is the difference.

## Withholding on distributions to domestic nonresident members (R&TC §18662)

- **Who**: domestic (U.S.) nonresident members: individuals who are not California residents; corporations not qualified to do business in California or without a permanent place of business here; nonresident estates, trusts, partnerships, and LLCs without a permanent place of business in California.
- **Base and rate**: 7% of **distributions** (including property) of California-source income. An LLC with income inside and outside California applies a reasonable California ratio; the prior year's ratio is generally accepted. Returns of capital and distributions that are not taxable income need no withholding.
- **Threshold**: withholding is not required if the member's distributions of California-source income are $1,500 or less during the calendar year. Withholding on the first $1,500 is optional; it must begin once the calendar-year total exceeds $1,500 (2025 Form 592-PTE instructions).
- **Reduced rates / waivers**: requested in writing to Withholding Services and Compliance, MS F182, Franchise Tax Board, PO Box 942867, Sacramento CA 94267-0651, or online (search "588 online" at ftb.ca.gov); Form 589 requests a reduced rate (2025 booklet, General Information R; 2025 Form 592-PTE instructions).

### Payment — Form 592-Q (Payment Voucher for Pass-Through Entity Withholding)

Withholding is remitted in four payment periods (2025 Form 592-PTE instructions; 2025 Form 592-Q):

| Period (calendar 2025) | Due |
|------------------------|-----|
| January 1 – March 31 | April 15, 2025 |
| April 1 – May 31 | June 16, 2025 |
| June 1 – August 31 | September 15, 2025 |
| September 1 – December 31 | January 15, 2026 |

A weekend or holiday due date moves to the next business day. Mail Form 592-Q to Withholding Services and Compliance MS 182, Franchise Tax Board, PO Box 942867, Sacramento CA 94267-0651, or pay electronically.

### Annual return — Form 592-PTE (Pass-Through Entity Annual Withholding Return)

For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2020, a pass-through entity that withheld on behalf of a nonresident owner (or was withheld upon) reports the year's withholding on Form 592-PTE, filed no later than **January 31** of the year after the year the withholding was required to be remitted. A final payment due with Form 592-PTE uses the Supplemental Payment Voucher on Form 592-Q. Late or incomplete Form 592-PTE payee information: $40 per payee if filed 1–30 days late, $80 if 31 days to 6 months, $130 if more than 6 months; failure to withhold can make the LLC liable for the tax that should have been withheld (2025 Form 592-PTE instructions, E).

### Member statement — Form 592-B

Each member withheld upon gets a Form 592-B and attaches it to their California return to claim the credit.

## Foreign (non-U.S.) members (R&TC §18666)

If any portion of the LLC's California trade or business income is allocable under IRC §704 to a foreign nonresident member, the LLC withholds on the **allocable amount** at 8.84% (C corporations) or 12.3% (individuals, partnerships, LLCs, fiduciaries), plus 1% on a non-corporate foreign partner's taxable income over $1 million (2025 booklet, General Information R). Payments go on **Form 592-A** (installments due by the 15th day of the 4th, 6th, 9th, and 12th months), and the annual return is **Form 592-F**.

> **Out of scope**: foreign members also trigger federal §1446 withholding. Redirect to a CPA.

## When NOT to worry about nonresident obligations

The LLC can skip Schedule T and the 592 forms only if **all** of these are true:

- Every nonresident member signed FTB 3832 (attached to Form 568), or there are no nonresident members
- No foreign (non-U.S.) members
- No domestic nonresident member received more than $1,500 of distributions of California-source income in the calendar year

---

## Common mistakes

1. **Treating FTB 3832 as a withholding waiver.** It only avoids Schedule T. Distributions above $1,500 to a domestic nonresident still require 7% withholding.
2. **Filing Form 568 without FTB 3832 for a nonresident member and without Schedule T.** The LLC owes the nonconsenting nonresident members' tax at the member's rate, plus penalties and interest if late.
3. **Not issuing Form 592-B.** The member can't claim the credit without it.
4. **Withholding on the distributive share instead of distributions** for domestic members (distributive share is the base for Schedule T and for foreign members, not for domestic withholding).
5. **Part-year residents.** Residency for California tax purposes is fact-specific (R&TC §17014; FTB Pub. 1031). Ask the user for the member's residency facts; don't assume.
6. **Corporate members.** A corporate member of an LLC doing business in California is also considered doing business in California and may have its own filing requirements (2025 booklet, General Information F; FTB Pub. 1060).
