# Form 6252 Line-by-Line Reference

Every line of the **2025 Form 6252, Installment Sale Income (Created 5/28/25)**, built from the PDF text of the form and its instructions (printed on pages 2–4 of the same PDF). Check https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-6252 for the next revision before using this map for a later year.

Source PDF: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f6252.pdf. Supporting publication: Pub. 537 (2025), https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p537.pdf.

## Which parts to complete

| Situation | Complete |
|-----------|----------|
| Every year of the agreement, including the year of final payment, even with no payment | Lines 1–4, Part I, Part II |
| Sold to a related party | Also Part III for the year of sale and 2 years after the year of sale, unless the final payment was received during the tax year |
| Line 14 is zero or less | Do not file Form 6252; report the sale on Form 4797, Form 8949, or Schedule D |
| Line 24 would be less than zero (a loss) | Do not file Form 6252; report the entire sale on Form 4797, Form 8949, or Schedule D |
| Seller elected out | Do not file Form 6252 |

Use a separate Form 6252 for each sale or other disposition on the installment method (form header). For several assets that must be reported separately, Pub. 537 ("Reporting an Installment Sale") allows a single Form 6252 with an attached schedule per asset: in the year of sale write "See attached schedule" in the Part I margin and enter totals on lines 24, 25, 26 (and 35, 36, 37 in Part III).

Header: name(s) shown on return and identifying number. Attachment Sequence No. 67.

## Top of form (lines 1–4)

| Line | Form text | Rule (instructions) |
|------|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 | Description of property | Enter a code and a description. Code 1: sale property is a timeshare or residential lot. Code 2: sale by an individual of personal-use property (within the meaning of §1275(b)(3)). Code 3: sale of any property used or produced in the trade or business of farming (within the meaning of §2032A(e)(4) or (5)). Code 4: all other installment sales not listed. |
| 2a | Date acquired (mm/dd/yyyy) | Acquisition date; with 2b sets the holding period. More than 1 year = long-term (Pub. 537, "Other forms"). |
| 2b | Date sold (mm/dd/yyyy) | Closing date of the sale. |
| 3 | Was the property sold to a related party? | If "Yes," complete Part III for the year of sale and 2 years after, unless the final payment was received during the tax year. Related party for this purpose includes spouse, child, grandchild, parent, or sibling; or a related corporation, S corporation, partnership, estate, or trust (see §453(f)(1)). |
| 4 | Can the total selling price be determined by the close of the tax year in which the sale occurs? | If not, it is a contingent payment sale (Temp. Reg. §15a.453-1(c)); check "No." If "No" once, every later year must also be "No." |

## Part I — Gross Profit and Contract Price (lines 5–18)

"Complete this part for all years of the installment agreement." In later years the amounts are the year-of-sale amounts; they do not change unless the selling price is later reduced (Pub. 537, "Selling Price Reduced," Worksheet B).

| Line | Form text | What goes here |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| 5 | Selling price including mortgages and other debts. Don't include interest, whether stated or unstated | Total of money, face amount of the installment obligation, and FMV of other property or services received or to be received. Include any existing mortgage or other debt the buyer assumed or took the property subject to. Exclude stated interest, unstated interest, amounts recomputed or recharacterized as interest, and OID. Contingent sale with a stated maximum: enter the maximum. No stated maximum: attach a schedule showing the gain computation and enter the taxable part of the payment on line 24 (and line 35 if Part III applies). |
| 6 | Mortgages, debts, and other liabilities the buyer assumed or took the property subject to | Only the seller's existing debts the buyer assumed or took the property subject to. Not new mortgages the buyer gets from a bank, the seller, or other sources. |
| 7 | Subtract line 6 from line 5 | Computed. |
| 8 | Cost or other basis of property sold | Original cost and other acquisition expenses, plus improvements, minus casualty losses and the credits listed in the instructions (nonbusiness energy property, residential energy efficient property, adoption, DC first-time homebuyer, disabled access, new markets, employer-provided childcare, energy efficient home, alternative motor vehicle, alternative fuel vehicle refueling property, qualified railroad track maintenance, enhanced oil recovery, plug-in and electric vehicle credits). See Pub. 551. |
| 9 | Depreciation allowed or allowable | All depreciation or amortization deducted or allowed to be deducted from purchase to sale. Add §179 expense, commercial revitalization deduction, clean-fuel deductions, §190 and §193 deductions, certain §1253(d) deductions, and basis reduction for investment credit property. Subtract recapture amounts and credits listed in the instructions (§179 or §280F recapture, clean-fuel, investment credit, and others). |
| 10 | Adjusted basis. Subtract line 9 from line 8 | Computed. |
| 11 | Commissions and other expenses of sale | Sales commissions, advertising, attorney and legal fees, other selling expenses. |
| 12 | Income recapture from Form 4797, Part III | Ordinary income recapture under §1245 or §1250 (including §179 and §291), fully taxable in the year of sale even if no payment was received. It is the amount on Form 4797 line 31; enter it here and on Form 4797 line 13. Do not enter any gain for this property on Form 4797 line 32; if Form 4797 was used only to figure line 12, enter "N/A" on Form 4797 line 32. |
| 13 | Add lines 10, 11, and 12 | Installment sale basis (Pub. 537 Worksheet A, line 5). |
| 14 | Subtract line 13 from line 5. If zero or less, don't complete the rest of this form | If zero or less, do not file Form 6252; report the entire sale on Form 4797, Form 8949, or Schedule D. |
| 15 | If the property was your main home, enter the amount of your excluded gain. Otherwise, enter -0- | §121 exclusion; see Pub. 523. Excluded gain is not part of gross profit (Pub. 537, "Sale of your home"). |
| 16 | Gross profit. Subtract line 15 from line 14 | Total gain to be reported over the life of the note. |
| 17 | Subtract line 13 from line 6. If zero or less, enter -0- | Assumed debt in excess of installment sale basis; treated as a payment in the year of sale (Pub. 537, "Mortgage more than basis"). |
| 18 | Contract price. Add line 7 and line 17 | Total the seller will receive directly plus the deemed payment on line 17. When line 17 > 0, line 18 equals line 16 (if line 15 is 0) and the percentage is 100% (Pub. 537 tip). |

## Part II — Installment Sale Income (lines 19–26)

"Complete this part for all years of the installment agreement."

| Line | Form text | What goes here |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| 19 | Gross profit percentage (expressed as a decimal amount). Divide line 16 by line 18. (For years after the year of sale, see instructions.) | Year-of-sale percentage, even if Form 6252 was not filed that year. Enter as a decimal rounded to at least 4 digits, including zeros (25% = 0.2500; 35.25% = 0.3525; 100% = 1.0000). |
| 20 | If this is the year of sale, enter the amount from line 17. Otherwise, enter -0- | Deemed payment from assumed debt above basis. |
| 21 | Payments received during year. Don't include interest, whether stated or unstated | All money and FMV of property or services received this tax year. Include amounts withheld to pay off a mortgage or other debt, or to pay broker and legal fees. Do not include the buyer's note, a mortgage, or other debt assumed by the buyer, unless the note or debt is payable on demand or readily tradable on an established securities market (for sales before Oct. 22, 2004, only if issued by a corporation or governmental entity). Enter zero if no payment. Amounts entered on the equivalent of line 34 in a prior year go on line 23, not here. Pledge rule proceeds may be payments. |
| 22 | Add lines 20 and 21 | Computed. |
| 23 | Payments received in prior years. Don't include interest, whether stated or unstated | All money and FMV received before this tax year, including allocable installment income and deemed payments: a second disposition by a related party, the pledge rule of §453A(d), and liabilities assumed in excess of basis (the year-of-sale line 20; Reg. §15a.453-1(b)(3)(i)). |
| 24 | Installment sale income. Multiply line 22 by line 19. This amount cannot be less than zero | If the computed amount is less than zero, do not file Form 6252 (§453 and Rev. Rul. 70-430: no loss on the installment method). |
| 25 | Enter the part of line 24 that is ordinary income under the recapture rules | Ordinary income recapture on §1252, §1254, or §1255 property for the year of sale, or all remaining recapture from a prior-year sale; also on Form 4797 line 15. Do not include ordinary income from a §179 deduction. In the year of sale complete Form 4797 Part III; the amount from Form 4797 line 27c, 28b, or 29b is the recapture, and no gain for this property goes on Form 4797 lines 31 or 32 ("N/A" on both if Form 4797 was used only for this). Also report remaining recapture on §1245 or §1250 property sold before June 7, 1984. Never more than line 24; carry any excess to later years until all recapture is reported. |
| 26 | Subtract line 25 from line 24. Enter here and on Schedule D or Form 4797 | Trade or business property held more than 1 year → Form 4797 line 4. Held 1 year or less, or ordinary gain from a noncapital asset → Form 4797 line 10 with "From Form 6252." Section 1250 property held more than 1 year → figure unrecaptured §1250 gain included here on the Schedule D instructions' Unrecaptured Section 1250 Gain Worksheet. Capital assets → Schedule D as short-term (line 4) or long-term (line 11). |

## Part III — Related Party Installment Sale Income (lines 27–37)

"Don't complete if you received the final payment this tax year."

| Line | Form text | What goes here |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| 27 | Name, address, and taxpayer identifying number of related party | Buyer's identifying information. |
| 28 | Did the related party resell or dispose of the property ("second disposition") during this tax year? | Yes / No. If "No," stop Part III for the year. |
| 29a | The second disposition was more than 2 years after the first disposition (other than dispositions of marketable securities). If checked, enter the date of disposition | Check and skip lines 30–37. |
| 29b | The first disposition was a sale or exchange of stock to the issuing corporation | Check and skip lines 30–37. |
| 29c | The second disposition was an involuntary conversion and the threat of conversion occurred after the first disposition | Check and skip lines 30–37. |
| 29d | The second disposition occurred after the death of the original seller or buyer | Check and skip lines 30–37. |
| 29e | It can be established to the satisfaction of the IRS that tax avoidance wasn't a principal purpose for either of the dispositions | Check, attach an explanation, skip lines 30–37. Generally applies if the second disposition was involuntary (foreclosure by a creditor of the related party, bankruptcy) or was an installment sale with payment terms substantially equal to or longer than the first sale's, without significant deferral of the first sale's gain. |
| 30 | Selling price of property sold by related party | Amount realized from the part resold this year; if part was sold earlier and part this year, the cumulative amount realized. Amount realized = money + FMV of property or services received + liabilities assumed by the buyer or to which the property is subject (Pub. 544). For a gift or other non-sale disposition, Pub. 537 uses the FMV of the property. |
| 31 | Enter contract price from line 18 for year of first sale | Year-of-sale line 18. |
| 32 | Enter the smaller of line 30 or line 31 | Computed. |
| 33 | Total payments received by the end of this tax year | Sum of lines 22 and 23 from Part II: all money and FMV received by year end, including deemed payments; no interest. |
| 34 | Subtract line 33 from line 32. If zero or less, enter -0- | Amount treated as received because of the second disposition. In later years this amount goes on line 23, not line 21. |
| 35 | Multiply line 34 by the gross profit percentage on line 19 for year of first sale | Computed. |
| 36 | Enter the part of line 35 that is ordinary income under the recapture rules | Same rules as line 25; not more than line 35; excess carries to later years. |
| 37 | Subtract line 36 from line 35. Enter here and on Schedule D or Form 4797 | Same routing as line 26. |
