# Form 720 Common Mistakes

Errors that produce wrong Form 720 drafts, §6651 failure-to-file or failure-to-pay penalties, or §6656 failure-to-deposit penalties. Each item names the check the agent runs before declaring a draft ready.

**Verified against:** Form 720 (Rev. June 2026), Instructions for Form 720 (Rev. June 2026), Pub. 510 (Rev. Dec. 2025), Notices 2024-83 and 2025-61, Instructions for Form 2290 (Rev. July 2026), on 2026-10-06. Re-check against the current revisions at https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-720.

---

## Line and IRS No. errors

1. **Using an old line map.** IRS Nos. are not sequential and some old numbers are gone. On the June 2026 revision, 22 is local telephone service (not air transportation), 26 is transportation of persons by air, 28 is property by air, 27 is international air travel facilities, 30 is foreign insurance, 104 is diesel-water emulsion, 125 is LUST on inland waterways fuel. Check: every IRS No. in the draft matches the text of the current Form 720 PDF.

2. **Fishing rods on IRS No. 41.** Rods and poles are IRS No. 110 (10%, max $10 per article). Electric outboard motors are 42 and tackle boxes are 114, both 3%.

3. **Entering expired taxes.** IRS Nos. 18 and 21 (oil spill liability taxes) expired after 2025, and Schedule C lines 12 and 13 (biodiesel/renewable diesel mixture and alternative fuel credits) are reserved because those credits expired after 2024 (i720 caution and "What's New"; Pub. 510 p. 2). Check: no entries on those lines unless Congress extends the taxes or credits.

4. **Missing the new remittance transfer tax.** Remittance transfer providers owe 1% of cash-funded transfers made after Dec 31, 2025 on IRS No. 155 (§4475). Deposit penalty relief for Q1–Q3 2026 is in Notice 2025-55. Check: ask any money transmitter client whether it collects the tax.

5. **Wrong PCOR row or rate.** The rate depends on the plan year end: $3.47 for plan years ending Oct 1, 2024 – Sep 30, 2025 (rows 133(a)/(c)); $3.84 for Oct 1, 2025 – Sep 30, 2026 (rows 133(b)/(d)) (Notices 2024-83, 2025-61). A calendar-2025 plan belongs on row 133(d), not 133(c).

## Schedule and deposit errors

6. **Putting Part II taxes on Schedule A.** Schedule A is for Part I taxes only. PCOR, sport fishing, archery, indoor tanning, and other Part II taxes never go on it (Form 720 page 4 note).

7. **Skipping Schedule A for a small Part I liability.** Schedule A is completed whenever Part I shows a liability, even under $2,500 (i720 "Schedule A").

8. **Missing semimonthly deposits.** If the quarter's Part I net liability exceeds $2,500, deposits are due by EFT by the 14th day after each semimonthly period, plus the special September deposit. Paying everything with the return instead can trigger the §6656 penalty (2%, 5%, 10%, or 15%) (i720 "Payment of Taxes", Part III line 10 caution).

9. **Making deposits that aren't required, then misreporting them.** PCOR fees and Part II taxes (other than the ODC floor stocks tax) need no deposits. If the filer did pay through EFTPS, report the payment on line 5; for PCOR apply it to the second quarter (i720 "Reporting and paying the fee").

10. **Misreading Part III.** Line 5 is deposits, not "net tax". Line 6 is overpayment carried from earlier quarters (and Form 720-X line 5b); line 7 is the Form 720-X part of line 6; line 8 = 5 + 6; line 9 = 4 + 8; line 10 or line 11a is the difference from line 3.

## Claims errors

11. **Claiming HVUT on Form 720.** Schedule C has no Heavy Highway Vehicle Use Tax line. HVUT credits for vehicles sold, destroyed, or stolen before June 1 go on the next Form 2290 (line 5) or Form 8849 Schedule 6 (Instructions for Form 2290, Rev. July 2026, "Line 5").

12. **Using Schedule C without a Form 720 liability.** Schedule C is only for filers reporting a liability in Part I or II. Others use Form 8849 or Form 4136 (i720 "Schedule C. Claims").

13. **Wrong CRN or rate.** CRNs are printed next to each Schedule C line; for example, undyed diesel nontaxable use and farm use are CRN 360 at $.243, gasoline nontaxable use is CRN 362 at $.183, undyed kerosene is CRN 346. Check every claim against the current form.

14. **Claims below the minimum.** Lines 1–6 and 14b–14d need a combined $750 for the quarter (or aggregated quarters of the income tax year); ultimate vendor claims on lines 7, 10, 11 need $200 and lines 8–9 need $100. Otherwise the claim is made annually on Form 4136 (i720 claim requirements).

15. **Ultimate vendor claim without registration.** Schedule C lines 7–11 and 14e require the Form 637 registration number.

## Filing and payment errors

16. **Filing PCOR on the wrong quarter.** The PCOR fee is reported only on the Q2 Form 720, due July 31 of the year after the plan year ends. A PCOR-only filer doesn't file Q1, Q3, or Q4 returns.

17. **Stopping quarterly filing without a final return.** A filer liable in a prior quarter must keep filing until it files a final return; with no tax for the quarter, enter "None" on line 3 and sign (i720 "Who Must File", "How To File").

18. **Mailing to an old address.** The June 2026 instructions list one address for Form 720: Department of the Treasury, Internal Revenue Service, Ogden, UT 84201-0009. Check the current instructions before mailing.

19. **Assuming e-file is mandatory or that it can't carry a payment.** E-filing Form 720 is optional (IRS Form 720 e-file FAQ). An e-filed return can pay the balance by electronic funds withdrawal; paper filers can pay by check or money order with Form 720-V, EFTPS, or Direct Pay (i720 "Making a Payment").

20. **Fixing a prior quarter on the current return.** Adjustments to earlier quarters go on Form 720-X, not on Form 720.
