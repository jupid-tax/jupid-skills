# Form 720 Line-by-Line Reference

Complete walkthrough of every section on Form 720 (Quarterly Federal Excise Tax Return).

**Revision:** built from the text of Form 720 (Rev. June 2026) and the Instructions for Form 720 (Rev. June 2026), downloaded from irs.gov on 2026-10-06. Form 720 is revised when rates or taxes change, not every year. Before using this map for a later quarter, check https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-720 and re-check every IRS No., rate, and CRN against the current PDF.

Form 720 has a header, three numbered Parts, Schedule A (page 4), Schedule T (page 4), Schedule C (pages 5–7), and the Form 720-V payment voucher. Most filers use only a handful of lines.

## Schedule A — Excise tax liability (Part I taxes only)

Complete Schedule A whenever Part I shows a liability, even if the net liability is under $2,500. Don't complete it for Part II taxes or for a one-time gas guzzler filing (Form 720 page 4 note; i720 "Schedule A").

**Line 1, regular method taxes (record of net tax liability):**

| Month of quarter | 1st–15th day | 16th–last day |
|------------------|--------------|---------------|
| First month | A | B |
| Second month | C | D |
| Third month | E | F |
| Special rule for September | G | |

Line 1(b) = sum of the boxes.

**Line 2, alternative method taxes (IRS Nos. 22, 26, 28, 27), record of taxes considered collected:** boxes M–R by the same semimonthly grid, S for the September special rule; line 2(b) = sum.

Net tax liability for a semimonthly period is the liability for the period minus Schedule C claims allowed for the period. It may be figured as the month's net liability ÷ 2 if done for every period in the quarter. It isn't reduced by Form 720-X amounts.

**September special rule (2026 dates, i720 "Additional Deposit of Taxes in September 2026"):** regular method liability for Sept. 16–26 is deposited by Sept. 29; liability for Sept. 27–30 is deposited by Oct. 10, moved to the preceding business day because Oct. 10, 2026 is a Saturday (Friday, Oct. 9). On the Q3 Schedule A, the regular method liability for Sept. 27–30 goes in box F, and box G is completed only as the instructions direct for the special September period. Alternative method: Sept. 1–11 deposited by Sept. 29; amounts for Sept. 12–15 and Sept. 16–30 are reported in boxes M and N of the Q4 return; see Pub. 509 for deposit dates.

## Deposit rules (Part I taxes)

- **Who deposits:** generally anyone with Part I liability. No deposit is required when the Part I net liability for the quarter is $2,500 or less, for a one-time gas guzzler filing, for the PCOR fee on the Q2 return, or for Part II taxes other than the ODC floor stocks tax (i720 "Payment of Taxes").
- **How:** by electronic funds transfer (EFTPS or same-day wire). EFTPS deposits must be initiated by 8:00 p.m. Eastern the day before the due date.
- **When (regular method):** by the 14th day after the semimonthly period: generally the 29th of the same month for the 1st–15th and the 14th of the next month for the 16th–last day. A due date on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday moves to the preceding business day.
- **When (alternative method, IRS Nos. 22, 26, 27, 28):** tax in amounts billed or tickets sold during a semimonthly period is treated as collected in the first 7 days of the second following semimonthly period and is deposited by the third business day after that 7th day.
- **Amount:** at least 95% of the period's net tax liability, unless the safe harbor applies.
- **Safe harbor:** a filer who filed Form 720 for the lookback quarter (the second calendar quarter before the current one) meets the requirement if each semimonthly deposit is at least 1/6 (16.67%) of the lookback quarter's net liability (11/90 for the additional September deposit), pays any underpayment by the return due date, and checks the box on line 5. Not available for the first two quarters after a rate increase (unless deposits reflect the higher rate) or for any quarter with a tax not in effect throughout the lookback quarter.
- **Penalty:** §6656, 2% (1–5 days late), 5% (6–15 days), 10% (more than 15 days), 15% (not paid within 10 days after the first delinquency notice).

Claim minimums and timing (i720): lines 1–6 and 14b–14d need a combined claim of at least $750 for the quarter (or aggregated quarters of the income tax year not otherwise claimed), filed by the end of the first quarter after the last quarter included; only one claim per quarter. ## Signature block

- Signature of a person authorized by the entity, date, title, typed or printed name, telephone number
- Third Party Designee (name, phone, 5-digit PIN); the authorization expires 1 year after the return's due date
- Paid Preparer Use Only: name, signature, date, PTIN, firm name, firm EIN, firm address, phone

For e-file, the provider handles the signature, often with Form 8453-EX (Excise Tax Declaration for an IRS e-file Return, Rev. Dec. 2011), which also carries the electronic funds withdrawal authorization.

## Special timing rules

1. **PCOR fee**: filed once per year on the Q2 Form 720, due July 31 of the calendar year immediately following the last day of the plan or policy year. Plan year ending Dec 31, 2024 → July 31, 2025. Plan year ending June 30, 2025 or Dec 31, 2025 → July 31, 2026. The rate row depends on the plan year end date, not the filing year.

2. **No-tax-due quarters**: a filer who has filed Form 720 before and hasn't filed a final return must keep filing each quarter. If no tax is due, enter "None" on Part III line 3 and sign the return. A filer who files only for the PCOR fee doesn't file for Q1, Q3, or Q4 (i720 "Who Must File", "How To File").

3. **Final return**: when the filer goes out of business or won't owe Form 720 taxes in future quarters, check the "Final return" box. A filer reporting zero tax with no future liability can check the box on that return.

4. **Combined returns**: a single Form 720 covers all IRS Nos. for the same EIN for the quarter. A filer with both the PCOR fee and sport fishing tax files one Q2 Form 720 with both lines completed.

5. **Corrections**: adjustments to prior quarters go on Form 720-X, not on Form 720.

6. **Successor businesses**: when the business is acquired or merged, the new entity files under its own EIN; the prior entity files a final return.
