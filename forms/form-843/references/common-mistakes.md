# Form 843 — Common Mistakes

Failure patterns to check before a draft goes to the user. Each entry gives the mistake, why it happens, the fix, and the authority.

---

## 1. Filing Form 843 for an income tax refund

**Mistake:** The user overpaid income tax (missed deduction, wrong 1099) and files Form 843.

**Why:** "Claim for Refund" in the title.

**Fix:** Income tax refunds and changes to Form 1040 amounts go on Form 1040-X. Penalties and interest connected to income tax can still use Form 843 (line 4 box e).

**Authority:** i843 (Rev. 12-2024), caution under Purpose of Form: "Do not use Form 843 to ... request a refund of income tax or Additional Medicare Tax."

## 2. Aiming Form 843 at the estimated tax penalty

**Mistake:** Requesting First Time Abate or reasonable cause for a §6654 penalty.

**Why:** It is called a penalty and shows on the same notice as other penalties.

**Fix:** FTA covers only §6651(a)(1)–(3), §6698(a)(1), §6699(a)(1), and §6656. The IRS reasonable cause page says reasonable cause "doesn't apply to certain penalties such as the estimated tax penalty." Route to Form 2210 waiver rules (casualty, disaster, unusual circumstance; retirement after 62 or disability).

**Authority:** IRM 20.1.1.3.3.2.1(1); https://www.irs.gov/payments/penalty-relief-for-reasonable-cause; Instructions for Form 2210, "Waiver of Penalty".

## 3. One form for several years or tax types

**Mistake:** A single Form 843 listing 2022, 2023, and 2024 penalties.

**Fix:** "Generally, you must file a separate Form 843 for each tax period or fee year or type of tax or fee." Only net-interest-zero requests and a single IRS act under §6404(e) affecting several years are exceptions.

**Authority:** i843, "Separate Form Required".

## 4. More than one reason box checked at the top

**Mistake:** Checking both the penalty box and the interest box because the notice shows both.

**Fix:** "Do not check more than one box." Interest on an abated penalty comes off automatically. Interest on the tax itself needs its own §6404(e) form if it qualifies.

**Authority:** i843, "Checkboxes"; IRS reasonable cause page, "Interest relief".

## 5. Mailing a written FTA request when a call would do

**Mistake:** The user has a notice and a clean history but waits weeks for a paper decision.

**Fix:** The IRS FTA page lists the toll-free number on the notice as the first route. Use Form 843 when the call cannot approve it, the request is for a refund close to the §6511 deadline (a written claim must be filed within the period), or there is no notice.

**Authority:** https://www.irs.gov/payments/penalty-relief-due-to-first-time-abate-or-other-administrative-waiver; IRC §6511(b)(1).

## 6. Missing the refund lookback

**Mistake:** The penalty was paid in 2021 on a 2020 return filed in 2021; the claim goes in now.

**Fix:** Run the §6511 worksheet in `refund-and-interest-rules.md`. If the claim is outside 3 years from filing and 2 years from payment, no refund is allowed regardless of merit. If inside, the refund is capped by payments inside the lookback.

**Authority:** IRC §6511(a), (b)(2); i843, "Separate Form Required" paragraph on claim timing.

## 7. Using a reason the IRS lists as generally insufficient

**Mistake:** "My accountant forgot," "I didn't know," "I didn't have the money," as the only ground.

**Fix:** Tell the user these generally do not qualify. Ask for other facts (illness, disaster, records unavailable, IRS system problems) and documents. If none exist, check FTA eligibility instead.

**Authority:** https://www.irs.gov/payments/penalty-relief-for-reasonable-cause, "factors don't generally qualify".

## 8. A narrative without dates or documents

**Mistake:** Line 8 says "I was going through a hard time" with nothing attached.

**Fix:** State what happened, when it started and ended, how it prevented filing or payment, and what the taxpayer did once it ended. Attach dated evidence (hospital records, insurance or FEMA documents, death certificate, correspondence). Put the name and TIN on every attachment.

**Authority:** IRS reasonable cause page, "How to request penalty relief"; i843, Line 8.

## 9. One explanation stretched over two penalties

**Mistake:** Hospital dates explain the late filing, but the user also asks for failure-to-pay relief for months after discharge.

**Fix:** Address each penalty separately. If the facts cover only part of one penalty, request only that part and tell the user why.

**Authority:** IRM 20.1.1 (each penalty is a different type of failure; only the penalties requested are considered).

## 10. Assuming a prior FTA does not matter

**Mistake:** The user got FTA two years ago and asks for it again.

**Fix:** A penalty removed under FTA (Penalty Reason Code 018 or 020) in the three-year lookback blocks FTA. Move to reasonable cause or another ground.

**Authority:** IRM 20.1.1.3.3.2.1(4)(c); IRM letter text: "This type of penalty removal is only available one time."

## 11. Wrong line 6 or line 4 entry

**Mistake:** Line 6 left blank for a penalty, or line 4 set to the penalty type instead of the tax type.

**Fix:** Line 6 is the Code section from the notice (for example 6651(a)(1)). Line 4 is the tax the penalty relates to (income for a Form 1040 penalty, employment for a Form 941 penalty).

**Authority:** i843, Lines 4 and 6.

## 12. Joint return signed by one spouse

**Mistake:** Only the spouse who handled the taxes signs.

**Fix:** "If you are filing Form 843 to request a refund or an abatement relating to a joint return, both you and your spouse from the related joint return must sign."

**Authority:** i843, Signature.

## 13. Representative files without Form 2848

**Fix:** Attach the original or a copy of Form 2848 signed by the taxpayer and covering the matter.

**Authority:** i843, "Who Can File".

## 14. Treating a denial as final

**Mistake:** Ignoring Letter 854C.

**Fix:** The appeal window is generally 30 days from the denial letter. Use the small case request when tax and penalties per period are $25,000 or less; otherwise a formal written protest.

**Authority:** https://www.irs.gov/appeals/penalty-appeal; Publication 5 (Rev. 4-2021).

## 15. Asking FTA for a period that AEP should have covered

**Mistake:** A 2025 Form 1040 penalty with a clean three-year history; the user files Form 843 without first asking why AEP did not apply.

**Fix:** AEP applies automatically to 2025 tax year returns and 2026 quarterly returns of eligible series. If a penalty was assessed anyway, call the notice number first; the IRS says "contact us."

**Authority:** https://www.irs.gov/payments/penalty-relief-due-to-first-time-abate-or-other-administrative-waiver (AEP section).
