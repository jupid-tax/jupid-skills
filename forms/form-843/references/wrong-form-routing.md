# When Form 843 Is the Wrong Form

The Form 843 caution line reads: "Do not use Form 843 when you must use a different tax form." The instructions list the replacements. Run every request through this table before drafting. If the request lands on another form, stop and route; do not file Form 843 "just in case".

Source: Instructions for Form 843 (Rev. December 2024), "Purpose of Form" and "Do not use Form 843 when you must use a different tax form", https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i843.pdf; IRC §6404(b), https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6404.

---

## Hard exclusions from the instructions

| The user wants to | Use instead | Sibling skill |
|---|---|---|
| Get a refund of income tax, or change any amount on Form 1040, 1040-SR, 1040-NR (including amounts the IRS already adjusted), or make a late election under Treas. Reg. §301.9100 | Form 1040-X | `../../form-1040-x/SKILL.md` |
| Abate income, estate, or gift **tax** itself | Not available by claim: IRC §6404(b) says "No claim for abatement shall be filed by a taxpayer in respect of an assessment of any tax imposed under subtitle A or B." Dispute through the notice process, audit reconsideration, or amended return | `../../form-1040-x/SKILL.md` |
| Correct Additional Medicare Tax | Form 1040-X with a corrected Form 8959 | `../../form-8959/SKILL.md` |
| Employer: adjust or refund FICA, RRTA, or income tax withholding | The matching X form: 941-X, 943-X, 944-X, 945-X, CT-1 X (Spanish versions likewise). Schedule H errors: Pub. 926 | `../../form-941/SKILL.md`, `../../schedule-h/SKILL.md` |
| Amend Form 940 (FUTA) | A corrected Form 940 for the year | `../../form-940/SKILL.md` |
| Claim a credit for nontaxable use of fuel | Form 4136 (annual) or Form 8849 (periodic refund) | none |
| Get a refund of excise tax reported on Form 11-C or 2290, or other excise refunds not from liability adjustments | Form 8849 (see Pub. 510) | none |
| Adjust liability on a filed Form 720 | Form 720-X (Form 8849 for refunds) | none |
| Claim a credit or refund of wagering tax | Form 730 or Schedule 6 (Form 8849) | none |
| Get back tax return preparer or promoter penalties | Form 6118 | none |
| Claim a share of a joint refund taken for a spouse's debt | Form 8379 (injured spouse) | none |
| Quick refund from an NOL, credit carryback, or claim of right adjustment (individuals, estates, trusts) | Form 1045 within 1 year, or Form 1040-X; estates and trusts can file an amended Form 1041 | `../../form-1040-x/SKILL.md` |

| The user wants to | Route | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Remove the estimated tax penalty (§6654) | Form 2210 Part II waiver on the return, or a written statement to the notice address if already billed. ## What Form 843 does cover (from the instructions)

- Abatement or refund of tax other than income, estate, or gift tax, when no other form applies.
- Employee refunds of excess social security, Medicare, or RRTA tax from one employer that will not adjust; excess tier 2 RRTA with multiple railroad employers; social security or Medicare withheld in error when the employer will not adjust.
- Tier 1 RRTA for an employee representative.
- Abatement or refund of penalties and additions to tax for reasonable cause or another reason allowed by law, including the §6676 erroneous refund claim penalty.
- Trust Fund Recovery Penalty (§6672) abatement or refund.
- §6695A appraisal misstatement and §6715 dyed fuel penalty refunds.
- §6404(f) erroneous written advice relief.
- §6404(e)(1) interest abatement for IRS error or delay.
- Net interest rate of zero under Rev. Proc. 2000-26.
- Relief for taxpayers unable to read and respond to standard print notices.
- Branded prescription drug fee and health insurance provider fee refunds.

## Decision questions to ask the user

1. "Is the amount you want back income tax, or is it a penalty, interest, or a non-income tax?" Income tax goes to Form 1040-X.
2. "Is the penalty the estimated tax penalty (the notice says 'underpayment of estimated tax' or cites section 6654)?" If yes, route to form-2210.
3. "Are you an employer correcting payroll tax returns?" If yes, use the X form for that return.
4. "Did the IRS notice tell you how to respond?" If yes, follow the notice, which may be a phone call or a reply to the notice address.
5. "Is this about a user fee for a payment plan, offer, or lien?" If yes, Form 843 cannot refund it.
