# Form 5500 Series Line-by-Line Reference

Lookup for Form 5500, Form 5500-SF, and Form 5500-EZ. Use when the agent needs to confirm where a piece of plan data belongs or what a line means.

**Revision verified:** 2025 Form 5500, 2025 Form 5500-SF, and 2025 Schedules H and I (DOL/IRS/PBGC, for plan years beginning in 2025, filed in 2026), 2025 Form 5500-EZ (IRS), and Form 5558 (Rev. January 2025). Line numbers move between years: re-check the next year's forms at https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ebsa/employers-and-advisers/plan-administration-and-compliance/reporting-and-filing/form-5500 and https://www.irs.gov/Form5500EZ before using this map for a 2026 plan year.

## Form 5500-EZ (one-participant plans and foreign plans)

Five parts, lines A–E and 1a–12. Source: 2025 Form 5500-EZ and its instructions.

### Part I — Annual Return Identification Information

| Line | Field | What goes here |
|------|-------|----------------|
| Plan year (top) | Calendar plan year 2025, or fiscal year beginning/ending dates | Not more than 12 months |
| A(1) | First return filed for the plan | Check only if no return (any form, including Form 5500 or 5500-SF) was ever filed for this plan |
| A(2) | Amended return | Correcting a previously filed 2025 return |
| A(3) | Final return | All assets distributed or transferred to another plan |
| A(4) | Short plan year return | Less than 12 months; enter dates at top |
| B | Filing under Form 5558 / automatic extension / special extension | Automatic extension = employer's income tax extension with same tax year and plan year |
| C | Foreign plan | Pension plan maintained outside the U.S. primarily for nonresident aliens |
| D | IRS Late Filer Penalty Relief Program | Paper filing only (Rev. Proc. 2015-32) |
| E | Retroactively adopted plan (SECURE Act §201) | Plan adopted after year-end by the sponsor's tax return due date |

### Part II — Basic Plan Information

| Line | Field | What goes here |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 1a | Name of plan | Formal plan name |
| 1b | Three-digit plan number (PN) | 001 for the first plan; never reused for another plan |
| 1c | Date plan first became effective | MM/DD/YYYY |
| 2a | Employer's name, trade name, in-care-of name, mailing address | One item per row |
| 2b | Employer EIN | Never an SSN |
| 2c | Employer's telephone number | |
| 2d | Business code | Six-digit principal business activity code from the instructions |
| 3a | Plan administrator's name and address | Enter "Same" if the administrator is the employer; then leave 3a rest, 3b, 3c blank |
| 3b | Administrator's EIN | Required if 3a is not "Same" |
| 3c | Administrator's telephone number | |
| 4a–4d | Prior employer name, EIN, plan name, PN | Only if the employer name, EIN, or plan name changed since the last return |
| 5a(1) | Total participants at beginning of plan year | Owner (and spouse) or partners (and spouses) |
| 5a(2) | Active participants at beginning of plan year | |
| 5b(1) | Total participants at end of plan year | |
| 5b(2) | Active participants at end of plan year | |
| 5c | Participants who terminated employment during the year with accrued benefits less than 100% vested | Usually 0 |

### Part III — Financial Information

| Line | Field | What goes here |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 6a(1)/(2) | Total plan assets, beginning / end of year | Include rollovers, transfers, unrealized gains, participant loans. Column (1) excludes contributions designated for 2025. Line 6a(2) is the figure used for the $250,000 test |
| 6b(1)/(2) | Total plan liabilities | Benefit claims payable, operating payables, acquisition indebtedness; not future distributions |
| 6c(1)/(2) | Net plan assets | 6a − 6b. Column (1) must equal last year's column (2) |
| 7a | Contributions received or receivable from employers | Cash employer contributions for the plan year |
| 7b | Contributions from participants | Elective deferrals |
| 7c | Other contributions (including rollovers) | Value on date contributed |

There are no income, expense, or distribution lines on Form 5500-EZ. Keep the full rollforward in the workpapers.

### Part IV — Plan Characteristics

| Line | Field | What goes here |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 8 | Two-character plan characteristic codes | Do not leave blank. Typical solo 401(k): 2E (profit-sharing), 2J (section 401(k) feature), 3B (plan covering self-employed individuals, when the owner is self-employed), 3D (pre-approved plan with an IRS opinion letter). Take every code from the plan document; see the code list in the 5500-EZ instructions |

### Part V — Compliance and Funding Questions

| Line | Question | Answer |
|------|----------|--------|
| 9 | Any participant loans during the year? | Yes/No; if Yes, enter the year-end amount outstanding (exclude loans deemed distributed) |
| 10 | Defined benefit plan subject to minimum funding? | If Yes, the enrolled actuary completes Schedule SB; keep it in records, do not file it |
| 10a | Unpaid minimum required contributions | From Schedule SB line 40 |
| 11 | Defined contribution plan subject to §412 minimum funding? | Money purchase / target benefit plans only |
| 11a | Date of letter ruling granting a funding waiver being amortized | |
| 11b–11d | Minimum required contribution, employer contribution, difference | 11d = 11b − 11c |
| 11e | Will 11d be met by the funding deadline? | Yes / No / N/A |
| 12 | Opinion letter date and serial number | If the sponsor adopted a pre-approved plan; serial number is a capital letter plus six digits |

### Sign here

The employer or plan administrator signs and dates a paper Form 5500-EZ.

### Required attachments

None. Do not file Schedule SB or MB with Form 5500-EZ; keep the signed schedules with the plan records (5500-EZ instructions, What To File).

## Form 5500-SF (small plan, short form)

Eligibility (all required; 2025 Form 5500-SF instructions, Who May File): fewer than 100 participants at the beginning of the plan year (or the 80–120 rule); no employer securities at any time; 100% of assets in "eligible plan assets" at all times; eligible for the small plan audit waiver under 29 CFR 2520.104-46 other than through enhanced bonding; not a multiemployer plan, pooled employer plan, Form M-1 filer, ESOP, or DCG member. Defined contribution plans count participants with account balances (line 5c(1); line 5c(2) on a first return).

### Signature

The plan administrator signs electronically in EFAST2 (UserID + PIN). There is also a line for the employer/plan sponsor.

### Attachments

Schedule SB (single-employer and multiple-employer DB plans), Schedule MB lines 3, 9, 10 (money purchase plans amortizing a funding waiver), Schedule MEP (multiple-employer pension plans). Nothing else. Do not attach Form 8955-SSA; it goes directly to the IRS.

### IQPA audit attachment (large plans)

ERISA §103(a)(3)(A) requires an audit by an independent qualified public accountant for plans filing Schedule H. The accountant's report is attached to the filing; Schedule H Part III records the opinion type. The DOL independence standards are in 29 CFR 2509.2022-01.

Audit types (Schedule H line 3b):
- **Full scope:** check box (3), neither regulation
- **ERISA section 103(a)(3)(C) audit:** box (1) for 29 CFR 2520.103-8 (certified investment information from a bank or insurer) and/or box (2) for 29 CFR 2520.103-12(d)

## Schedule A — Insurance Information

Required when any benefits are provided by an insurance company, insurance service, or similar organization, including investment contracts such as guaranteed investment contracts and pooled separate accounts. One Schedule A per contract. Not filed for administrative-services-only contracts. Reports carrier, contract, premiums, and commissions and fees paid to agents and brokers.

---

## Schedule C — Service Provider Information

Large plans only. Part I lists each service provider that received, directly or indirectly, $5,000 or more in reportable compensation in connection with the plan; Part II lists providers that failed to give the information needed for Part I; Part III reports terminated accountants or actuaries.

---

## Schedule R — Retirement Plan Information

Attached to Form 5500 for pension plans (tax-qualified and nonqualified), except IRA-funded plans. Reports distributions, funding, nondiscrimination and coverage, and plan amendments, plus certain DB information.

---

## Schedule SB — Single-Employer Defined Benefit Plan Actuarial Information

Single-employer and multiple-employer DB plans subject to minimum funding (including eligible combined plans). Completed and signed by the enrolled actuary. Filed with Form 5500 or 5500-SF; kept in records (not filed) with Form 5500-EZ.

---

## Schedule MB — Multiemployer DB Plan and Certain Money Purchase Plan Actuarial Information

Multiemployer DB plans, and money purchase plans amortizing a funding waiver (lines 3, 9, and 10 only).

---

## Schedule D — DFE/Participating Plan Information

Part I for a plan that invested in any CCT, PSA, MTIA, or 103-12 IE during the year. Part II for DFE filings (other than DCGs).

---

## Schedule G — Financial Transaction Schedules

Large plans and certain DFEs when Schedule H line 4b, 4c, or 4d is "Yes": Part I loans/fixed income obligations in default or uncollectible, Part II leases in default or uncollectible, Part III nonexempt transactions.

---

## Schedule MEP and Schedule DCG

Schedule MEP: multiple-employer pension plans (Form 5500 or 5500-SF). Schedule DCG: individual plan information for plans in a defined contribution group reporting arrangement.

## Form 5558 — Application for Extension of Time To File Certain Employee Plan Returns (Rev. January 2025)

| Item | Field |
|------|-------|
| A | Name and address of filer, plan administrator, or plan sponsor |
| B | EIN |
| C | Plan name |
| D | Plan number |
| E | Plan year end date |
| Line 1 | Check if the extension is for the plan's first Form 5500 series return |
| Line 2 | Requested due date for Form 5500 / 5500-SF / 5500-EZ (no later than the 15th day of the 3rd month after the normal due date) |
| Line 3 | Requested due date for Form 8955-SSA |

File on or before the normal due date (July 31 for a calendar plan year; extended date October 15). Approval is automatic for a timely, complete form. Beginning January 1, 2025, file electronically through EFAST2 or on paper with the Internal Revenue Service Center, Ogden, UT 84201-0045. One Form 5558 per plan. It does not extend the PBGC premium filing.
