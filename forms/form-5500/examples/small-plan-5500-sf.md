# Example — 50-employee 401(k); Form 5500-SF small-plan filing

## Scenario

**Filer**: TechWidget LLC, a software services company

**Plan**: "TechWidget LLC 401(k) Plan" — established 2018, calendar plan year

**Filing year**: Plan year 2025

**Why this matters**: TechWidget has 50 active employees in the 401(k). The participant count is well under the 100-participant threshold. The plan invests entirely in mutual funds and a bank money market account (all eligible plan assets) and meets the small plan audit waiver. Filing is on Form 5500-SF (Short Form): no schedules attach and no IQPA audit is required.

## Eligible-asset test for 5500-SF

Confirmed every plan asset was an "eligible plan asset" at all times during 2025 (2025 Form 5500-SF instructions, line 6a):

- Index and target-date mutual funds (registered investment companies) ✓
- Money market fund shares (registered investment company) ✓
- Cash held by the bank trustee ✓
- One participant loan meeting ERISA §408(b)(1) ✓

No employer securities at any time. No real estate, hedge funds, or private equity.

Audit waiver (29 CFR 2520.104-46): 100% of assets were qualifying plan assets on 12/31/2024, the SAR carries the waiver disclosure, and the plan will furnish institution statements on request. The waiver is met without relying on an enhanced bond.

Not a multiemployer plan, pooled employer plan, ESOP, DCG member, or Form M-1 filer.

→ Plan **qualifies for Form 5500-SF**.

## Variant selection

Walked the decision tree:

1. SEP-IRA / SIMPLE IRA / governmental / church plan? No — qualified 401(k).
2. One-participant plan? No (50 active employees).
3. Welfare plan? No (pension plan).
4. Pension plan with < 100 participants with account balances on 1/1/2025? Yes (52).
5. Every 5500-SF condition met (eligible plan assets, no employer securities, audit waiver without enhanced bond, not multiemployer/PEP/ESOP/DCG/M-1)? Yes (verified above).
6. → File **Form 5500-SF**.

## Filing channel

Form 5500-SF must be filed **electronically through EFAST2** (2025 Form 5500-SF instructions, How To File).

TechWidget's CFO is registered in EFAST2 as a Filing Signer (Login.gov sign-in, User ID + PIN). The plan's TPA, registered as Filing Author, prepared the filing in IFILE; the CFO reviews, signs with User ID + PIN, and the filing is submitted.

EFAST2 assigns an Acknowledgment ID (AckID) and should show a filing status within about 20 minutes; the target status is "Filing Received". Nearly all 5500-SF filings are then posted on the public Form 5500 Search page.

## Validation summary

- **Math**:
  - 8c = 8a(1) + 8a(2) + 8a(3) + 8b: $172,000 + $385,000 + $42,000 + $361,500 = $960,500 ✓
  - 8h = 8d + 8e + 8f + 8g: $94,000 + $0 + $1,800 + $0 = $95,800 ✓
  - 8i = 8c − 8h: $960,500 − $95,800 = $864,700 ✓
  - 7c(b) = 7c(a) + 8i + 8j: $4,820,000 + $864,700 + $0 = $5,684,700 ✓
  - Participants: 47 active + 8 separated = 55 (BOY); 50 + 9 = 59 (EOY) ✓
  - Size test: 5c(1) = 52 < 100 ✓

- **Sanity**:
  - Participant count growth 7% (from 55 to 59) — normal hiring
  - Year-over-year asset growth 18% (from $4.82M to $5.68M) — contributions of $599,000 plus investment income of $361,500 (7.5% of beginning assets) — reasonable

- **Cross-form / cross-year**:
  - Plan number 001 (consistent with prior year) ✓
  - 7a(a) $4,820,000 = prior year's 7a(b) ✓
  - EIN 88-XXXXXXX (matches prior year) ✓

  - Plan year unchanged (calendar) ✓

## Schedule I note

Form 5500-SF takes no schedules here (Schedule SB, MB, or MEP attach only for DB plans, money purchase plans amortizing a waiver, or multiple-employer plans). Lines 7 and 8 replace the financial statements that a small plan filing Form 5500 would report on Schedule I.

If TechWidget's plan failed any 5500-SF condition (for example, it bought a limited partnership interest or held employer securities), it would file Form 5500 with Schedule I, which adds the "specific assets" lines 3a–3g and compliance questions 4a–4n, and Schedule R.
