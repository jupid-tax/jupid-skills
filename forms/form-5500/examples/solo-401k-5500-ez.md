# Example — Solo 401(k) crossing $250K threshold; first-time 5500-EZ filing

## Scenario

**Filer**: Maria Chen, sole proprietor running a consulting LLC (single-member, disregarded for federal tax)

**Plan**: "Maria Chen Consulting LLC Solo 401(k)" — established January 2022, calendar plan year

**Filing year**: Plan year 2025 (January 1, 2025 – December 31, 2025)

**Why this matters**: Maria's solo 401(k) had assets of $250,000 or less at year-end 2022, 2023, and 2024, so no 5500-EZ filing was required (2025 Form 5500-EZ instructions, Who Does Not Have To File). At year-end 2025 the plan holds more than $250,000 for the first time. The IRC §6058(a) filing requirement now applies, and she files her first Form 5500-EZ.

## Variant selection

Walked the decision tree:

1. SEP-IRA / SIMPLE IRA / governmental / church plan? No — qualified solo 401(k).
2. One-participant plan? Yes (owner only, no spouse, no employees).
3. Combined year-end assets of all her one-participant plans over $250,000? **Yes — $288,350.**
4. → File **Form 5500-EZ**.
5. Channel: fewer than 10 IRS returns in calendar 2025, so paper or EFAST2 (her choice).

This is Maria's first-ever 5500-EZ filing. Prior years (2022, 2023, 2024) were $250,000 or less and NOT required. The plan effective date is 1/1/2022 but reporting starts only with the year that the threshold is crossed.

## Filing channel

Maria's business files fewer than 10 returns with the IRS in calendar 2025, so EFAST2 is optional (2025 Form 5500-EZ instructions, Mandatory electronic filing). She has two options:

**Option A — Paper to IRS Ogden**: Print, sign, and mail (certified mail keeps proof of the mailing date) to:

```
Department of the Treasury
Internal Revenue Service
Ogden, UT 84201-0020
```

Mail by 7/31/2026 (last day of the 7th month after plan year end). The IRS sends no receipt for a paper 5500-EZ.

**Option B — Electronic via EFAST2**: Allowed for plan years beginning after 2019. Maria signs in to www.efast.dol.gov with Login.gov, registers as Filing Author and Filing Signer, and gets her User ID and 4-digit PIN on the confirmation page; there is no mailed PIN (EFAST2 Guide for Filers and Service Providers, v4.0).

**Decision**: Maria picks paper this year. If her business ever has to file 10 or more IRS returns in a calendar year, EFAST2 becomes mandatory for the 5500-EZ for the plan year beginning in that year.

## Validation summary

- **Math**:
  - 6c = 6a − 6b: $231,400 − $0 = $231,400; $288,350 − $0 = $288,350 ✓
  - Workpaper rollforward: $231,400 + $14,500 + $23,500 + $0 + $19,200 − $0 − $250 = $288,350 ✓ (matches 6a(2))
  - Contributions 7a + 7b + 7c = $38,000, within §402(g) ($23,500) and §415(c) ($70,000) for 2025
  - Line 5: 1 participant at both dates, 1 active ✓
  - Threshold: $288,350 > $250,000, no other one-participant plans to aggregate → filing required ✓

- **Sanity warnings**: None.

- **Cross-form / cross-year**:
  - This is the first filing — no prior 5500 to reconcile
  - Plan number 001 will be used in all future filings
  - Next year's 6a(1)/6c(1) must equal $288,350
