# Example — 200-employee plan; full Form 5500 + Schedule H + IQPA audit

## Scenario

**Filer**: Acme Manufacturing Inc., a mid-size US manufacturer

**Plan**: "Acme Manufacturing Inc. 401(k) Profit-Sharing Plan" — established 2005, calendar plan year

**Filing year**: Plan year 2025

**Why this matters**: Acme has 200 active employees plus former employees and beneficiaries — 205 participants with account balances on the first day of plan year 2025. The plan is **large** (100 or more), so it files the full Form 5500 with Schedule H and an IQPA report. Auditor independence is a live issue: Acme's prior auditor was not independent under DOL rules because the firm also kept the plan's books.

## Variant selection

Walked the decision tree:

1. SEP-IRA / SIMPLE IRA / governmental / church plan? No — qualified 401(k).
2. One-participant plan? No (200 active).
3. Welfare plan? No (pension plan).
4. Pension plan with < 100 participants with account balances at 1/1/2025? No (205).
5. → File full **Form 5500** + Schedule H + IQPA report + Schedule R, plus Schedule C (service providers paid $5,000 or more).

## Auditor independence — a red flag

Acme's prior IQPA firm also maintained the plan's financial records (it kept the plan's books and reconciled the trust). Under 29 CFR 2509.2022-01(b)(3), DOL will not consider an accountant independent with respect to a plan if the accountant or a member of the firm maintains financial records for the plan. DOL raised this in Acme's 2023 examination.

What 29 CFR 2509.2022-01(b) disqualifies, during the engagement period or the period covered by the financial statements:

- A direct financial interest, or a material indirect financial interest, in the plan or the plan sponsor
- A connection with the plan or sponsor as promoter, underwriter, investment advisor, voting trustee, director, officer, or employee
- Maintaining financial records for the plan

Under 29 CFR 2509.2022-01(c)(1), being separately engaged by the plan sponsor (for example, preparing the company's income tax return) or having an associated actuary serve the plan does not by itself end independence, but the (b) prohibitions still apply and multiple services can show a lack of independence.

Acme switched to **Smith & Jones LLP** starting with the plan year 2024 audit. Smith & Jones provides only audit services to the plan and does not keep its books.

## Auditor independence — what to verify

Each year, before signing the engagement, Acme's Benefits Committee confirms that Smith & Jones LLP, during the engagement and the period covered by the financial statements:

1. Has no direct financial interest, and no material indirect financial interest, in the plan or Acme Manufacturing
2. Is not connected with the plan or Acme as promoter, underwriter, investment advisor, voting trustee, director, officer, or employee
3. Does not maintain financial records for the plan
4. Is not providing other services that would be subject to its own audit procedures (29 CFR 2509.2022-01(c)(2))

The committee documents this in the audit engagement file. DOL examiners may request it.
