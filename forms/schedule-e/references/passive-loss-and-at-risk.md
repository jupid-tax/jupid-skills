# Loss Limits on Schedule E: Basis, At-Risk, Passive, Excess Business Loss

Load this file whenever any Schedule E line 21 (rental or royalty) or any K-1 line in Parts II or III is a loss. Sources: 2025 Instructions for Schedule E (Limitation on Losses, At-Risk Rules, Passive Activity Loss Rules, Line 22, Part II); 2025 Form 8582 and its instructions (Nov 26, 2025); Pub. 925 (2025); Pub. 527 (2025) ch. 3; Instructions for Form 7203 (Rev. Dec. 2022); 2025 Instructions for Form 461; IRC §465, §469.

## 1. The gate order

Apply the limits in this order and record the result of each gate in the draft (Instructions for Form 7203, Limitations on Losses; Instructions for Schedule E, Special Rules That Limit Losses):

1. **Basis** (partnership and S corporation K-1 losses only). Partnership: loss limited to adjusted basis of the partnership interest at the end of the partnership year; S corporation: limited to stock basis plus loans from you to the corporation (Form 7203). Disallowed amounts carry forward indefinitely.
2. **At-risk** (Form 6198). Applies to rental real estate, royalties, and K-1 losses when some amount is not at risk. Not at risk, in most cases: nonrecourse loans not secured by your own property (except qualified nonrecourse financing for real property), amounts protected by guarantees or stop-loss arrangements, and amounts borrowed from someone with an interest in the activity. Real property placed in service before 1987 is outside the at-risk rules. Disallowed loss is treated as a deduction of the same activity next year.
3. **Passive activity loss** (Form 8582). Passive losses offset passive income only, except the $25,000 special allowance below.
4. **Excess business loss** (Form 461). Figured after Schedule E is complete; the disallowed amount is not reflected on Schedule E but added back on Schedule 1, line 8p and carried forward as a net operating loss. 2025 threshold: $313,000 ($626,000 joint) (Instructions for Form 461, 2025, citing Rev. Proc. 2024-40).

Forms 6198, 7203, 8582 and 461 have no sibling skills in this repo. Prepare them as worksheets shown in the draft, label them "worksheet, verify on the official form", and recommend CPA review whenever a gate actually limits a loss.

## 2. What is passive

- Every **rental activity** is passive, even with material participation, unless you are a real estate professional who materially participated (Pub. 925, Rental Activities).
- A **trade or business** in which you did not materially participate is passive. Limited partners are generally not treated as materially participating (Instructions for Schedule E, Passive Activity).
- **Not passive:** rental real estate of a real estate professional with material participation; a dwelling unit used as a home (see `personal-use-and-vacation-homes.md`); a working interest in oil or gas held without limited liability; royalty income not derived in the ordinary course of a trade or business (in most cases); REMIC residual interests (Instructions for Schedule E; Pub. 925).

### When a "rental" is not a rental activity

An activity is not a rental activity if any of these applies (Pub. 925, Rental Activities, Exceptions; Instructions for Form 8582, Exceptions):

1. Average period of customer use is 7 days or less.
2. Average period of customer use is 30 days or less and significant personal services are provided with the rentals.
3. Extraordinary personal services are provided (customers' use is incidental to the services).
4. The rental is incidental to a nonrental activity (investment-holding test: gross rental income under 2% of the smaller of unadjusted basis or FMV; trade-or-business test also requires use in the business in the current year or 2 of the 5 preceding years).
5. The property is customarily available during defined business hours for nonexclusive use by various customers.
6. You provide the property for use in a nonrental activity of a partnership, S corporation, or joint venture in your capacity as an owner.

Average period of customer use = total days in all rental periods ÷ number of rentals in the year; with more than one class of property, weight each class's average by its share of gross rental income (Pub. 925; Treas. Reg. §1.469-1(e)(3)(iii) as cited in the Form 8582 instructions).

If an exception applies, the activity is not a rental activity. Then decide (Instructions for Form 8582, Reporting Income and Losses From the Activities):

- Is it a trade or business activity? If yes and you **materially participated**: nonpassive; report on the forms normally used. If yes and you did **not** materially participate: passive, reported in Form 8582 Part V ("all other passive activities"), with no $25,000 allowance.

Which schedule? That is a separate question. Significant services to the renter such as maid service move the income to Schedule C (Instructions for Schedule E, Line 3). Pub. 527 ch. 3 lists "regular cleaning, changing linen, or maid service" primarily for the tenant's convenience as substantial services, and says substantial services do not include heat and light, cleaning of public areas, trash collection. A short average stay alone does not move the activity to Schedule C. Ask the user exactly which services are provided, how often, and by whom. If the answer is mixed, present both readings and send the user to a CPA; do not pick.

### Self-rental and recharacterization

Code 7 (self-rental) on line 1b: rent from property you lease to a trade or business in which you materially participate. Net rental income is treated as nonpassive (Pub. 925, Rental of Property to a Nonpassive Activity), so it cannot absorb other passive losses. The rule as stated in Pub. 925 covers net rental income only; if the self-rental shows a loss, flag it for CPA review rather than treating it as nonpassive. Other recharacterization rules (rental of nondepreciable property where less than 30% of unadjusted basis is depreciable, rental incidental to development) are in Pub. 925; flag them for CPA review.

## 3. Material participation (Pub. 925): any one of seven tests

1. More than 500 hours in the activity.
2. Your participation was substantially all participation of all individuals.
3. More than 100 hours and not less than any other individual.
4. Significant participation activities (each over 100 hours, not material otherwise) total more than 500 hours.
5. Material participation in any 5 of the preceding 10 tax years.
6. Personal service activity with material participation in any 3 preceding tax years.
7. Regular, continuous, and substantial participation based on all facts and circumstances (not available at 100 hours or less, or if someone else was paid to manage or managed more hours).

Spouse's participation counts even if the spouse owns no interest and you file separately. Investor-type work (reviewing statements, monitoring finances in a nonmanagerial capacity) does not count. No contemporaneous log is required, but the user must be able to prove hours by a reasonable method (appointment books, calendars, narrative summaries) (Pub. 925).

## 4. Real estate professional

You are a real estate professional for 2025 only if both are true (Instructions for Schedule E; Pub. 527 ch. 3):

- More than half of your personal services in all trades or businesses were in real property trades or businesses in which you materially participated; and
- More than 750 hours of services in real property trades or businesses in which you materially participated.

Joint filers: one spouse must meet both conditions alone (spouse hours are not combined for this test, though spouse participation counts for material participation). Employee hours count only if you own more than 5% of the employer. Real property trade or business: development, redevelopment, construction, reconstruction, acquisition, conversion, rental, operation, management, leasing, or brokerage.

Each rental interest is a separate activity for material participation unless the user elects under §469(c)(7)(A) to treat all interests as one (statement attached to an original return; binding for later years as a real estate professional; late election relief in Rev. Proc. 2011-34). A real estate professional completes Schedule E line 43.

Ask for an hours log. Do not accept "I am a real estate agent" as proof of either condition.

## 5. The $25,000 special allowance (rental real estate with active participation)

**Active participation**: management decisions in a significant and bona fide sense, such as approving new tenants, deciding rental terms, approving capital or repair expenditures. You must own at least 10% (by value, including your spouse's interest) of the activity throughout the year. Limited partners generally do not actively participate (Instructions for Schedule E; Pub. 925).

**Amount and phase-out** (Pub. 925; Form 8582 Part II):

| Filing status | Maximum allowance | Reduced by | Fully phased out at MAGI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single, HOH, MFJ | $25,000 | 50% of MAGI over $100,000 | $150,000 |
| MFS, lived apart all year | $12,500 | 50% of MAGI over $50,000 | $75,000 |
| MFS, lived together at any time | $0 | Not applicable | Not applicable |

Form 8582 mechanics: line 4 = smaller of the loss on line 1d or line 3; line 5 = $150,000; line 6 = MAGI (not less than zero); line 7 = line 5 − line 6; line 8 = 50% of line 7, not more than $25,000; line 9 = smaller of line 4 or line 8. MFS filers follow the line 5 and line 8 instructions.

**MAGI for this purpose** = AGI figured without: passive activity losses; rental real estate losses allowed to real estate professionals; taxable social security and tier 1 railroad retirement benefits; deductible IRA and §501(c)(18) contributions; the deductible part of self-employment tax; the EE/I savings bond interest exclusion; the adoption-assistance exclusion; the student loan interest deduction; the FDII/GILTI deduction (Instructions for Form 8582, Line 6; Pub. 925). Ask the user for every one of these items; do not use Form 1040 AGI unadjusted.

### When Form 8582 is not needed

Skip Form 8582 and enter the line 21 loss on line 22 only if every condition holds (Instructions for Schedule E, Exception for Certain Rental Real Estate Activities):

1. Rental real estate activities are your only passive activities.
2. No prior-year unallowed losses from any passive activity.
3. If there is an overall net loss: active participation in all rental real estate activities; if MFS, lived apart all year; overall net loss $25,000 or less ($12,500 MFS); no current or prior-year unallowed passive credits; MAGI $100,000 or less ($50,000 MFS); no rental real estate interest held as a limited partner or as a beneficiary of an estate or trust.

### Allocating an allowed amount among properties

With more than one loss property, Form 8582 Part VI spreads line 9 by each activity's share of the total losses: ratio = activity loss ÷ total losses; allowed share = ratio × line 9. The unallowed remainder goes to Part VII, then Parts VIII/IX produce the allowed loss per activity that goes on Schedule E line 22 (Instructions for Form 8582, Parts VI–IX). Round to whole dollars and force the column total to equal line 9.

### Carryforward and release

Unallowed passive losses carry forward on Form 8582. They are released in full in the year you dispose of your entire interest in the activity in a fully taxable transaction to an unrelated person (Pub. 925). Ask for last year's Form 8582 Parts VII–IX (or the preparer's carryforward schedule) before every Schedule E with a passive loss.

## 6. Questions to ask before computing any loss

1. "What is your filing status? If married filing separately, did you live apart from your spouse for all of 2025?"
2. "List each item from this set that is on your return: taxable social security, IRA deduction, student loan interest, half of self-employment tax, savings bond interest exclusion, adoption assistance exclusion." (MAGI)
3. "For each rental: what share do you own, and who approves tenants, rent levels, and repairs?" (active participation, 10%)
4. "How many hours did you work in real estate businesses in 2025, and how many hours in all jobs and businesses combined?" (real estate professional)
5. "For short-term rentals: how many separate stays, total nights booked, and what services do guests receive during a stay?" (average customer use; services)
6. "Do you have suspended passive losses or credits from prior years? Please share last year's Form 8582."
7. "Is any of the financing nonrecourse, seller-provided, or from someone with an interest in the property?" (at-risk)
8. "For each K-1: your basis worksheet or Form 7203, and whether you work in that business and how many hours." (basis; material participation)
