# Schedule E (Form 1040) Line-by-Line Reference

Every line of Schedule E, built from the text of the **2025 Schedule E (Form 1040)** (Created 5/6/25) and the **2025 Instructions for Schedule E** (dated Nov 12, 2025). This is the revision filed in 2026. Before using this map for any later year, download the new revision from https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-schedule-e-form-1040 and re-check every line number.

Conventions used here:

- "Col A/B/C" = the three property columns in Part I. More than three properties: attach more Schedules E, but answer lines A and B and fill lines 23a–26 on only one of them, with combined totals (Instructions, Part I).
- Losses are entered in parentheses (General Instructions).
- Report every line in the draft, including zeros, so the reviewer can see a line was considered.

### Expenses (per property column)

General rule: ordinary and necessary expenses; never the value of your own labor; never capital improvements (Instructions, Lines 5 Through 21). Renting only part of a home: deduct only the part of each expense that applies to the rented part (same). Personal-use days: allocate expenses first (see `personal-use-and-vacation-homes.md`).

## Where totals go

- Line 26 → Schedule 1, line 5, when only Part I applies; otherwise line 41 → Schedule 1, line 5 (form text). Schedule 1 line 5 label verified on the 2025 Schedule 1.
- Losses limited by the excess business loss rule are not reduced on Schedule E; Form 461 adds the excess back on Schedule 1, line 8p (Instructions, Reminders).
