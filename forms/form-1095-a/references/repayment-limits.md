# Excess APTC Repayment Limits (Form 8962 Instructions, Table 5)

When advance Premium Tax Credit (APTC) exceeded the actual PTC the user qualified for, the excess is generally repaid via Form 8962 Line 29 → Schedule 2 Line 1a. For tax years before 2026, Table 5 of the Form 8962 instructions caps the repayment for filers below 400% FPL (IRC §36B(f)(2)(B)). For tax years beginning after December 31, 2025, there is no cap at any income (P.L. 119-21 §71305; IRS FS-2025-10, Q31).

The cap depends on:
1. Filing status (single vs. all others)
2. Household income as percentage of FPL (Form 8962 Line 5)

---

## 2025 Repayment Limits (use for tax year 2025 filing)

| Line 5 (% of FPL) | Single | All Other Filing Statuses |
|-------------------|--------|---------------------------|
| Less than 200% | $375 | $750 |
| At least 200% but less than 300% | $975 | $1,950 |
| At least 300% but less than 400% | $1,625 | $3,250 |
| 400% or more | NO CAP — leave Line 28 blank, full repayment | NO CAP — leave Line 28 blank, full repayment |

(Figures from Table 5 of the 2025 Form 8962 instructions, Line 28.)

## Year-by-year reference

The dollar caps adjusted annually for inflation through 2025 (each year's Form 8962 instructions, Table 5):

| Tax Year | Single < 200% | Single 200-300% | Single 300-400% | Other < 200% | Other 200-300% | Other 300-400% |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|-----------------|--------------|----------------|----------------|
| 2023 | $350 | $900 | $1,500 | $700 | $1,800 | $3,000 |
| 2024 | $375 | $950 | $1,575 | $750 | $1,900 | $3,150 |
| 2025 | $375 | $975 | $1,625 | $750 | $1,950 | $3,250 |
| 2026 | No limit | No limit | No limit | No limit | No limit | No limit |

## How to apply

1. From Form 8962 Line 27 (Excess APTC) and Line 5 (Income % FPL):
2. Determine filing status: Single = single; all others (MFJ, MFS, HoH, QSS) = "any other filing status"
3. Look up the cap in the appropriate row (tax year 2025 or earlier only)
4. Form 8962 Line 28 = the cap (blank if Line 5 is 400 or more, and for tax years after 2025)
5. Form 8962 Line 29 = lesser of Line 27 or Line 28 (Line 27 if Line 28 is blank) → flows to Schedule 2 Line 1a

---

## ARPA repayment relief (one-time, tax year 2020 only)

The American Rescue Plan Act waived all excess APTC repayment for tax year 2020 only, in response to COVID-19 income volatility. This was a one-time provision and does NOT apply to 2021 or later years.

---

## Edge cases

### Case 1 — Income reported under 100% FPL
If actual household income came in below 100% FPL but APTC was paid because the Marketplace estimated household income of at least 100% FPL, the user is still treated as an applicable taxpayer and can take the PTC (2025 Form 8962 instructions, Line 5). For 2025 the "alien lawfully present" rule (IRC §36B(c)(1)(B)) also applies; P.L. 119-21 §71302 repealed it for tax years after 2025. Any 2025 repayment is limited per the Table 5 row "less than 200".

### Case 2 — Income reported over 400% FPL
Under ARPA/IRA (2021–2025), no cap applies but PTC is still available (applicable figure 0.0850). For 2026 the enhanced credit expired: no PTC above 400% FPL, so the entire APTC received is repaid, with no limitation.

### Case 3 — Married filing separately
MFS uses the "any other filing status" column, and Table 5 applies to each spouse separately based on the household income on each return (2025 Form 8962 instructions, Line 28). An MFS filer who does not meet the domestic abuse / spousal abandonment exception is not an applicable taxpayer but still repays allocated APTC subject to the line 28 limit.
