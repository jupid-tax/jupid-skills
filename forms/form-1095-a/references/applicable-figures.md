# Applicable Figures (Form 8962 Line 7)

The Applicable Figure is the percentage of household income the user is expected to contribute toward Marketplace premiums. PTC fills the gap between this contribution and the SLCSP benchmark.

Two regimes:
1. **Statutory table, indexed yearly (2014–2020, and again from 2026)**: applicable percentages from about 2% (below 133% FPL) to about 10% (300–400% FPL); above 400% FPL → no PTC
2. **ARPA/IRA-extended (2021–2025)**: lower applicable figures across the board; above 400% FPL → still eligible if benchmark plan exceeds 8.5% of income

**The enhanced figures expired after 2025** (IRC §36B(b)(3)(A)(iii) and (c)(1)(E) apply only to tax years beginning before January 1, 2026). No extension had been enacted as of 2026-10-06. Verify the Form 8962 instructions and Pub 974 for the year being filed.

---

## ARPA/IRA-extended Applicable Figures (2021–2025)

Source for 2025: Rev. Proc. 2024-35; 2025 Form 8962 instructions, Table 2.

| Line 5 (% of FPL) | Applicable Figure |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| 150% or less | 0.0000 (0%) |
| 200% | 0.0200 (2.0%) |
| 250% | 0.0400 (4.0%) |
| 300% | 0.0600 (6.0%) |
| 400% | 0.0850 (8.5%) |
| Over 400% (enter 401) | 0.0850 (capped at 8.5%) |

Linear between those points; Table 2 lists the value for every whole percent. Example: at 175% FPL, applicable figure = (175 − 150) / (200 − 150) × (0.02 − 0.00) = 0.0100 (1.0%), matching Table 2.

## 2026 Applicable Percentages (enhancements expired)

Source: Rev. Proc. 2025-25, §3.01. The 2026 Form 8962 instructions (with the 2026 Table 2) had not been released on 2026-10-06; use their Table 2 once published.

| Household income (% of FPL) | Initial percentage | Final percentage |
|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Less than 133% | 2.10% | 2.10% |
| At least 133% but less than 150% | 3.14% | 4.19% |
| At least 150% but less than 200% | 4.19% | 6.60% |
| At least 200% but less than 250% | 6.60% | 8.44% |
| At least 250% but less than 300% | 8.44% | 9.96% |
| At least 300% but not more than 400% | 9.96% | 9.96% |
| Over 400% | NO PTC ELIGIBILITY | |

The most consequential difference: for 2026, anyone over 400% FPL gets **zero** PTC, often called the "subsidy cliff," and repays the entire APTC with no limitation (P.L. 119-21 §71305). ARPA/IRA had removed this cliff for 2021–2025.

---

## How to look up

1. Determine Line 5 (Income % of FPL).
2. Find the row that matches or interpolate between rows.
3. For the 2025 (ARPA/IRA-extended) table, anything 150% and below → 0.0000 (no contribution required).
4. Enter the figure on Form 8962 Line 7.
