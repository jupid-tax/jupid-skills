# Filing Form 1120-S

How to take a validated Form 1120-S draft from SKILL.md to the IRS. Produce and validate the draft first, then pick a channel with the decision tree, then follow that channel's steps. The agent never submits, signs, or pays without the user's explicit consent at that moment.

Sources: 2025 Instructions for Form 1120-S (Electronic Filing; When To File; Where To File; Who Must Sign; Tax Payments; Assembling the Return); Instructions for Form 7004 (Rev. December 2025); Treas. Reg. §301.6037-2; IRS pages "Form 1120/1120-F/1120-H/1120-L/1120-PC/1120-REIT/1120-RIC/1120-S e-file" and "Approved IRS e-file for business providers". Re-check addresses and dates each year.

---

## Key dates

| Event | Date for calendar 2025 returns | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Return and K-1s due | March 16, 2026 (March 15 was a Sunday) | 15th day of 3rd month after year end; next business day rule (Instr., When To File) |
| Extended due date with Form 7004 | September 15, 2026 | 6-month automatic extension (Instructions for Form 7004) |
| Tax on line 23c | Due by the original due date; Form 7004 does not extend payment | Instr., Tax Payments; Form 7004 instructions |
| 2026 tax-year return | March 15, 2027 (a Monday) | Same 15th-day-of-3rd-month rule; confirm on the 2026 instructions |

A corporation that dissolved files by the 15th day of the 3rd month after the date it dissolved. A terminated election (other than by a §336(e) election) files the S short-year return by the due date, including extensions, of the C short-year return (Instr., When To File).

## Channel decision tree

```
Does a CPA or enrolled agent prepare the return?
  YES → Section 1: handoff package. The preparer e-files under their own EFIN.

Is the corporation required to e-file? (required to file 10 or more returns of any type during the
  calendar year ending with or within its tax year: income tax, employment tax (each Form 941 counts),
  excise, and information returns such as each Form W-2 and 1099; Reg. §301.6037-2(a)(1), (d)(5))
  YES → Section 2: e-file through approved software (or a preparer). Paper only with an IRS waiver
        or the religious exemption.
  NO  → Choose:
        e-file through approved software (Section 2), or
        paper (Section 3).

Is there no IRS free option? Correct: "Corporations must use approved software and/or a tax professional
who is an authorized e-file provider" (IRS, Form 1120/1120-S e-file page). IRS Free File Fillable Forms
covers individual returns only.
```

Count the returns for the e-file test with the user. For a calendar-2025 Form 1120-S the count is the returns the corporation was required to file during calendar 2025 (the calendar year ending with its 2025 tax year), including the 2024 Form 1120-S filed in 2025. Ask: "During 2025, how many returns was the corporation required to file, counting last year's Form 1120-S, each Form 941, Form 940, each Form W-2 and each Form 1099?" The regulation's example: one Form 1120-S, two Forms W-2, two Forms 1099-DIV, one Form 940 and four Forms 941 filed during 2023 total 10, so the 2023 Form 1120-S must be e-filed (Reg. §301.6037-2(e)). Schedules attached to the Form 1120-S do not count.

---

## Section 1 — Handoff package for a preparer

Give the preparer:

1. The full draft (every line, including zeros) and the validation summary with open items
2. Trial balance, general ledger detail for line 20 and Schedule L, Form 4562 asset list
3. Forms W-2/W-3 and the four Forms 941; Forms 1099 issued
4. Shareholder register with dates of every change; item G computation
5. Prior-year Form 1120-S, prior Schedule M-2 balances, Form 2553 acceptance letter
6. Estimated tax and Form 7004 payment confirmations
7. Open boundary flags (built-in gains, passive income, M-3, K-2/K-3, allocation elections)

The preparer signs the "Paid Preparer Use Only" section. If the officer wants the IRS to be able to discuss the return with that preparer, check "Yes" in the signature area (authorization ends no later than the due date, excluding extensions, of the 2026 return; Instr., Paid Preparer Authorization).

---

## Section 2 — E-file through approved software

1. The user picks software listed on the IRS page "Approved IRS e-file for business providers" (1120x Modernized e-File providers): https://www.irs.gov/e-file-providers/approved-irs-e-file-for-business-providers. The IRS does not endorse any listed company.
2. The agent may enter values from the draft into the software only with the user's permission and while the user controls the session. Enter by line number; after entry, compare every software-computed line (1c, 3, 6, 21, 22, 23c, 24z, 26/27, Schedule K line 18, M-1 line 8, M-2 line 8, K-1 totals) with the draft. If they disagree, stop: one of the two is wrong.
3. EIN must be issued; "Applied for" is not accepted on an e-filed return (Instr., Item D).
4. Signature: the authorized officer signs electronically through the software's process (for preparer-transmitted returns the officer authorizes with Form 8879-S, IRS e-file Signature Authorization for Form 1120-S, kept by the preparer). The agent never enters a PIN on the officer's behalf.
5. Balance due on line 26: pay by electronic funds transfer (see Payments below), or authorize an electronic funds withdrawal in the software when offered.
6. Submit only after the user says, at that moment, "Submit the return." Save the submission ID and the acknowledgment (accepted or rejected with a reject code). On rejection, read the code, fix the specific field, re-validate, and ask again before resubmitting.

## Section 3 — Paper filing

### Assemble

Attach all schedules and forms after page 5 of Form 1120-S in this order (Instr., Assembling the Return): Form 8825; Form 1125-A; Schedule D (Form 1120-S); Form 8949; Form 8996; Form 4797; Schedule N (Form 1120); Form 8941; Form 3800; Form 8997; Form 8283; Form 6252; Schedule A (Form 8936); Schedules K-1; Form 8938; additional schedules in alphabetical order (including Schedules K-2 and K-3); additional forms in numerical order; supporting statements last, in the order of the forms they support, each with the corporation's name and EIN. Forms not named in the list (for example Form 1125-E, Form 4562, Schedule B-1) follow the last two rules: schedules in alphabetical order, forms in numerical order. Form 7203 is the shareholder's form and is never attached to Form 1120-S. Do not write "See attached" in any entry space.

### Sign

An officer signs and dates page 1: president, vice president, treasurer, assistant treasurer, chief accounting officer, or another officer authorized to sign (Instr., Who Must Sign). A receiver, trustee or assignee signs instead when one is filing for the corporation.

### Proof of timely filing

Use USPS certified mail or an IRS-designated private delivery service (IRS.gov/PDS) to rely on the timely-mailing-as-timely-filing rule. Private delivery services cannot deliver to P.O. boxes; use the PDS street addresses at IRS.gov/PDSStreetAddresses (Instr., Private Delivery Services).

## Payments

Any tax on line 23c (and estimated tax for entity-level taxes) is paid by electronic funds transfer: EFTPS (www.EFTPS.gov, 800-555-4477) or the corporation's IRS business tax account (IRS.gov/BusinessAccount). EFTPS same-day cutoff is 3:00 p.m. Eastern for payments of $1 million or less; larger payments must be submitted by 8:00 p.m. Eastern the day before. Same-day wire through the Federal Tax Collection Service is the fallback (Instr., Electronic Deposit Requirement). If the corporation cannot pay in full, an online installment agreement is available when the amount owed is $25,000 or less and payable within 24 months (Instr., line 26; IRS.gov/OPA).

---

## Extension

File Form 7004 by the original due date, form code 25 for Form 1120-S; it gives 6 months. The IRS no longer sends approval notices; it writes only if the request is disallowed (Instructions for Form 7004). Pay any line 23c tax with the extension. See [`../form-7004/SKILL.md`](../form-7004/SKILL.md).

---

## After filing

- Furnish each K-1 (and K-3 when required, or the domestic-filing-exception notice) to shareholders on or before the day the return is required to be filed, with the Shareholder's Instructions for Schedule K-1 or item-specific instructions.
- Keep records supporting income and deductions at least 3 years from the date each shareholder's return is due or filed, whichever is later; keep asset basis records as long as needed (Instr., Recordkeeping).
- Penalty notices: do not attach a reasonable-cause explanation to the return; respond to the notice (Instr., Interest and Penalties, Caution).
- To correct the return later: file an amended Form 1120-S with box H(4) checked, a statement of each changed line with reasons, and amended K-1s marked "Amended K-1" (Instr., Amended Return).

---

## Consent and security rules for the agent

1. Never submit, sign, or pay without the user's explicit instruction at that moment. Prior approval of the draft is not approval to file.
2. Never store or log SSNs, EINs of individual shareholders, bank routing and account numbers, PINs, or passwords. Mask them in shared drafts (show the last four digits).
3. Never enter a signature PIN or complete identity verification for the officer.
4. If any software-computed amount disagrees with the validated draft, stop and report the line.
5. Do not file a return for a year before the S election's effective date, or a duplicate of a return a preparer already filed.
6. If an IRS screen, captcha, or multi-factor prompt appears, hand control to the user.
