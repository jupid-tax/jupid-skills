# Example: Foreign Owner Without SSN or ITIN, Single-Member LLC for Form 5472 (Telephone)

A nonresident who lives in Portugal forms a Wyoming single-member LLC. The LLC needs an EIN because a U.S. disregarded entity wholly owned by a foreign person must file Form 5472 (with a pro forma Form 1120) under its own name and EIN. The owner has no SSN and no ITIN, so the online application is closed to him.

## The applicant

- **Owner:** Tomás M. Alvarenga, citizen and resident of Portugal, lives in Lisbon. No SSN, no ITIN, never applied for either
- **Entity:** Alvarenga Labs LLC, organized in Wyoming, effective Thursday, August 27, 2026
- **Business:** subscription project-management software sold online to customers in several countries. No U.S. office, warehouse, or employees; Tomás runs everything from Lisbon
- **Classification plan:** default (disregarded); no Form 8832 planned. The agent asked; Tomás confirmed with his adviser
- **Why an EIN:** Form 5472 and pro forma Form 1120 for the LLC, and a U.S. business bank account
- **Application:** telephone, Tuesday, September 8, 2026, 3:00 p.m. Lisbon time (10:00 a.m. Eastern)

## Decisions

1. **Responsible party and line 7b:** Tomás owns and controls the LLC. He has no SSN or ITIN and is ineligible to obtain an SSN, so line 7b reads "Foreign" (Instructions, Lines 7a–7b TIP: enter "foreign" or N/A). He does not need an ITIN to get the EIN. Whether he needs an ITIN for his own U.S. return is a separate question (see Next steps).
2. **Line 9a:** U.S. disregarded entity wholly owned by a foreign person, EIN requested to file Form 5472 → **Other**, "Foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity-Form 5472" (Instructions, Disregarded entities).
3. **Line 10:** **Other**, "Foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity filing Form 5472" (Instructions, Line 10, Other).
4. **Line 6:** the instructions ask for the entity's primary physical location. The business is physically run from Lisbon, so the draft says "Lisbon, Portugal". The instructions do not address a location outside the U.S. for this line; the agent flags the entry for the owner's adviser.
5. **Line 11:** for foreign applicants this is the date the business began or was acquired in the United States; the U.S. entity came into existence on 08/27/2026.
6. **Channel:** online is unavailable (no U.S. principal place of business; no SSN or ITIN). As an international applicant he may call **267-941-1099** (6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Eastern, Monday to Friday), fax **304-707-9471** from Portugal, or mail to the **EIN International Operation**. He picks the phone because the EIN is assigned during the call.

## Why each non-obvious choice

**Why "Foreign" on line 7b instead of leaving it blank or applying for an ITIN first?** The instructions require an entry on 7b and allow "foreign" or "N/A" when the responsible party doesn't have and is ineligible to obtain an SSN or ITIN. An ITIN serves an individual's own federal tax purposes and is requested on Form W-7 under that form's reasons and exceptions; the EIN application itself does not require one.

**Why not the online application with a friend's SSN?** Line 7b must belong to the person on line 7a, and that person must be the one who controls the entity. The online tool requires the responsible party's SSN or ITIN and a U.S. principal place of business; Tomás meets neither condition.

**Why Other on line 10 instead of Started new business?** The instructions give a specific line 10 text for a foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity required to file Form 5472. Using it matches line 9a and tells the IRS why the entity exists in its records.

**What if he had chosen fax?** From Portugal he would fax to 304-707-9471 (855-215-1627 is for faxing from inside the U.S.) and include a return fax number; the EIN generally comes back within 4 business days. Sent on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, that is Monday, September 14, 2026.

**What if he had chosen mail?** Internal Revenue Service, Attn: EIN International Operation, Cincinnati, OH 45999, about 4 weeks, so around October 6, 2026 for a September 8 mailing, plus international postal time.

**Date checks (Python):** August 27, 2026 is a Thursday; September 8, 2026 a Tuesday; 24 hours later is September 9, 2026; 4 business days after September 8 is September 14, 2026; 4 weeks after is October 6, 2026.
