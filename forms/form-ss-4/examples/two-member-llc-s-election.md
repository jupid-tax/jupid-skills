# Example: Two-Member LLC That Will Elect S Corporation Status (Online, Third Party Designee)

Two partners form a Texas LLC for an HVAC business. With their CPA they decided before applying that the LLC will elect S corporation status from its first day. They will put themselves and one technician on payroll. The CPA applies online as their third party designee.

## The applicants

- **Entity:** Pecan Ridge Air LLC, organized with the Texas Secretary of State, effective Thursday, October 1, 2026
- **Members:** Marisol R. Vega (51%, managing member) and Dale T. Kincaid (49%), both U.S. citizens with SSNs, both live in Austin
- **Business:** residential HVAC installation, repair, and maintenance; shop at 2208 Barton Hills Dr, Ste 140, Austin, TX 78704 (Travis County)
- **Classification plan:** elect S corporation status on Form 2553, effective October 1, 2026 (decided with their CPA; the skill did not make this choice)
- **Employees in the next 12 months:** Marisol and Dale as officer-employees, plus one technician
- **Expected wages, next 12 months:** Marisol $48,000; Dale $48,000; technician $41,600; first payroll Friday, November 13, 2026
- **Designee:** Hannah Lu, CPA, Lu & Ortega CPAs, 600 Congress Ave, Ste 1400, Austin, TX 78701, phone (512) 555-0147, fax (512) 555-0148
- **Application date:** Wednesday, October 7, 2026

## Decisions

1. **Responsible party:** two people could qualify. The responsible parties page says to list the person you want the IRS to recognize; the members chose Marisol.
2. **Line 9a:** a domestic LLC that will file Form 2553 checks **Corporation** and writes the return it will file, **1120-S** (Instructions, Lines 8a–8c). It is a two-member LLC, so the "Single-member" write-in does not apply. Line 9a is not the election; Form 2553 is.
3. **Line 9b:** line 9a is Corporation, so line 9b is completed with the state of organization, **Texas**. The instructions have no line 9b text and the LLC was organized, not incorporated; flagged for the CPA.
4. **Line 10:** **Started new business**, "HVAC installation and repair contractor".
5. **Lines 13 to 15:** Other employees = 3. Expected wages $137,600 exceed $5,000, so employment tax liability will not be $1,000 or less; line 14 stays unchecked and the LLC files Form 941 quarterly (Instructions, Line 14). Line 15 = 11/13/2026.
6. **Line 16:** Construction. The instructions include special trade contractors such as HVAC in Construction.
7. **Designee and channel:** the members sign the SS-4 with the designee block completed; the designee's address and phone differ from the LLC's, so online is allowed. Domestic entity, U.S. principal place of business, responsible party SSN → **online**. The EIN is released to Hannah online; the EIN notice is mailed to the LLC (Instructions, Third-party designee).
8. **Form 2553 timing (handed off):** the SS-4 instructions say Form 2553 is due no later than the 15th day of the 3rd month of the tax year it takes effect. Under the Form 2553 instructions' counting rule, a tax year beginning October 1, 2026 gives a 2-month period ending November 30, 2026, plus 15 days: **December 15, 2026**. The agent asks the members to confirm October 1 is the earliest date the LLC had members, acquired assets, or began business, and hands the exact computation to `../../form-2553/SKILL.md`.

## Why each non-obvious choice

**Why Corporation on 9a before the election is made?** The instructions tell an LLC that will file Form 2553 to check Corporation and write 1120-S. The same instructions warn that until Form 2553 is received and approved, the entity is considered a Form 1120 filer, which is why the Form 2553 deadline goes into the hand-off.

**Why not Partnership, the default for two members?** Line 9a describes the classification the entity will use. The members already decided on the S election. If they had not decided, the agent would stop: a Partnership EIN application followed by an S election later is a different path and a decision for their CPA.

**Why line 13 counts the owners?** Line 13 asks for the highest number of employees expected in the next 12 months. The members told the agent they will both be on the corporation's payroll as officers, so they count alongside the technician. How much salary they take is a question for their CPA, not for this form.

**Why line 14 is not checked?** The instructions' rule of thumb ties a $1,000 employment tax liability to about $5,000 of wages. $48,000 + $48,000 + $41,600 = $137,600, far above it.

**Why online with a designee is allowed?** The online tool accepts an authorized representative, and a third party designee needs signed authorization (Get an EIN page). The instructions require mail or fax only when the designee's address or phone matches the taxpayer's; here they differ.

**Date checks (Python):** October 1, 2026 is a Thursday; October 7, 2026 a Wednesday; November 13, 2026 a Friday. Form 2553 rule: 2-month period from October 1 ends November 30, 2026; plus 15 days is December 15, 2026 (a Tuesday). October 7 plus 14 days is October 21, 2026. Expected wages sum to $137,600.
