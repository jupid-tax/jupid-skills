# Output Formats

Use concise, evidence-forward formats. Do not bury citations at the end.

## Standard answer

```markdown
## Short Answer
[Direct answer, or "Not determined from the located official sources."]

## Authorities
| Point | Source | Pinpoint | What it supports |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Issue] | [Official URL] | [Section/page/line] | [Narrow support] |

## Analysis
- [Claim.] Source: [Official URL], [pinpoint].
- [Claim.] Source: [Official URL], [pinpoint].

## Not Determined / Open Facts
- [Missing fact, missing source, or unresolved issue.]

## Practical Next Step
[One conservative next action.]
```

## Evidence table

Use this when the user asks for audit support or workpaper-style output.

```markdown
| Claim | Evidence level | Source | Pinpoint | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Claim] | Official authority | [URL] | [Pinpoint] | Supported |
| [Claim] | None | N/A | N/A | Not determined |
```

## Refusal / narrowing

```markdown
## Short Answer
Not determined from the located official sources.

## What the source supports
- [Narrow supported point.] Source: [Official URL], [pinpoint].

## What is not supported
- [Requested broader conclusion.]

## Next step
[Specific source to locate or fact to confirm.]
```

## Research handoff

Use this when the agent is preparing work for a CPA, EA, attorney, or reviewer.

```markdown
## Research Question
[Narrow question.]

## Located Authorities
| Authority | Pinpoint | Relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Supported Conclusions
- [Conclusion.] Source: [URL], [pinpoint].

## Unsupported / Reviewer Questions
- [Issue requiring professional judgment.]

## Files / URLs Reviewed
- [URL or file path]
```
