# Example: Insufficient Source Refusal

User:

```text
Can I deduct 100% of my home internet because I work from home?
```

Answer:

```markdown
## Short Answer
Not determined from the facts provided. I cannot support a 100% deduction from
the official sources without facts showing the internet expense was entirely
business-use.

## What the source supports
- Business expenses must be ordinary and necessary to be deductible. Source:
  IRC Section 162(a), https://uscode.house.gov/, Section 162(a).
- Personal, living, or family expenses are generally not deductible unless a
  specific Code provision allows them. Source: IRC Section 262(a),
  https://uscode.house.gov/, Section 262(a).

## What is not supported
- The provided facts do not support that the entire internet bill was a
  business expense rather than a mixed personal/business expense.

## Next step
Determine the business-use percentage and keep support for how it was computed.
```
