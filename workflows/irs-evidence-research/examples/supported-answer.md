# Example: Supported Answer

User:

```text
What official source says a sole proprietor reports business income on Schedule C?
```

Answer:

```markdown
## Short Answer
A sole proprietor reports profit or loss from a business on Schedule C, which is
filed with Form 1040.

## Authorities
| Point | Source | Pinpoint | What it supports |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Schedule C is for profit or loss from business as a sole proprietor | IRS Schedule C instructions | Instructions for Schedule C, "Use Schedule C (Form 1040) to report income or loss from a business you operated or a profession you practiced as a sole proprietor" | Schedule C is the reporting form for sole proprietor business income/loss |

## Analysis
- The controlling form instruction identifies Schedule C as the place to report
  income or loss from a business operated as a sole proprietor. Source:
  https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-schedule-c-form-1040, "About Schedule C
  (Form 1040)".

## Not Determined / Open Facts
- This does not determine whether a particular user's activity is a trade or
  business, whether a different entity return is required, or whether a
  specific expense is deductible.

## Practical Next Step
Confirm the taxpayer's entity type and activity facts before using a
form-specific Schedule C skill.
```
